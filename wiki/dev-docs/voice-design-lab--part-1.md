# Voice Design Lab

*Part 1 of 4 — see also: [Part 2](voice-design-lab--part-2.md), [Part 3](voice-design-lab--part-3.md), [Part 4](voice-design-lab--part-4.md)*

The Voice Design Lab lets you create custom voices for text-to-speech in two ways: by describing a voice in natural language (Design a Voice) or by cloning from an audio sample (Clone from Audio). Both flows produce a production-ready voice clone that can be used across AI Assistants, Call Control, and the TTS API.

## Overview

The [Voice Design](https://portal.telnyx.com/#/app/ai/voice-design-lab) lab provides two ways to create custom voices for text-to-speech — no recording studio, training datasets, or waiting required:

- **Design a Voice** — describe the voice you want in plain text (age, tone, accent, energy) and the AI generates it from scratch.
- **Clone from Audio** — upload or record an audio sample and the AI captures that voice identity.

Both flows produce a production-ready voice clone with a unique voice ID that can be used in AI Assistants, Call Control, and the TTS API.
