# Telnyx Text-to-Speech

*Part 4 of 4 — see also: [Part 1](telnyx-text-to-speech--part-1.md), [Part 2](telnyx-text-to-speech--part-2.md), [Part 3](telnyx-text-to-speech--part-3.md)*

Telnyx Text-to-Speech (TTS) provides streaming speech synthesis over both REST and WebSocket transports, with multiple voice providers (Telnyx-branded models and xAI Grok), OpenAI SDK compatibility, and provider-specific tuning for speed, language, emotion, and voice cloning.

## Pricing

Pricing for WebSocket TTS varies by engine and model. Contact [sales](https://telnyx.com/contact-us) or check the [pricing page](https://telnyx.com/pricing/text-to-speech) for current rates.
