# LiveKit on Telnyx pricing

LiveKit on Telnyx consolidates compute, models, and telephony billing onto a single Telnyx invoice, eliminating third-party SIP fees charged by other platforms. Pricing for individual components such as STT, TTS, and LLM is published on the main Telnyx pricing pages, while the platform-exclusive Telnyx Ultra voice is available only to agents deployed on LiveKit on Telnyx.

## Overview

All services — compute, models, and telephony — are billed on a single Telnyx invoice. On other platforms, third-party SIP fees are charged on top of usage; on Telnyx, those fees are eliminated entirely.

## Telnyx Ultra — Platform Exclusive

Telnyx Ultra is the highest-fidelity voice offered by Telnyx. It is available exclusively for agents deployed on LiveKit on Telnyx and is not accessible through the plugin alone. See the [TTS Available Voices](tts-available-voices.md) page for the full list of supported voices, languages, and accents.
