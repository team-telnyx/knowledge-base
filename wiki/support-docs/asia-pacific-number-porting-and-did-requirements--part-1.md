Consolidated reference for Telnyx number porting and DID acquisition across APAC and Finland, covering portable number types, expected timeframes, port categories, document requirements, LOA downloads, and country-specific rules for Australia, New Zealand, Singapore, China, Hong Kong, Vietnam, Malaysia, Thailand, Indonesia, the Philippines, and Finland.

## Asia-Pacific Number Porting and DID Requirements

This page consolidates Telnyx's Asia-Pacific (APAC) number porting and DID acquisition requirements, covering Australia, New Zealand, Singapore, China, Hong Kong, Vietnam, Malaysia, Thailand, Indonesia, the Philippines, and Finland. Use it to identify portable number types, expected timeframes, document requirements, and country-specific rules before submitting a port or DID order.

## Australia Number Porting

Port Australian phone numbers to Telnyx across local, national, toll-free, and mobile categories.

### Portable Number Types

| Number Type | Portable | Port Category |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Local Numbers | Yes | CAT A or CAT C |
| National Numbers | Yes | CAT A or CAT C |
| Toll-Free (1800) | Yes | CAT Special |
| Mobile (+614) | Yes | Mobile |

### Expected Timeframes

| Port Type | Expected Timeframe |
| --- | --- |
| CAT A (Simple) | 3–6 business days |
| CAT C (Complex) | 5+ weeks |
| CAT Special (Toll-Free) | 3–6 business days |
| Mobile | 1–5 business days |

These are estimates and may be extended depending on order complexity, the losing carrier, or rejections.

### Port Categories

- **CAT A (Simple Port):** Single telephone numbers, "home phone" style numbers, no complex services attached.
- **CAT C (Complex Port):** Required for ISDN lines, hunt groups, PBX services, bundled service packages, or multiple numbers on a single service. Complex services associated with the number(s) will be canceled during the porting process.
- **CAT Special:** Required for toll-free (1800) and shared cost numbers.

### Document Requirements

**Local / National / Toll-Free Numbers**

- LOA (Letter of Authorization) with a local Australian address
- ABN (Australian Business Number) for business accounts
- Latest invoice from current carrier, dated within the last 30 days

**Mobile Numbers**

- LOA with a local Australian address
- Latest invoice from current service provider
- Service type (Prepaid or Postpaid)
- Two forms of Australian Government-issued ID (see list below)
- For prepaid services: end-user's full name, date of birth, and account number

Accepted identity documents (any two):

- State or Territory Driver's License
- Australian Passport
- Australian Citizenship Certificate
- Proof-of-Age card
- Australian Birth Certificate
- Foreign military ID card
- Current foreign passport with valid entry stamp or visa
- Australian Government-issued Photo ID card
- Trade/work/business license

### Mobile Verification

Before submitting an Australian mobile port, complete a verification step:

1. During the port request, reach the "Mobile Verification" step.
2. A unique code is sent to each phone number (via SMS or call).
3. Enter each code to verify ownership.
4. All numbers must be verified before the port can proceed.

If you cannot verify all numbers, the port will remain in draft status.

### LOA and Common Rejections

📄 [Download the Australia Letter of Authorization](https://assets.ctfassets.net/taysl255dolk/72Cl4rcgvAl9N3Lt4iUalk/34cad211371dd434f52088f573a5f6b4/LoA_-_TELNYX_-_australia.pdf)

Before completing the LOA:

- Save in PDF format (non-editable)
- Fill in all required fields completely
- Ensure information matches your current carrier's records exactly
- Address must be a local Australian address (for emergency services)

Common rejection reasons:

- **Company information mismatch:** Verify exact company name and details with your current carrier before submitting.
- **Complex services not declared:** Check for hunt groups, ISDN, or bundled services — select CAT C if any exist.
- **Associated numbers:** Ask current carrier to disassociate linked numbers, or port all associated numbers together.
- **Disconnected number:** Ensure all numbers are still in-service — do not cancel with current carrier before port completes.

### Changing Your FOC Date

Once your port request reaches FOC confirmed status, date changes require at least 48 business hours notice before the scheduled FOC. Contact Telnyx porting support to request a change.

### Porting Hours

Standard time 8 AM to 12 PM local.

## New Zealand Number Porting

Port New Zealand local, national, and toll-free numbers to Telnyx. Mobile numbers are not available for porting.

### Portable Number Types

| Number Type | Portable |
| --- | --- |
| Local Numbers | Yes |
| National Numbers | Yes |
| Toll-Free Numbers | Yes |
| Mobile Numbers | Not available |

### Expected Timeframes

| Number Type | Expected Timeframe |
| --- | --- |
| Local / National / Toll-Free | 5–10 business days |

Actual times may vary based on the losing carrier's responsiveness. A separate table in the source also lists 10+ business days for local, national, and toll-free ports; treat the longer estimate as the conservative figure.

### Document Requirements

- LOA signed by the authorized person
- Latest invoice from current carrier, dated within the last 30 days
- Full contact information for the authorized person
- Local address within New Zealand

**Personal accounts:** full name, contact phone number, local address within New Zealand.

**Business accounts:** full name of authorized person, contact phone number, company name, local address within New Zealand.

### LOA

📄 [Download the New Zealand Letter of Authorization](https://assets.ctfassets.net/taysl255dolk/6YYtHkPiDoOfhR8Vp3QxUz/c9e0cb60d5051829eb44349be299df79/LoA_-_Telnyx_-_INTL.pdf)

The New Zealand LOA uses the same format as the Australian LOA. Before completing it:

- Save in PDF format (non-editable)
- Fill in all required fields completely
- Ensure information matches your current carrier's records exactly
- Include a valid New Zealand address

### Porting Process

1. Gather all required documents.
2. Submit port request through [Mission Control](https://portal.telnyx.com).
3. Upload LOA and invoice.
4. Telnyx submits request to the losing carrier.
5. Receive FOC confirmation with activation date.
6. Numbers activate on the scheduled FOC date.

### Porting Hours

Monday to Friday through two time slots: 8 AM or 12 PM.
