# DID Requirements: Saudi Arabia, UAE, Kuwait, Oman, Zambia, Mozambique, Mayotte, Reunion

Consolidated Telnyx guidance for acquiring DID numbers across eight markets, detailing identity and address verification, required forms (e.g., LOI), business-use constraints, special routing rules, and any minimum service periods or activation timelines.

## General guidance
- Identity and address verification are required; documents must be current and legible.
- Business use is required in several markets; private use may be disallowed as noted below.
- Letters of Intent (LOI) and service acceptance forms may be required; use the country-specific templates where provided.
- Some countries enforce minimum service periods or special routing/end‑user rules; review the “Additional information” items per country before ordering.

## Saudi Arabia
### Local numbers (business use only)
Business identity verification:
- Authorized representative’s full name and contact phone number
- Company name
- Passport or ID copy of the authorized representative (must be Saudi‑issued for Saudi companies)
- Company registration certificate
- Trade License (Saudi companies only)
- Traffic forecast
- Signed Service Agreement Form (provided by Telnyx/NumberOps)

Address verification:
- Saudi address (street, building number, postal code, city, country)
- Proof of address dated within 3 months

Additional information:
- Business use required; private use not allowed
- Minimum service period: 1 year; early termination not permitted before the end of this period and all charges/fees apply for the full term

### Toll‑free numbers (business use only)
Business identity verification:
- Authorized representative’s full name and contact phone number
- Company name
- Passport or ID copy of the authorized representative (must be Saudi‑issued for Saudi companies)
- Company registration certificate
- Trade License (Saudi companies only)
- Traffic forecast
- Signed LOI (dated within 1 month) — LOI template: [LOI Template .docx](https://telnyx-48416ce2297b.intercom-attachments-7.com/i/o/ltcafuzd/1859025267/5652fb13683c7d1ee3b4a8fe81e9/LOI+Template+.docx)
- Signed Service Agreement Form (provided by Telnyx/NumberOps) — [Service Acceptance Doc.xlsx](https://telnyx-48416ce2297b.intercom-attachments-2.com/i/o/ltcafuzd/1859025488/a8c212dc6e2506f4e7574b1c446b/Service+Acceptance+Doc.xlsx)

Address verification:
- Worldwide address (street, building number, postal code, city, country)
- Proof of address dated within 3 months

Additional information:
- Business use required; private use not allowed
- Minimum service period: 1 year; early termination not permitted and all charges/fees apply for the full term

## United Arab Emirates (UAE)
### Local numbers (business use only)
Business identity verification:
- Owner’s full name and company name
- Owner’s contact phone number
- Certificate of registration
- Copy of owner’s ID or passport
- Business use case
- Traffic forecast

Address verification:
- UAE address (street, building number, postal code, city, country)

Additional information:
- Business use required; private use not allowed
- Compliance review required; requests may be declined at Telnyx’s discretion without obligation to provide a reason
- Minimum service period: 1 year; early termination not permitted and all charges/fees apply for the full term

### Toll‑free numbers (business use only)
Business identity verification:
- Authorized representative’s full name, contact phone number, and contact email
- Company name
- Company registration certificate (must be a non‑UAE entity)
- Recently signed LOI (within 1 month) — template: [LOI Template.docx](https://telnyx-48416ce2297b.intercom-attachments-7.com/i/o/ltcafuzd/2242616930/4cd7f33140a4663f17ecb43d6888/LOI+Template.docx)
- Traffic forecast

Address verification:
- UAE address (street, building number, postal code, city, country)

Additional information:
- Business use required; private use not allowed
- Call routing: toll‑free numbers cannot land or be forwarded to destinations within the UAE
- Usage: subject to disconnection if no traffic for 6 consecutive months
- End‑user must not be based in the UAE
- Minimum service period: 1 year; early termination not permitted and all charges/fees apply for the full term
