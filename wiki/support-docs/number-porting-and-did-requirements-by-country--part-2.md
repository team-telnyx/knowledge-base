# Number Porting and DID Requirements by Country

*Part 2 of 2 — see also: [Part 1](number-porting-and-did-requirements-by-country--part-1.md)*

This page consolidates Telnyx's country-specific number porting and DID acquisition requirements across the Americas, Europe, and the Caribbean. It covers required documentation (LOA, invoices, IDs, tax IDs, company registration), porting hours, expected timeframes, and links to downloadable LOA templates for each supported country.

## Anguilla

### Toll-Free Numbers

For business identity verification:
- Company name
- Signed LOI (dated within 1 month)

For address verification:
- Address worldwide (street, building number, postal code, city, and country)

> Business use is required; private use is not allowed.

See the [Toll-Free Numbers pricing page](https://telnyx.com/pricing/numbers) to purchase.

## Antigua and Barbuda

### Toll-Free Numbers

For business identity verification:
- Company name
- Signed LOI (dated within 1 month)

For address verification:
- Address worldwide (street, building number, postal code, city, and country)

> Business use is required; private use is not allowed.

See the [Antigua and Barbuda numbers pricing page](https://telnyx.com/pricing/numbers) to purchase.

## Spain

### Local, National, and Toll-Free Numbers
1. LOA
2. CIF/NIF
3. Latest Invoice
4. Copy of Authorized person Passport/ID
5. Number Type
6. Proof of address

### Number Type Options
- **Analog Geographic (AG):** Select for a simple port of a geographic number with no associated services.
- **PBX Geographic (PBX G):** Select for numbers that are part of a PBX extension.
- **Intelligent Network (IN):** Select for [VoIP numbers](https://telnyx.com/resources/voip-number).

Download the [Spain LOA](https://assets.ctfassets.net/taysl255dolk/4mfyR6jMGb8TvN0p5GQOUZ/b98742b348ba0c623ffab3d99ba3db31/TELNYX_ES_LOA.pdf).

## Cyprus

### Local Numbers
1. LOA (with Local address)
2. Latest Invoice with the current carrier
3. Company Registration (if the number belongs to a company)
4. ID or Passport Number of the legal representative
5. Proof of address

### National and Toll-Free Numbers
1. LOA (with National address)
2. Latest Invoice with the current carrier
3. Company Registration (if the number belongs to a company)
4. ID or Passport Number of the legal representative
5. Proof of address

Download the [Cyprus LOA](https://assets.ctfassets.net/taysl255dolk/1Cq2IXxuZiFRu03aA3LXB6/8006b5ee40f97ac95f050be7013c8bcb/CYPRUS_LOA-_Telnyx.pdf).

## Turkey

### Local, National Numbers
1. The most recent invoice with the carrier
2. LOA with name and address of the business, local company registration number
3. Copy of the ID of the Authorized Person
4. Copy of official TAX ID issued by the Turkish government
5. Copy of corporate signatories filed with the Turkish government
6. A description of the activities of the business

Download the [Turkey LOA](https://assets.ctfassets.net/taysl255dolk/6YYtHkPiDoOfhR8Vp3QxUz/3f7b77cf78ecdebe280c6c0226ac12ea/LoA_-_Telnyx_-_INTL.pdf).

## General Notes

- **Processing times** are typical estimates; actual times may vary based on the losing carrier.
- **LOA templates** are available for most countries via the links above. For countries without a direct download link, contact [porting.intl@telnyx.com](mailto:porting.intl@telnyx.com).
- **DID numbers** are virtual and can be assigned or reassigned to any phone or device. Businesses can create unlimited DIDs on a single SIP trunk with few limitations.
- **Telnyx connectivity:** Telnyx currently offers local calls in 49 countries and PSTN replacement in 35+ countries, and owns and operates its own IP network for more secure and reliable calls.
- **Benefits of local DIDs:** Customers are [400% more likely to answer calls from local numbers](https://gtmnow.com/5-ways-to-get-your-prospects-to-pick-up-their-phones/), DIDs improve call routing, scale with business growth, enhance privacy, and enable advanced call tracking and analytics.
