# Telnyx Global Number Requirements and Porting Guide

*Part 4 of 4 — see also: [Part 1](telnyx-global-number-requirements-and-porting-guide--part-1.md), [Part 2](telnyx-global-number-requirements-and-porting-guide--part-2.md), [Part 3](telnyx-global-number-requirements-and-porting-guide--part-3.md)*

A consolidated reference covering Telnyx DID purchase requirements across multiple countries (Turkey, Australia, Uruguay, Cyprus, Greece, Montenegro, Brazil, North Macedonia, Serbia) and number porting procedures for Australia, New Zealand, and Singapore, including document checklists, processing timeframes, and category-specific rules.

## Singapore Number Porting

Port Singapore phone numbers to Telnyx. This covers requirements, timelines, and the porting process for local numbers.

### Portable Number Types

| Number Type | Portable | Port Type |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Local Numbers (Level 6) | Yes | DEL or DID/DDI |
| National Numbers | Not available | — |
| Toll-Free Numbers | Not available | — |
| Mobile Numbers | Not available | — |

### Expected Timeframes

| Port Type | Expected Timeframe |
| --- | --- |
| DEL (Simple) | 6–7 business days |
| DID/DDI (Complex) | 8–18 business days |

These are expected timeframes between carriers. Delays may occur if the losing carrier is slow to respond.

### Understanding Port Types

**DEL (Direct Exchange Line) — Simple Port** is selected for single-line PSTN services, standard telephone services, and numbers without complex features.

**DID/DDI (Direct Inward Dialling) — Complex Port** is selected for number ranges or blocks, numbers with complex services, PABX or private system numbers, and numbers that allow direct dialling without operator intervention. If porting a number block, the full range must be ported or the current carrier must disassociate the specific numbers to be ported.

### Document Requirements

For **company accounts**:
- LOA — country-specific LOA signed by the authorized person
- Business Registration — Business Registration Certificate or Certificate of Incorporation
- Work Permit — Work Permit or Employment Pass (for non-citizen applicants)
- Latest Invoice — from current carrier

For **personal accounts**:
- LOA — country-specific LOA signed by the end-user
- NRIC/Passport — National Registration Identity Card or Passport
- Work Permit — Work Permit or Employment Pass (for non-citizen applicants)
- Latest Invoice — from current carrier

### Download LOA

[Download the Singapore Letter of Authorization](https://images.telnyx.com/Telnyx-Singapore-LOA.pdf)

Before completing the LOA:
- Save in PDF format (non-editable)
- Fill in all designated fields correctly
- Ensure information matches your current carrier's records exactly
- Signature must be from the authorized person (company) or end-user (personal)

### Changing Your FOC Date

Once a porting date has been confirmed with the losing carrier, date changes require at least 72 business hours (SGT) notice before the scheduled cutover. Contact Telnyx porting support to request a change.

### Porting Process

1. Gather all required documents based on the account type.
2. Submit port request through [Mission Control](https://portal.telnyx.com).
3. Select the correct port type (DEL or DID/DDI).
4. Upload LOA and supporting documents.
5. Telnyx submits a request to the losing carrier.
6. Receive FOC confirmation with activation date.
7. Numbers activate on the scheduled FOC date.

For questions about Singapore number porting, contact [porting@telnyx.com](mailto:porting@telnyx.com).
