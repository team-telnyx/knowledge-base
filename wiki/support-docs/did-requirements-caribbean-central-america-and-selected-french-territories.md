# DID Requirements: Caribbean, Central America, and Selected French Territories

This page consolidates Telnyx’s documented requirements to purchase DID numbers across select Caribbean, Central American, and French overseas locations, detailing the identity, address, LOI, and other country‑specific prerequisites by number type.

## Overview
Use this guide to understand what documents and information Telnyx needs to provision phone numbers (DIDs) in the countries listed below. Requirements vary by country and number type (local, national, toll‑free, mobile) and may specify who can buy (personal vs. business), what proof is required, and whether local presence or business use is mandated.

## Key terms used
- Personal identity: Typically full name, contact phone, and a passport/ID copy.
- Business identity: Company name, contact phone, and company registration/incorporation proof; sometimes an authorized representative’s details.
- Address verification: “Address Worldwide” means any global address; “Address in <country>” requires an in‑country address. Some countries also require recent proof of address (e.g., dated within 3 months).
- LOI (Letter of Intent): A signed letter (often dated within the last month) authorizing number use/ordering. Some pages reference a Telnyx-provided LOI template.
- Traffic forecast: An expected-usage forecast that must be provided before ordering.
- Business use required: Numbers can only be used for business purposes (no private use).

## Anguilla
Number type: Toll‑Free
- Business identity: Company name; Signed LOI (dated within 1 month).
- Address verification: Address Worldwide.
- Notes: Business use required (no private use).

## Antigua and Barbuda
Number type: Toll‑Free
- Business identity: Company name; Signed LOI (dated within 1 month).
- Address verification: Address Worldwide.
- Notes: Business use required (no private use).

## Costa Rica
Number type: National
- Business identity: Name, last name; Contact phone number; Company name; Company incorporation certificate copy.
- Address verification: Address Worldwide.
- Notes: Business use required (no private use).

Number type: Toll‑Free
- Business identity: Name, last name of an authorized representative; Company name; Contact phone number; Passport or ID copy of an authorized representative; Power of attorney; Extract from the Commercial Register (Chamber of Commerce).
- Address verification: Address Worldwide; Proof of address dated within 3 months.
- Notes: Business use required (no private use).

## Dominican Republic
Number types: Local and Toll‑Free
- Personal identity: Name, last name; Contact phone number; Passport or ID copy.
- Business identity: Company name; Contact phone number; Company registration certificate.
- Address verification: Address Worldwide.
- Pricing: See Dominican Republic number pricing at https://telnyx.com/pricing/numbers/do

## El Salvador
Number type: Local
- Required: Traffic forecast (expected usage) must be provided.

## Honduras
Number type: Local
- Personal identity: Name, last name; Contact phone number; LOI (Telnyx template: https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/ltcafuzd/1974079146/f3eb9460203bfedda610a97379a8/LOI+Template.docx).
- Business identity: Name, last name of an authorized representative; Company name; Contact phone number; LOI (same Telnyx template above).
- Address verification: Address in Honduras; Proof of address dated within 3 months.

## Jamaica
Number type: Local
- Personal identity: Signed LOI (dated within 1 month).
- Business identity: Signed LOI (dated within 1 month).
- Address verification: Address Worldwide.

Number type: Toll‑Free
- Personal identity: Name, last name; Signed LOI (dated within 1 month).
- Business identity: Company name; Signed LOI (dated within 1 month).
- Address verification: Address Worldwide.

## Nicaragua
Number type: Mobile
- Business identity: Name, last name of an authorized representative; Company name; Contact phone number; Local copy of Passport/ID; Local Company Registration Certificate; LOI (Telnyx template: https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/ltcafuzd/1974079146/f3eb9460203bfedda610a97379a8/LOI+Template.docx).
- Address verification: Address in Nicaragua; Proof of address dated within 3 months.
- Notes: Business use required (no private use).

## French overseas territories in scope (French Guiana, Guadeloupe, Martinique, Mayotte, Reunion, Saint Barthélemy, Saint Martin)
Number type: Local
- Personal identity: Name, last name; Contact phone number; Passport or ID copy.
- Business identity: Company name; Contact phone number; Company registration certificate.
- Address verification: Address Worldwide.

## Compliance tips
- Date sensitivity: Where specified, LOIs must be dated within the last month; proofs of address often must be dated within the last 3 months.
- Match number type to requirements: Some countries only list specific types (e.g., Nicaragua: Mobile only; Anguilla/Antigua: Toll‑Free only).
- Local presence: Where “Address in <country>” is required (e.g., Honduras, Nicaragua), ensure you can provide a valid in‑country address and recent proof.
- Business-only markets: Observe “business use required” restrictions to avoid provisioning delays (e.g., Anguilla, Antigua and Barbuda, Costa Rica, Nicaragua).
- Traffic forecast: Prepare an expected usage plan for El Salvador local numbers.
- Always verify: Requirements can change; confirm details during checkout in the Telnyx portal before submitting documentation.
