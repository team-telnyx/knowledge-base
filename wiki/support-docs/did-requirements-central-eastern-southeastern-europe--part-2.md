# DID Requirements — Central, Eastern & Southeastern Europe

*Part 2 of 3 — see also: [Part 1](did-requirements-central-eastern-southeastern-europe--part-1.md), [Part 3](did-requirements-central-eastern-southeastern-europe--part-3.md)*

A consolidated guide to Telnyx KYC and address requirements for purchasing DID numbers across Albania, Bosnia & Herzegovina, Bulgaria, Croatia, Cyprus, Czech Republic, Hungary, North Macedonia, Poland, Romania, Russia, Serbia, Slovakia, Slovenia, and Ukraine. Requirements are organized by number type (Local, National, Mobile, Toll‑Free) with country‑specific rules, document freshness, and special constraints.

## Country‑specific requirements (by number type)
### Albania
- Local (Business only):
  - Business identity: authorized representative’s name, company name, contact phone, authorized rep Passport/ID, Company Registration Certificate.
  - Address: worldwide accepted.
  - Additional: Business use required (private use not allowed).
- Toll‑Free (Business only):
  - Business identity: company name, authorized rep Passport/ID, Company Registration Certificate.
  - Address: worldwide accepted.
  - Additional: Business use required.

### Bosnia & Herzegovina
- Local:
  - Personal identity: name, surname, contact phone, Bosnian & Herzegovinian Passport/ID copy.
  - Business identity: authorized representative’s name, contact, company name, Bosnian & Herzegovinian Company Registration Certificate.
  - Address: must match DID area code + POA within 3 months.
- Toll‑Free & National:
  - Personal identity: name, surname, contact phone, Passport/ID copy.
  - Business identity: company name, contact, Company Registration Certificate.
  - Address: worldwide accepted.

### Bulgaria
- Local:
  - Personal identity: name, surname, contact phone, Passport/ID copy.
  - Business identity: authorized representative’s name, company name, contact, Company Registration Certificate.
  - Address: must match DID area code + POA within 3 months.
- Toll‑Free:
  - Personal identity: name, surname, contact phone.
  - Business identity: authorized representative’s name, company name, contact.
  - Address: worldwide accepted.

### Croatia
- Local (Business only):
  - Business identity: legal representative’s name, company name, contact phone, legal rep ID/passport, Company Registration Certificate.
  - Address: must match DID area code + POA within 3 months.
  - Additional: Business use required (private use not allowed).
- Toll‑Free:
  - Personal identity: name, surname, contact phone, Passport/ID copy.
  - Business identity: legal representative’s name, company name, contact, legal rep Passport/ID, Company Incorporation Certificate.
  - Address: must be in Croatia.

### Cyprus
- Local:
  - Personal identity: name, surname, contact phone, Passport/ID copy.
  - Business identity: authorized representative’s name, company name, contact, Cyprian Company Registration Certificate.
  - Address: worldwide accepted + POA within 3 months.
- National:
  - Personal identity: name, surname, contact phone, Passport/ID copy.
  - Business identity: name, surname, contact, company name, Cyprian Company Incorporation Certificate copy.
  - Address: worldwide accepted + utility bill less than 6 months old.
- Toll‑Free:
  - Personal identity: name, surname, contact phone, Passport/ID copy.
  - Business identity: authorized representative’s name, company name, contact, authorized rep Passport/ID.
  - Address: worldwide accepted + POA within 3 months.

### Czech Republic
- Local:
  - Personal identity: name, surname, contact phone, Local Passport/ID copy.
  - Business identity: authorized representative’s name, company name, contact, Local Company Registration Certificate.
  - Address: must match DID area code + POA within 3 months.
  - Additional: End‑users must be physically present in the country to purchase.
- Toll‑Free:
  - Personal identity: name, surname, contact phone, Local Passport/ID copy.
  - Business identity: company name, contact, Local Company Registration Certificate.
  - Address: must be in Czech Republic + POA within 3 months.
  - Additional: End‑users must be physically present in the country to purchase.

### Hungary
- Local:
  - Personal identity: name, surname, contact phone, Passport/ID copy.
  - Business identity: authorized representative’s name, company name, contact, Company Registration Certificate.
  - Address: must match DID area code + POA within 3 months.
- National:
  - Personal identity: name, surname, contact phone, Passport/ID copy.
  - Business identity: authorized representative’s name, company name, contact, Company Registration Certificate.
  - Address: must be in Hungary + POA within 3 months.
- Toll‑Free:
  - Personal identity: name, surname, contact phone, Passport/ID copy.
  - Business identity: authorized representative’s name, company name, contact, Company Registration Certificate.
  - Address: must be in Hungary + POA within 3 months.

### North Macedonia
- Local:
  - Personal identity: name, surname, contact phone, North Macedonian Passport/ID copy.
  - Business identity: authorized representative’s name, contact phone, company name, North Macedonian Company Incorporation Certificate copy.
  - Address: must match DID area code + utility bill less than 6 months old.
  - Additional: Service usage description required.
- Toll‑Free:
  - Personal identity: name, surname, contact phone, Passport/ID copy.
  - Business identity: company name, contact phone, Company Registration Certificate.
  - Address: worldwide accepted.

### Poland
- Local:
  - Personal identity: name, surname, contact phone, Local Passport/ID copy.
  - Business identity: authorized representative’s name, company name, contact, local legal papers of company registration.
  - Address: must match DID area code + POA within 3 months.
  - Additional: Service usage description; end‑users must be physically present in Poland to purchase.
- Mobile:
  - Personal identity: name, surname, contact phone, Local Passport/ID copy.
  - Business identity: authorized representative’s name, company name, contact, Local Company Registration Certificate.
  - Address: must be in Poland + POA within 3 months.
  - Additional: End‑users must be physically present in Poland to purchase.
- Toll‑Free:
  - Personal identity: name, surname, contact phone, Local Passport/ID copy.
  - Business identity: authorized representative’s name, company name, contact, copy of PESEL, NIP, REGON, KRS or foreign ID of the authorized representative, Local Company Registration Certificate.
  - Address: must be in Poland + POA within 3 months.
  - Additional: End‑users must be physically present in Poland to purchase.

### Romania
- Local, National, Toll‑Free (same pattern for all):
  - Personal identity: name, surname, contact phone, Local Passport/ID copy.
  - Business identity: authorized representative’s name, company name, contact, Local Company Registration Certificate.
  - Address: must be in Romania + POA within 3 months.
  - Additional: End‑users must be physically present in Romania to purchase.

### Russia
- Mobile (Business only):
  - Business identity: authorized representative’s name, company name, contact phone, authorized rep Passport/ID, Business Registration Certificate, Signed LOI (dated within 1 month).
  - Address: worldwide accepted, with POA matching the address on the Business Registration Certificate (POA within 3 months).
  - Additional: Business use required (private use not allowed).

### Serbia
- Toll‑Free:
  - Personal identity: name, surname, contact phone, Passport/ID copy.
  - Business identity: company name, contact phone, Company Registration Certificate.
  - Address: worldwide accepted.

### Slovakia
- Local:
  - Personal identity: name, surname, contact phone, Passport/ID copy.
  - Business identity: authorized representative’s name, company name, contact phone, authorized rep Passport/ID copy, Company Registration Certificate.
  - Address: must match DID area code + POA within 3 months.
- Toll‑Free (Business only):
  - Business identity: authorized representative’s name, company name, Company Registration Certificate, VAT number.
  - Address: worldwide accepted.
  - Additional: Business use required (private use not allowed).

### Slovenia
- Local & National (Business only):
  - Business identity: name, company name, contact phone, ID/Passport copy.
  - Address: must match DID area code + POA within 3 months.
  - Additional: Business use required (private use not allowed).
- Toll‑Free:
  - Personal identity: name, surname, contact phone, Passport/ID copy.
  - Business identity: company name, contact phone, Company Registration Certificate.
  - Address: worldwide accepted.

### Ukraine
- Local & National (Business only):
  - Business identity: authorized representative’s name, company name, contact phone, authorized rep Passport/ID copy, Business Registration Certificate, Signed LOI (dated within 1 month).
  - Address: worldwide accepted, with POA matching the address on the Business Registration Certificate (POA within 3 months).
  - Additional: Business use required (private use not allowed).
- Mobile & Toll‑Free:
  - Personal identity: name, surname, contact phone, Passport/ID copy.
  - Business identity: company name, contact phone, Company Registration Certificate.
  - Address: worldwide accepted.
