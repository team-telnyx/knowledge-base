# Europe DID Requirements (Telnyx)

*Part 1 of 2 — see also: [Part 2](europe-did-requirements-telnyx--part-2.md)*

Consolidated regulatory requirements for purchasing Telnyx DID numbers across selected European countries. This guide lists identity and address documents by number type, highlights special constraints (e.g., business-only use, physical-presence rules, regulator forms), and notes where processing typically takes ~72 hours after document submission.

## How to read this guide
- Number types covered per country: Local, National, Toll-Free, Mobile (plus Shared-Cost where applicable).
- Common document themes:
  - Personal identity: Name, last name, contact phone; often local passport/ID copy; sometimes contact email, date/place of birth.
  - Business identity: Authorized representative’s name, company name, contact phone (and sometimes email/website); local business registration certificate; VAT/tax IDs where applicable.
  - Address verification: Service address (often must be in-country; for Local numbers it often must match the area code). Proof of address (utility bill or similar) typically dated within 3 months.
- Processing time: Several countries note activation typically ~72 hours after documentation is received.
- Requirement Groups: Mandatory for Italy, Sweden, Switzerland, and Portugal (from Sept 16, 2024). See the setup guide: https://support.telnyx.com/en/articles/9801714-requirement-groups-for-ordering-phone-numbers.

## Austria
### Local
- Personal: Name, last name, contact phone, local passport/ID copy.
- Business: Authorized representative name, company name, contact phone, local passport/ID copy (rep), local company registration certificate.
- Address: Address matching DID area code; proof of address (≤3 months).
- Notes: End-user must be physically present in Austria when purchasing.

### National
- Personal: Name, last name, contact phone, local passport/ID copy.
- Business: Name, contact phone, passport/ID copy, company name, local company incorporation certificate.
- Address: Address in Austria; utility bill (≤3 months).
- Notes: Physical presence required.

### Toll-Free & Mobile
- Personal: Name, last name, contact phone, local passport/ID copy.
- Business: Company name, contact phone, local company registration certificate.
- Address: Address in Austria; utility bill (≤3 months).
- Notes: Physical presence required.

## Belgium
### Local
- Personal: Name, last name, contact phone, local passport/ID copy.
- Business: Authorized representative name, company name, contact phone, local passport/ID (rep), local company registration certificate, VAT number.
- Address: Address matching DID area code; proof of address (≤3 months).
- Notes: End-user must be physically present in Belgium.

### Toll-Free
- Personal: Name, last name, contact phone, local passport/ID copy.
- Business: As Local (incl. VAT, company registration, rep ID).
- Address: Address in Belgium; proof of address (≤3 months).
- Notes: Physical presence required. ~72-hour validation after docs received.

### Mobile
- Personal: Name, last name, contact phone, contact email, local passport/ID copy.
- Business: Authorized representative name, company name, contact phone, contact email, VAT number, local company registration certificate, local passport/ID (rep).
- Address: Address in Belgium; proof of address (≤3 months).
- Notes: Physical presence required. ~72-hour validation.

## France
### Local
- Personal: Name, last name, contact phone, local passport/ID copy.
- Business: Authorized representative name, company name, contact phone, local company registration certificate.
- Address: Address in France; proof of address (≤3 months).
- Notes: Physical presence required.

### National
- Personal: Name, last name, contact phone, local passport/ID copy.
- Business: Authorized representative name, company name, contact phone, local company registration certificate.
- Address: Address in France; proof of address (≤3 months).
- Notes: Physical presence required.

### Toll-Free
- Business: Authorized representative name, company name, contact phone, local company registration certificate, SIREN (company registration number).
- Address: Address in France; proof of address (≤3 months).
- Notes: Physical presence required. Business use only (no private use). ~72-hour validation.

## Germany
### Local
- Personal: Name, last name, contact phone, local passport/ID copy, Germany registration form (signed; include rep name, surname, date, and company stamp or handwritten company name).
- Business: Authorized representative name, company name, contact phone, local company registration certificate, Germany registration form (signed as above).
- Address: Address matching DID area code; utility bill (≤3 months).
- Notes: Physical presence required. Only scanned documents are accepted.

### National
- Personal: Name, last name, contact phone, local passport/ID copy, Germany registration form (signed).
- Business: Authorized representative name, company name, contact phone, local passport/ID (rep), local company registration certificate, Germany registration form (signed).
- Address: Address in Germany matching PoA/ID; proof of address (≤3 months).
- Notes: Physical presence required. Only scanned documents accepted.

### Toll-Free
- Business: Authorized representative name, company name, contact phone, place of birth (rep), local passport/ID (rep), signed LOI (≤1 month), Power of Attorney (provided by Telnyx), local company registration certificate.
- Address: Address in Germany matching the company registration certificate; proof of address (≤3 months).
- Notes: Physical presence required. Only scanned docs accepted. Service usage description and marketing details in German required. Business use only. ~72-hour validation.

## Ireland
### Local
- Personal: Name, last name, contact phone, contact email, local passport/ID copy.
- Business: Name, last name, contact phone, company name, contact email, local business registration certificate.
- Address: Address matching DID area code; proof of address (≤3 months).
- Notes: Service usage description required.

### National & Toll-Free
- Personal: Name, last name, contact phone, contact email, local passport/ID copy.
- Business: Name, last name, contact phone, company name, contact email, local business registration certificate.
- Address: Address in Ireland; proof of address (≤3 months).
- Notes: Service usage description required. ~72-hour validation.

### Mobile
- Business: Name, last name, contact phone, company name, contact email, local business registration certificate.
- Address: Address in Ireland; proof of address (≤3 months).
- Notes: Service usage description required. Business use only (no private use). ~72-hour validation.

## Italy
- Requirement Groups mandatory for ordering. All documents must be submitted in .pdf. Additional validating details may be requested upon review.

### Local
- Customer types: natural_person (personal, non-commercial; tax code required; no VAT), legal_entity (has VAT; registered), sole_proprietorship (has VAT; owner-operator).
- Personal (natural persons & sole proprietorships): Name, last name, date of birth, place of birth, personal tax code, local passport/ID (front/back), ID type/issuer/number/expiry.
- Business (legal entities & sole proprietorships): Authorized representative name, rep date/place of birth, rep local passport/ID (front/back) with ID details, local company registration certificate, VAT number, supplemental address of legal representative’s residence in Italy (legal entity only).
- Address: Address in Italy; proof of address (≤3 months).
- Notes: Physical presence required.

### Toll-Free
- Business: Business registration certificate, VAT number, local passport/ID (rep, front/back), company representative’s Tax ID.
- Address: Address in Italy; proof of address (≤3 months).
- Notes: Physical presence required.
