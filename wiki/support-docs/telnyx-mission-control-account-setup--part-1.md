# Telnyx Mission Control Account Setup

*Part 1 of 5 — see also: [Part 2](telnyx-mission-control-account-setup--part-2.md), [Part 3](telnyx-mission-control-account-setup--part-3.md), [Part 4](telnyx-mission-control-account-setup--part-4.md), [Part 5](telnyx-mission-control-account-setup--part-5.md)*

A consolidated guide to creating and configuring a Telnyx Mission Control account, covering sign-up, verification, Freemium and Managed Accounts, AI Assistants, messaging, voice, and the in-portal AI support assistant.

## Overview

Telnyx Mission Control is the portal used to manage Telnyx communications services, including voice, messaging, AI, and networking products. This page consolidates the steps and resources needed to create an account, verify it, configure it for messaging or voice, set up AI Assistants, and use the in-portal AI support chat.

## Signing Up for a Telnyx Account

The sign-up landing page is at [telnyx.com/sign-up](https://telnyx.com/sign-up). Two options are available:

1. Single Sign-On with Gmail or Microsoft using the provided buttons.
2. Direct sign-up using the form, which requires an email address, full name, and password.

A business email address is mandatory — certain free email providers are disallowed. Promo codes can be applied via the link below the password field, and the opt-in checkbox controls marketing emails. Click **Create Account** to proceed.

### Know Your Customer (KYC)

As part of creating a trustworthy environment, Telnyx integrates a KYC procedure into sign-up. Users may be prompted to complete an identity check before the account is created. This step helps prevent identity theft, financial fraud, and money laundering.

### Email and Phone Verification

After submitting details, a "Verify Your Account" email is sent. Open it and click **Verify email address**. If the email is not visible, check the spam folder or use the **Resend verification email** link. Once the email is verified, phone verification may also be required via a one-time passcode.

### Use Case Selection

After activation, a use-case prompt appears. Enter the organization name, then select the relevant Telnyx features. The menu expands to include an **I'm a Developer** option along with company size and preferred contact number.

### Currency Limitation

New users signing up after 18 October 2022 can only choose USD. Existing accounts with more than one month of spend cannot change their account currency.

### Post-Sign-Up Routing

After completing setup, users are redirected based on their selected features:

- **SMS Use Cases** are routed to the [Learn & Build](https://portal.telnyx.com/#/app/messaging/learn-and-build) section, which introduces sending a first SMS, purchasing a number, and creating a messaging profile.
- **SIP Trunking Use Cases** receive a number (purchased using free sign-up credit), a SIP Connection, and an Outbound Voice Profile. The Outbound Voice Profile is assigned to the SIP Connection, and the Connection is assigned to the number.

### Troubleshooting Sign-Up

- If the login button is disabled, verify that the browser is not blocking Google scripts for reCAPTCHA.
- If sign-in fails with "You cannot sign in as your identity verification was rejected", email [support@telnyx.com](mailto:support@telnyx.com).

## Freemium Accounts

A Freemium account lets developers try Telnyx AI products with no credit card, no payment setup, and no commitment. Sign up, receive $25 in AI credits, and start building.

### Recent Freemium Improvements

- Freemium users can see remaining AI credits and the number of days left to use them.
- No password is required when the account is upgraded; users can continue signing in with a magic link and change the method on the Security page.
- The login page is streamlined for all account types.

### Getting Started

1. Sign up at [telnyx.com/sign-up](https://telnyx.com/sign-up) using an email address.
2. Check the inbox for a magic link — click it to log in. No password is needed.
3. Start building — the $25 AI credit is automatically applied.

A magic link is a secure, one-time login URL sent to the email. Each sign-in generates a new magic link instead of using a password.

### What Is Included

- **$25 in AI credits** covering usage across the entire Telnyx AI product suite.
- **One US local phone number** at no cost.
- **Full access to AI products** including Telnyx Inference and AI Assistants.

### How the $25 AI Credit Works

The credit is applied automatically as a discount on AI product usage. It covers up to $25 in total AI usage plus one US local phone number. No activation or redemption is required.

### What Is Available on Freemium

Freemium accounts are designed for exploring Telnyx AI products. Other products (Voice, Messaging, Networking, etc.) require a full account. Non-AI API calls on a Freemium account typically return an error.

### Upgrading to a Full Account

To upgrade:

1. Log in to the [Telnyx Portal](https://portal.telnyx.com).
2. Click the profile icon in the top corner.
3. Go to **Account Level** and click **Upgrade**.
4. Verify identity through **LinkedIn** or **GitHub**.

Notes on the upgrade process:

- There are two verification attempts — one via LinkedIn and one via GitHub. If one is rejected, the other can be tried.
- Once approved, the account becomes a standard Telnyx trial account with access to all products.
- After upgrading, the $25 AI credit is replaced with a $5 full-credit usable on any Telnyx product or service.

If the upgrade is rejected:

- Try the other verification method.
- If both are rejected, create a new Telnyx account using a business email address.
- If both routes have been tried with a business email, contact [Telnyx Support](https://telnyx.com/contact-us).

Each LinkedIn or GitHub account can only verify one Telnyx account.

### Cancelling a Freemium Account

To cancel, contact [Telnyx Support](https://telnyx.com/contact-us). To reuse the email address for a full business account, support can assist with the transition.

### Freemium FAQ

- **Can funds be added to a Freemium account?** No. Upgrade to a full account to add funds.
- **Can non-AI products be used?** No. Upgrade to use Voice, Messaging, Wireless, etc.
- **What happens when the $25 credit runs out?** AI usage stops. Upgrade and add funds to continue.
- **Where is the credit after upgrading?** The $25 AI credit is replaced with a $5 full-credit usable on any product.

## Account Verification

Level 1 verification is required before assigning a connection or messaging profile to a DID, or assigning a connection to an outbound voice profile. Level 2 verification is required to increase the default sending rate.

## Setting Up a Mission Control Account for Voice

To configure Mission Control for use with a PBX, softphone, or softswitch, complete the following requirements:

1. **Level 1 Verification** — required to assign connections, messaging profiles, and outbound voice profiles. See [Account Verification](account-verification.md).
2. **Connection Setup** — a connection authenticates the client with the Telnyx SIP proxy at [sip.telnyx.com](https://sip.telnyx.com/). Three authentication options are available: **Credentials**, **IP Address**, and **FQDN**. Select one method to whitelist the server to the SIP proxy domain.
3. **DIDs** — a DID is required to receive inbound calls. After purchasing a DID, assign the connection that should receive inbound calls.
4. **Outbound Voice Profile** — required to make outbound calls.
5. **Payments** — add a payment method to top up the balance, which is needed to purchase numbers, send messages, and make or receive calls.

Additional configuration resources, including signalling and media IPs for the SIP proxy, are available at [sip.telnyx.com](https://sip.telnyx.com/).
