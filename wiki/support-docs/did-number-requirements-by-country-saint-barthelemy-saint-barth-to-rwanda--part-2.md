This page consolidates the documentation requirements for purchasing Direct Inward Dialing (DID) numbers across multiple countries served by Telnyx, including Algeria, Antigua and Barbuda, Benin, Cameroon, the Democratic Republic of the Congo, Egypt, Ghana, Israel, Jamaica, Kenya, Nigeria, Rwanda, Saint Barthélemy, Saint Martin, and Uganda. It outlines the identity, business, and address verification documents required for each number type, along with general information on Telnyx's global DID offerings and benefits.

## Kenya

### Local Numbers in Kenya

For **business identity** verification:
- Name, last name of an authorized representative
- Contact phone number
- Company name

For **address** verification:
- Address worldwide (street, building number, postal code, city, and country)
- Proof of address (dated within 3 months)

**Additional information:**
- Service usage description
- Business use required: Business use is required, private use is not allowed.

- Proof of address (dated within 3 months)

## General Information on Acquiring DIDs

Telnyx [Global DID Numbers](https://telnyx.com/products/phone-numbers) are virtual and can be assigned or reassigned to any phone or device. They are flexible, allowing businesses to create unlimited DID numbers on a single [SIP trunk](https://telnyx.com/products/sip-trunks), with few limitations. Telnyx continuously expands global calling services for seamless connectivity, currently offering local calls in 49 countries and PSTN replacement in 35+ (with more coming soon).

Telnyx streamlines VoIP operations by providing both [DID numbers and SIP trunking](https://telnyx.com/resources/sip-did). This is more affordable, simplifies phone infrastructure, and reduces how many invoices you manage each month. Telnyx also owns and operates its own IPs, so calls bypass congestion on the public internet, making them more secure and reliable.
