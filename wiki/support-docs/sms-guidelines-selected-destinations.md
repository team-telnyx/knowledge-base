# SMS Guidelines: Selected Destinations

Consolidated Telnyx SMS guidelines for 15 destinations, including MCC, dial codes, sender ID support, and content restrictions. Most destinations support Alphanumeric Sender IDs without registration; Guam is the exception, requiring Long Code traffic only.

## Overview

This page consolidates Telnyx SMS guidelines for 15 destinations, covering Mobile Country Codes (MCC), international dial codes, sender ID support, and content restrictions. For most destinations listed, Alphanumeric Sender IDs are supported and maintained without registration, and there are no content restrictions. Guam is the notable exception, where Alphanumeric Sender IDs are not allowed and only Long Code traffic is permitted.

Always refer to the [Acceptable Use Policy for Messaging](acceptable-use-policy-for-messaging.md) when sending SMS to any of these destinations.

## Country Guidelines

### Andorra
- **MCC:** 213
- **Dial Code:** 376
- Alphanumeric Sender IDs are supported and maintained; no registration required.
- No content restrictions.
