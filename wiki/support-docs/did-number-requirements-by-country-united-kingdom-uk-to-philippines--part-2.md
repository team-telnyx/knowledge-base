# DID Number Requirements by Country - United Kingdom Uk to Philippines

*Part 2 of 3 — see also: [Part 1](did-number-requirements-by-country-united-kingdom-uk-to-philippines--part-1.md), [Part 3](did-number-requirements-by-country-united-kingdom-uk-to-philippines--part-3.md)*

This page consolidates Telnyx's documentation requirements for purchasing Direct Inward Dialing (DID) numbers across multiple countries, including the United Kingdom, Ireland, Reunion, Japan, China, Vietnam, Hong Kong, Malta, Singapore, Finland, Monaco, New Zealand, and the Philippines. It outlines the personal identity, business identity, and address verification documents required for each number type, along with country-specific rules and processing timelines.

## Vietnam

### Local Numbers

**Personal identity verification:**
- Name, last name
- Contact phone number
- Passport or ID copy
- Utility bill, not older than 3 months

**Business identity verification:**
- Name, last name of an authorized representative
- Contact phone number
- Contact e-mail
- Company name
- Passport or ID copy of an authorized representative
- Company registration certificate
- Signed LOI (dated within 1 month)
- Utility bill, not older than 3 months

**Address verification:**
- Address worldwide (street, building number, postal code, city, and country)

### Toll-Free Numbers

**Personal identity verification:**
- Name, last name
- Contact e-mail

**Business identity verification:**
- Company name
- Contact e-mail

**Address verification:**
- Address worldwide (street, building number, postal code, city, and country)

Calling card usage is not permitted; the number may not be used to offer calling card services.

## Hong Kong

### National Numbers

**Business identity verification:**
- Name, last name of an authorized representative
- Company name
- Contact phone number
- Company registration certificate
- Hong Kong Tripartite Agreement

**Address verification:**
- Address in Hong Kong (street, building number, postal code, city, and country)
- Proof of address (dated within 3 months)

### Toll-Free Numbers

**Business identity verification:**
- Name, last name of an authorized representative
- Company name
- Contact phone number

**Address verification:**
- Address worldwide (street, building number, postal code, city, and country)
- Proof of address (dated within 3 months)

Additional information: service usage description. Business use is required; private use is not allowed.
