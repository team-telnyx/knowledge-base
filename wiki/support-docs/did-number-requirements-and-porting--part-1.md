# DID Number Requirements and Porting

*Part 1 of 3 — see also: [Part 2](did-number-requirements-and-porting--part-2.md), [Part 3](did-number-requirements-and-porting--part-3.md)*

Consolidated reference for Telnyx DID number purchase requirements and number porting requirements across multiple countries, including Italy, Sweden, Switzerland, Denmark, Norway, Portugal, Iceland, South Africa, Zambia, Mozambique, Angola, Spain, and Portugal. Covers identity, business, and address verification requirements by number type, the mandatory Requirement Groups framework for several European countries, and porting documentation for Spain, South Africa, and Portugal.

## Overview

This page consolidates the regulatory and documentation requirements for purchasing DID (Direct Inward Dialing) phone numbers across a range of countries served by Telnyx, as well as the requirements for porting existing numbers into Telnyx in selected markets. Requirements vary by country and by number type (local, national, mobile, toll-free), and typically include identity verification, business verification, and address verification components. In several European countries, Telnyx now requires the use of **Requirement Groups** to streamline compliance when ordering numbers.

## Requirement Groups (Europe)

Starting **September 16, 2024**, **Requirement Groups** are mandatory for ordering phone numbers in the following European countries:

- Italy (IT)
- Sweden (SE)
- Switzerland (CH)
- Denmark (DK)
- Norway (NO)
- Portugal (PT)

Requirement Groups let you manage regulatory requirements by pre-filling necessary information once, allowing for reuse across multiple orders. For full details on setting up Requirement Groups, refer to the [Requirement Groups for Ordering Phone Numbers](https://support.telnyx.com/en/articles/9801714-requirement-groups-for-ordering-phone-numbers) guide.

## Identity Verification: What "Local" Means

For most orders, your proof of identity must be issued by the country in which you are making your purchase. As an exception, customers within the European Union may use a valid passport or national identity card from any EU member state, even if it differs from the EU country associated with the order; in these cases, the document will be treated as local.

All identification is subject to review, and Telnyx reserves the right to request additional documentation or decline any document that cannot be verified or that does not meet applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

## Italy

### Local Numbers

Italy supports three customer types:

- **Natural Person (Persona Fisica)** — Individual using the service for personal, non-commercial purposes at their residence. No VAT number required; no business registration; personal use only.
- **Legal Entity (Persona Giuridica)** — Public or private entities with legal capacity independent from their members (e.g., SpA, Srl, Ltd, Inc, foundations, municipalities, universities). Has VAT number; legally registered entity; business/institutional use.
- **Sole Proprietorship (Ditta Individuale)** — Business owned and operated by one individual who is personally responsible for all aspects (e.g., freelancers, small traders, artisans, consultants). Has VAT number; owner name often matches business name.

For **personal identity** verification:

- Name, last name
- Date of Birth
- Place of Birth
- Gender
- Nationality
- Personal Tax Code (for natural persons and sole proprietorships only)
- Local Copy of ID or Passport (front and back)
- Identity Document Type
- Identity Document Issuer
- Identity Document Number
- Identity Document Issuance Date (YYYY-MM-DD)
- Identity Document Expiration Date (YYYY-MM-DD)
- Contact E-mail

For **business identity** verification:

- Name, Last Name of an authorized representative (for legal entities and sole proprietorships only)
- Authorized Representative Date of Birth
- Authorized Representative Place of Birth
- Authorized Representative Gender
- Authorized Representative Nationality
- Local Copy of ID or Passport of an authorized representative (front and back)
- Identity Document Type
- Identity Document Issuer
- Identity Document Number
- Identity Document Issuance Date (YYYY-MM-DD)
- Identity Document Expiration Date (YYYY-MM-DD)
- Authorized Representative Contact E-mail
- Company Name
- Local Company Registration certificate (for legal entities and sole proprietorships only)
- VAT Number (for legal entities and sole proprietorships only)
- Supplemental Address of Legal Representative's Residence in Italy (Legal Entity Only)

For **address** verification:

- Address in Italy (street, building number, postal code, city, and country)
- Proof of address (dated within 3 months)

Additional details:

- End-users must be physically present in the country when purchasing numbers from that country.
- All documents must be submitted in `.pdf` format.
- Upon regulatory review, additional validating details may be requested in certain cases.

### Toll-Free Numbers

For **business identity** verification:

- Business Registration Certificate
- VAT number
- Local Passport or ID copy of an authorized representative (front and back)
- Company representative's Tax ID

For **address** verification:

- Address in Italy (street, building number, postal code, city and country)
- Proof of address (dated within 3 months)

Additional details:

- End-users must be physically present in the country when purchasing numbers from that country.
- Upon regulatory review, additional validating details may be requested in certain cases.

## Sweden

### Local Numbers

For **business identity** verification:

- Name, last name of an authorized representative
- Contact phone number
- Contact e-mail
- Company Name
- Passport or ID copy of an authorized representative
- Business registration certificate
- Business Registration Number

For **address** verification:

- Address matching the DID area code (street, building number, postal code, city, and country)
- Proof of address (dated within 3 months)

Additional details:

- End-users must be physically present in the country when purchasing numbers from that country.
- Business use required: Business use is required, private use is not allowed.

### National Numbers
