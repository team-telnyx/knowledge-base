# Spanish-Speaking & Latin American DID Requirements

*Part 3 of 3 — see also: [Part 1](spanish-speaking-latin-american-did-requirements--part-1.md), [Part 2](spanish-speaking-latin-american-did-requirements--part-2.md)*

This page consolidates the documentation requirements for acquiring Direct Inward Dialing (DID) numbers across Spanish-speaking and Latin American countries served by Telnyx, plus Spain. It also covers Mexico number porting. Each country has its own combination of personal identity, business identity, and address verification requirements, and many require a Letter of Intent (LOI) or specific local documentation. Where noted, validation typically takes approximately 72 hours after documentation is received.

## Venezuela

### Local Numbers

**Personal identity verification:**
- Name, last name
- Contact phone number
- Venezuelan Passport or ID copy

**Business identity verification:**
- Name, last name of an authorized representative
- Contact phone number
- Company name
- Venezuelan company registration certificate

**Address verification:**
- Address in Venezuela (street, building number, postal code, city and country)
- Proof of address (dated within 3 months)

**Additional information:**
- Service usage description

### Toll-Free Numbers

**Personal identity verification:**
- Name, last name
- Contact phone number

**Business identity verification:**
- Name, last name of an authorized representative
- Company name
- Contact phone number
- Company registration certificate

**Address verification:**
- Address worldwide (street, building number, postal code, city and country)
- Proof of address (dated within 3 months)

**Additional information:**
- Service usage description

*Please note that once the documentation is received it will take approximately 72 hours to validate the information and activate the number for use.*

See [Venezuela DID Requirements](venezuela-did-requirements.md) for the full source page.

## Mexico Number Porting

### Local / National Numbers

1. LOA (NIP for individual person) signed by the Authorized Person with a signature matching as closely as possible the one on the ID.
2. Copy of the National ID / Passport of the Authorized Person.
3. Latest Invoice.
4. If the number(s) belong to a Company, the Company Registration Certificate – "Acta Constitutiva".
5. If the number(s) belong to a Company and the Authorized Person is not mentioned on the Company Registration Certificate, an Attorney's Authorization for Representative – "Poder notarial".

### Toll-Free Numbers

1. LOA signed by the Authorized Person with a signature matching as closely as possible the one on the ID.
2. Copy of the ID / Passport of the Authorized Person.
3. Latest Invoice.
4. If the number(s) belong to a Company, the Company Registration Certificate – "Acta Constitutiva".
5. If the number(s) belong to a Company and the Authorized Person is not mentioned on the Company Registration Certificate, an Attorney's Authorization for Representative – "Poder notarial".

### Mobile Numbers

1. LOA signed by the Authorized Person with a signature matching as closely as possible the one on the ID.
2. Copy of the National ID / Passport of the Authorized Person.
3. Assignment Letter completed and signed by the Authorized Person.
4. If the number(s) belong to a Company, copy of the Notarial Deed (POA) in which the company grants General Administrative Powers to the representative who signs the LOA.

### Porting Hours and Earliest FOC Date

**For local/national/toll-free:**
- Porting hours (local time): Monday to Friday at 2:00 AM. The porting of the number will be automatically triggered from 2 AM local time and can take up to 2 hours to fully complete.
- Earliest FOC date: 2 business days from submission.

**For Mobile:**
- Porting hours (local time): Monday to Friday at 2:00 AM to 7:30 AM. When carrying out MOBILE portability to a VoIP service, there will be a loss of data service (the customer should consider the entire window as a period of service interruption). The customer won't be able to use internet data or maintain the connections they had with the previous provider once the port completes.
- Earliest FOC date: 7 business days from submission.
