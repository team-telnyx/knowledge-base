# Telnyx Mission Control Account Setup

*Part 5 of 5 — see also: [Part 1](telnyx-mission-control-account-setup--part-1.md), [Part 2](telnyx-mission-control-account-setup--part-2.md), [Part 3](telnyx-mission-control-account-setup--part-3.md), [Part 4](telnyx-mission-control-account-setup--part-4.md)*

A consolidated guide to creating and configuring a Telnyx Mission Control account, covering sign-up, verification, Freemium and Managed Accounts, AI Assistants, messaging, voice, and the in-portal AI support assistant.

## Mission Control AI Chat Support Assistant

The AI Assistant is accessible from the left-hand navigation bar in the Mission Control Portal. Click **Ask our AI Assistant** and the widget opens from the bottom right.

### How to Interact

The Assistant pulls from the Support Center, Developer Docs, and the main website to deliver relevant information. It has read-only access to portal account resources to assist with troubleshooting. If the Assistant cannot resolve a query, connect with a live agent via the widget or by clicking **Chat with us** on the left-hand navigation.

After a response, two buttons appear: **Chat to Support** (to speak with an agent) or **That helped** (to confirm the response resolved the question). To ask another question, type into the chat box and submit.

### What the AI Assistant Covers

Example topics include Hosted SMS, making calls, ordering Hosted SMS, and making calls with the Call Control Voice API. Vague questions may match a reference article without providing setup specifics; mentioning specific Telnyx products helps guide the Assistant to the right answer. Highly technical questions are routed to human support.

- **General Customer Support** — answers to common questions from Support Center documentation.
- **Developer Support** — APIs, coding questions, SDKs, code snippets (specify in the question), and relevant documentation.
- **Self-Service Support** — first-level support, with guidance on creating a support ticket or starting a chat with agents for complex inquiries.
- **24/7 Availability** — available around the clock. As a third-party provider is used, degradation may occur; this is clearly displayed. The Assistant complements human support rather than replacing it. Feedback can be provided via thumbs up and thumbs down buttons per message.

### Limitations and Complementary Use

The AI Assistant may not handle complex queries or requests requiring human intervention. Use **Chat to Support** for those cases. Conversation history is not stored in the widget — refreshing the portal or logging out clears the conversation.

### Data and Privacy

By using the AI Assistant:

- Processing of [personal data](https://telnyx.com/privacy-policy) is agreed to as necessary for providing chat services.
- Data is stored and processed by Telnyx in the United States.
- Data is transferred to OpenAI, a subprocessor in the United States, which assists in processing interactions and generating answers based on publicly accessible documentation.
- Personal data is retained as long as necessary to provide services or as required by law.
- Services involve automated decision-making, including AI processing of interactions.
- The Assistant may return incorrect or outdated information; contact support to clarify queries.

## Mission Control Resources

Four useful resources are available at the bottom left of the Mission Control account:

- **Support** — [support.telnyx.com](https://support.telnyx.com/en/) — knowledge base.
- **API Docs** — [developers.telnyx.com](https://developers.telnyx.com/) — developer documentation for APIs and SDKs.
- **Release Notes** — [telnyx.com/release-notes](https://telnyx.com/release-notes) — recent and historical release news.
- **System Status** — [status.telnyx.com](https://status.telnyx.com/) — subscribe for incident updates.

The [Telnyx resource center](https://telnyx.com/resources) provides industry topics, tutorials, and product demos.
