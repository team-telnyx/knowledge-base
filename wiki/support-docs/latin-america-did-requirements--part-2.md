# Latin America DID Requirements

*Part 2 of 2 — see also: [Part 1](latin-america-did-requirements--part-1.md)*

Use this guide to understand the documents Telnyx needs to provision phone numbers (DIDs) across Latin America, broken down by country and number type (local, mobile, toll-free, national, and shared cost). It also highlights timing expectations, templates, and country-specific notes.

## Tips to speed approval
- Ensure all scans are clear, legible, and unexpired; keep proof-of-address documents within the stated recency (often ≤3 months).
- Match address rules precisely:
  - Brazil local/toll-free: address must be in Brazil.
  - Peru local: address must match the DID area code; Peru toll-free: address in Peru.
  - Venezuela local: address must be in Venezuela.
  - Where “Worldwide” is allowed, any country is acceptable, provided the document is valid and recent.
- When LOI is required, sign/date it within the last month and ensure it reflects the correct number type and country.
- For business orders, include the authorized representative’s ID when requested and the correct, current company registration proof.
- Provide a brief service usage description when requested (e.g., Venezuela, Panama toll-free).

## Changes and support
- Requirements can change and additional documentation may be requested by regulators or local carriers. If in doubt, contact Telnyx Support from your Mission Control Portal account for the latest country-specific guidance.
