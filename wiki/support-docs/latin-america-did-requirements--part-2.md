# Latin America DID Requirements

*Part 2 of 3 — see also: [Part 1](latin-america-did-requirements--part-1.md), [Part 3](latin-america-did-requirements--part-3.md)*

This page consolidates Telnyx's documentation requirements for acquiring and porting Direct Inward Dialing (DID) numbers across Latin American countries, including Chile, Colombia, Costa Rica, Ecuador, Georgia, Honduras, Iceland, Mexico, Nicaragua, Panama, Paraguay, Peru, and Venezuela. It covers identity, business, and address verification documentation, as well as porting procedures and timelines for Mexico.

## Panama

### Local Numbers
- **Personal identity:** Name, last name; Contact phone number; Passport or ID copy.
- **Business identity:** Company name; Contact phone number; Company registration certificate.
- **Address:** Address worldwide (street, building number, postal code, city and country).

### Toll-Free Numbers
- **Business identity:** Name, last name of an authorized representative; Company name; Contact phone number; Passport or ID copy of an authorized representative; Panama registration form.
- **Address:** Address worldwide (street, building number, postal code, city and country).
- **Additional information:** Service usage description.
- Business use is required; private use is not allowed.

Once documentation is received, validation and activation take approximately 72 hours.

## Paraguay

### Local Numbers
- **Personal identity:** Name, last name; Contact phone number; Passport or ID copy.
- **Business identity:** Company name; Contact phone number; Company registration certificate.
- **Address:** Address worldwide (street, building number, postal code, city and country).

### Toll-Free Numbers
- **Personal identity:** Name, last name; Contact phone number; Passport or ID copy; Signed LOI (dated within 1 month).
- **Business identity:** Company name; Contact phone number; Company registration certificate; Signed LOI (dated within 1 month).
- **Address:** Address worldwide (street, building number, postal code, city and country).

## Peru

### Local Numbers
- **Business identity:** Name, last name of an authorized representative; Company name; Contact phone number; Passport or ID copy of an authorized representative; Peruvian company registration certificate.
- **Address:** Address matching the DID area code (street, building number, postal code, city and country); Proof of address (dated within 3 months).
- Business use is required; private use is not allowed.

### Toll-Free Numbers
- **Business identity:** Name, last name of an authorized representative; Company name; Contact phone number; Passport or ID copy of an authorized representative; Peruvian company registration certificate.
- **Address:** Address in Peru (street, building number, postal code, city and country); Proof of address (dated within 3 months).
- Business use is required; private use is not allowed.

### Shared Cost Numbers
- **Personal identity:** Name, last name; Contact phone number; Passport or ID copy.
- **Business identity:** Company name; Contact phone number; Company registration certificate.
- **Address:** Address worldwide (street, building number, postal code, city and country).

Once documentation is received, validation and activation take approximately 72 hours.

## Venezuela

### Local Numbers
- **Personal identity:** Name, last name; Contact phone number; Venezuelan Passport or ID copy.
- **Business identity:** Name, last name of an authorized representative; Contact phone number; Company name; Venezuelan company registration certificate.
- **Address:** Address in Venezuela (street, building number, postal code, city and country); Proof of address (dated within 3 months).
- **Additional information:** Service usage description.

### Toll-Free Numbers
- **Personal identity:** Name, last name; Contact phone number.
- **Business identity:** Name, last name of an authorized representative; Company name; Contact phone number; Company registration certificate.
- **Address:** Address worldwide (street, building number, postal code, city and country); Proof of address (dated within 3 months).
- **Additional information:** Service usage description.

Once documentation is received, validation and activation take approximately 72 hours.

## Mexico Number Porting

### Local / National Numbers
1. LOA (NIP for individual persons) signed by the Authorized Person with a signature matching as closely as possible the one on the ID.
2. Copy of the National ID / Passport of the Authorized Person.
3. Latest invoice.
4. If the number(s) belong to a company, the Company Registration Certificate – "Acta Constitutiva".
5. If the number(s) belong to a company and the Authorized Person is not mentioned on the Company Registration Certificate, an Attorney's Authorization for Representative – "Poder notarial".

### Toll-Free Numbers
1. LOA signed by the Authorized Person with a signature matching as closely as possible the one on the ID.
2. Copy of the ID / Passport of the Authorized Person.
3. Latest invoice.
4. If the number(s) belong to a company, the Company Registration Certificate – "Acta Constitutiva".
5. If the number(s) belong to a company and the Authorized Person is not mentioned on the Company Registration Certificate, an Attorney's Authorization for Representative – "Poder notarial".

### Mobile Numbers
1. LOA signed by the Authorized Person with a signature matching as closely as possible the one on the ID.
2. Copy of the National ID / Passport of the Authorized Person.
3. Assignment Letter completed and signed by the Authorized Person.
4. If the number(s) belong to a company, copy of the Notarial Deed (POA) in which the company grants General Administrative Powers to the representative who signs the LOA.

### Porting Hours and Earliest FOC Date

**Local / National / Toll-Free**
- Porting hours (local time): Monday to Friday at 2:00 AM. The porting is automatically triggered from 2 AM local time and can take up to 2 hours to fully complete.
- Earliest FOC date: 2 business days from submission.

**Mobile**
- Porting hours (local time): Monday to Friday from 2:00 AM to 7:30 AM. When carrying out mobile portability to a VoIP service, there will be a loss of data service; the customer should consider the entire window as a period of service interruption. The customer will not be able to use internet data or maintain the connections they had with the previous provider once the port completes.
- Earliest FOC date: 7 business days from submission.
