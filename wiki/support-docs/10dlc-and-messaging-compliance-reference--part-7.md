## Assigning a DID to a 10DLC Campaign

If you are assigning a DID to a 10DLC Campaign and it fails, please reach out to [10dlcquestions@telnyx.com](mailto:10dlcquestions@telnyx.com). Share your Telnyx account email, Campaign IDs, and any errors returned so that the team can help more quickly.
