# DID Number Requirements by Country - Saint Barthelemy Saint Barth to Rwanda

*Part 1 of 2 — see also: [Part 2](did-number-requirements-by-country-saint-barthelemy-saint-barth-to-rwanda--part-2.md)*

This page consolidates the documentation requirements for purchasing Direct Inward Dialing (DID) numbers across multiple countries served by Telnyx, including Algeria, Antigua and Barbuda, Benin, Cameroon, the Democratic Republic of the Congo, Egypt, Ghana, Israel, Jamaica, Kenya, Nigeria, Rwanda, Saint Barthélemy, Saint Martin, and Uganda. It outlines the identity, business, and address verification documents required for each number type, along with general information on Telnyx's global DID offerings and benefits.

## Algeria

### National Numbers in Algeria

- **End-user name**: The company name for business usage or the surname for private usage is needed when ordering.
- **Business use — Copy ID of an authorized representative**: Copy ID of an authorized representative.
- **Business use — Copy company registration**: A copy of the company registration must be provided.
- **Representative contact**: Name, telephone number, and email address of the legal representative.

For custom number pricing in Algeria, see the [Algeria number pricing](https://telnyx.com/pricing/numbers/dz) page.

## Antigua and Barbuda

### Toll-Free Numbers in Antigua and Barbuda

For **business identity** verification:
- Company name
- Signed LOI (dated within 1 month)

For **address** verification:
- Address worldwide (street, building number, postal code, city, and country)

**Additional information:**
- Business use required: Business use is required, private use is not allowed.

You can search for and [purchase toll-free numbers for Antigua and Barbuda](https://telnyx.com/pricing/numbers) by checking out the [numbers pricing page](https://telnyx.com/pricing/numbers).

## Benin

### Mobile Numbers in Benin

For **personal identity** verification:
- Name, last name
- Contact phone number
- Contact e-mail
- Passport or ID copy

For **business identity** verification:
- Name, last name of an authorized representative
- Company name
- Contact phone number
- Contact e-mail
- Company registration certificate

For **address** verification:
- Address worldwide (street, building number, postal code, city, and country)
- Proof of address (dated within 3 months)

Please note that once the documentation is received it will take approximately 72 hours to validate the information and activate the number for use.
