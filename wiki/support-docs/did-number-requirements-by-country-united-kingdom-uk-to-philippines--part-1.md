# DID Number Requirements by Country - United Kingdom Uk to Philippines

*Part 1 of 3 — see also: [Part 2](did-number-requirements-by-country-united-kingdom-uk-to-philippines--part-2.md), [Part 3](did-number-requirements-by-country-united-kingdom-uk-to-philippines--part-3.md)*

This page consolidates Telnyx's documentation requirements for purchasing Direct Inward Dialing (DID) numbers across multiple countries, including the United Kingdom, Ireland, Reunion, Japan, China, Vietnam, Hong Kong, Malta, Singapore, Finland, Monaco, New Zealand, and the Philippines. It outlines the personal identity, business identity, and address verification documents required for each number type, along with country-specific rules and processing timelines.

## Overview

Telnyx requires customers to provide identity, business, and address documentation when purchasing DID (Direct Inward Dialing) numbers. Requirements vary by country and by number type (local, national, mobile, toll-free, or shared cost). For most orders, proof of identity must be issued by the country in which the purchase is being made. As an exception, customers within the European Union may use a valid passport or national identity card from any EU member state, even if it differs from the EU country associated with the order; in these cases, the document will be treated as local. All identification is subject to review, and Telnyx reserves the right to request additional documentation or decline any document that cannot be verified or that does not meet applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

Once documentation is received, validation and number activation typically take approximately 72 hours.

## United Kingdom

### Local, National, Toll-Free, and Mobile Numbers

**Personal identity verification:**
- Name, last name
- Contact phone number
- Local passport or ID copy

**Business identity verification:**
- Name, last name of an authorized representative
- Company name
- Contact phone number
- Company website
- Local company registration certificate
- Business use case (for toll-free and mobile, indicate if sub-allocating)

**Address verification:**
- Address within the UK (street, building number, postal code, city, and country)
- A copy of a utility bill (less than 3 months old)

End-users must be physically present in the country when purchasing numbers from that country.

## Ireland

### Local Numbers

**Personal identity verification:**
- Name, last name
- Contact phone number
- Contact e-mail
- Local copy of ID/passport

**Business identity verification:**
- Name, last name
- Contact phone number
- Company name
- Contact e-mail
- Local business registration certificate

**Address verification:**
- Address matching DID area code (street, building number, postal code, city, and country)
- Proof of address (dated within 3 months)

Additional information: service usage description.

### National and Toll-Free Numbers

Same personal, business, and address verification requirements as local numbers, except the address only needs to be in Ireland (not necessarily matching the area code).

### Mobile Numbers

Business identity verification only:
- Name, last name
- Contact phone number
- Company name
- Contact e-mail
- Local business registration certificate

Address verification: address in Ireland and proof of address (dated within 3 months).

Business use is required for IE mobile numbers; private use is not allowed.

## Reunion

**Business identity verification:**

- Contact phone number
- Company registration certificate

**Address verification:**
- Address worldwide (street, building number, postal code, city, and country)

## Japan

### National Numbers

**Personal identity verification:**
- Name, last name
- Contact phone number
- Certificate of the person in charge (one of the following):
  1. Local driver's license with both sides of driving history certificate
  2. My Number Card (front only)
  3. Japanese passport
  4. Health insurance cards (redacted basic pension number and insurance number), National Pension Handbook, Maternal and Child Health Handbook, Seal Registration Certificate, or Basic Resident Registration Card
  5. Residence card (dated within 3 months) or Special Permanent Resident Certificate
  6. Certificate of Residence (redacted personal number)

**Business identity verification:**
- Name, last name of an authorized representative
- Contact phone number
- Company name
- Certificate of contractor (one or both):
  1. Company registered matters
  2. Certificate of company seal registration (dated within 3 months)
- Certificate of authority of the person in charge (supplemental): Power of Attorney (must include name and seal of the representative of the corporation)

**Address verification:**
- Address in Japan (street, building number, postal code, city, and country)
- Proof of address (A or B):
  - **Supplementary documents for A** (if the address of the Certificate of contractor differs from the current address or place of use): receipts of national and local taxes (stamped and dated within 3 months), Certificate of Tax Payment (stamped and dated within 3 months), receipts of social insurance premiums (stamped and dated within 3 months), or certificate of receipt of utility bills (electricity, city gas, water, and landline phone invoices acceptable)
  - **Supplementary documents for B** (if the address of the Certificate of the person in charge differs from the current address): receipt of utility bills (electricity, city gas, water, and landline phone invoices acceptable), Certificate of Tax Payment (stamped and dated within 3 months), or receipt of social insurance (stamped and dated within 3 months)

Certificates with an expiration date must be within the expiration date, and certificates without an expiration date must be within 3 months of the date of issue. A Power of Attorney is not required if the address and name of the person in charge of the application match the registered name of the representative by the documents in the Company registered matters.

## China

### Local Numbers

**Business identity verification:**
- Name, last name of an authorized representative
- Company name
- Contact phone number of an authorized representative
- Copy of the ID card of an authorized representative with the company stamp
- Picture of the authorized representative holding the ID card
- Chinese Business License with the company stamp (must contain "副本")

**Additional information:**
- Signed and stamped Customer Liability Authorization Letter (provided by NumberOps)
- Signed and stamped Network and Information Security Commitment Letter (provided by NumberOps)
- Signed and stamped Scenario & Script Declaration (write at least 3 outbound script examples; if no outbound is needed, write the inbound script — provided by NumberOps)

Business use is required for China local and toll-free numbers; private use is not allowed. A copy of the ID card of the business representative is required; any other ID (such as passport or driver's license) is not acceptable.

### Shared Cost Numbers

**Business identity verification:**
- Name, last name of an authorized representative
- Contact phone number of an authorized representative
- Passport or ID copy of an authorized representative
- A photo of the authorized representative holding their passport/ID
- Company name
- Company incorporation certificate copy
- Recently signed LOI (signed within 1 month, provided by NumberOps)

**Address verification:**
- Address worldwide (street, building number, postal code, city, and country)
- A copy of a utility bill (less than 3 months old)

Additional information: service usage description. Business use is required for China shared cost numbers; private use is not allowed.
