# European DID Requirements

*Part 1 of 3 — see also: [Part 2](european-did-requirements--part-2.md), [Part 3](european-did-requirements--part-3.md)*

This page consolidates Telnyx documentation requirements for acquiring Direct Inward Dialing (DID) numbers across multiple European countries, including Belgium, Netherlands, Bosnia & Herzegovina, Croatia, Czech Republic, Hungary, Latvia, Lithuania, Poland, Slovakia, and Slovenia, as well as Indonesia. It details the identity, business, and address verification documents required for each number type, along with processing timelines and EU-specific identity verification rules.

## Overview

Telnyx offers Direct Inward Dialing (DID) numbers across many European countries. Requirements vary by country and by number type (local, national, mobile, or toll-free). In general, customers must provide identity verification, business verification (where applicable), and address verification documents. Once documentation is received, validation and activation typically takes approximately 72 hours.

## Identity Verification: What "Local" Means

For most orders, proof of identity must be issued by the country in which the purchase is being made. As an exception, customers within the European Union may use a valid passport or national identity card from any EU member state, even if it differs from the EU country associated with the order; in these cases, the document will be treated as local.

All identification is subject to review, and Telnyx reserves the right to request additional documentation or decline any document that cannot be verified or that does not meet applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

## Belgium

### Local Numbers

**Personal identity verification:**
- Name, last name
- Contact phone number
- Local Passport or ID copy

**Business identity verification:**
- Name, last name of an authorized representative
- Company name
- Contact phone number
- Local Passport or ID copy of an authorized representative
- Local Company registration certificate
- VAT number

**Address verification:**
- Address matching the DID area code (street, building number, postal code, city and country)
- Proof of address (dated within 3 months)

End-users must be physically present in the country when purchasing numbers from that country.

### Toll-Free Numbers

**Personal identity verification:**
- Name, last name
- Contact phone number
- Passport or ID copy

**Business identity verification:**
- Name, last name
- Contact phone number
- Company name
- VAT number
- Company incorporation certificate copy

**Address verification:**
- Address in Belgium (street, building number, postal code, city and country)
- Proof of address (dated within 3 months)

End-users must be physically present in the country when purchasing numbers from that country.

### Mobile Numbers

**Personal identity verification:**
- Name, last name
- Contact phone number
- Contact e-mail
- Local Passport or ID copy

**Business identity verification:**
- Name, last name of an authorized representative
- Company name
- Contact phone number
- Contact e-mail
- VAT number
- Local Company registration certificate
- Local Passport or ID copy of an authorized representative

**Address verification:**
- Address in Belgium (street, building number, postal code, city and country)
- Proof of address (dated within 3 months)

End-users must be physically present in the country when purchasing numbers from that country.

**Additional information:**
- Service usage description
- Email address

Business use is required for Netherlands toll-free numbers; personal use is not allowed.
