# European DID Requirements

*Part 1 of 2 — see also: [Part 2](european-did-requirements--part-2.md)*

A consolidated reference for regulatory requirements when purchasing Telnyx DID numbers across European countries, including identity, business, and address verification details by number type for each jurisdiction.

## Shared Identity Verification Rules

For most orders, proof of identity must be issued by the country in which you are making your purchase. As an exception, customers within the European Union may use a valid passport or national identity card from any EU member state—even if it differs from the EU country associated with the order—and the document will be treated as local. All identification is subject to review, and Telnyx reserves the right to request additional documentation or decline any document that cannot be verified or does not meet applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

## Requirement Groups

Starting **September 16, 2024**, Requirement Groups are mandatory for ordering phone numbers in **Denmark (DK)**, **Norway (NO)**, and **Sweden (SE)**. Requirement Groups let you manage regulatory requirements by pre-filling necessary information once, allowing for reuse across multiple orders. For full details on setting up Requirement Groups, refer to the [Telnyx Requirement Groups guide](https://support.telnyx.com/en/articles/9801714-requirement-groups-for-ordering-phone-numbers).

## Activation Timeline

For countries that require documentation review, once documentation is received it takes approximately **72 hours** to validate the information and activate the number(s) for use. This applies to Iceland, Ireland, Lithuania, and the United Kingdom, among others.

## United Kingdom

### UK Local and National Numbers

**Personal identity verification:**
- Name, last name
- Contact phone number
- Local Passport or ID copy

**Business identity verification:**
- Name, last name of an authorized representative
- Company name
- Contact phone number
- Company website
- Local Company registration certificate
- Business use case

**Address verification:**
- Address within the UK (street, building number, postal code, city, and country)
- Copy of a utility bill (less than 3 months old)

**Additional details:**
- End-users must be physically present in the country when purchasing numbers

### UK Toll-Free and Mobile Numbers

Requirements are the same as Local/National with one addition: for business identity verification, you must indicate in your business use case whether you are sub-allocating the number.

## Ireland

### Ireland Local Numbers

**Personal identity verification:**
- Name, last name
- Contact phone number
- Contact e-mail
- Local Copy of ID/Passport

**Business identity verification:**
- Name, last name
- Contact phone number
- Company name
- Contact e-mail
- Local Business Registration Certificate

**Address verification:**
- Address matching DID area code (street, building number, postal code, city, and country)
- Proof of Address (dated within 3 months)

**Additional information:**
- Service usage description

### Ireland National and Toll-Free Numbers

Same as Local Numbers, except address verification requires an address anywhere in Ireland (not necessarily matching the DID area code).

### Ireland Mobile Numbers

Mobile numbers require **business identity verification only** (no personal option):
- Name, last name
- Contact phone number
- Company name
- Contact e-mail
- Local Business Registration Certificate

**Address verification:**
- Address in Ireland (street, building number, postal code, city, and country)
- Proof of Address (dated within 3 months)

**Additional information:**
- Service usage description
- Business use required; private use is not allowed
