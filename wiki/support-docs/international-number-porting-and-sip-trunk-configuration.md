# International Number Porting and SIP Trunk Configuration

A comprehensive reference for number porting requirements across the Americas and French overseas territories, including LOA templates, expected timeframes, and post-porting SIP trunk configuration for Yeastar P-Series PBX systems.

## North America

### United States

**Local numbers** require: (1) LOA — must include BTN, account number, and local address; (2) Latest invoice.

**Toll-free numbers** require: (1) LOA — must include local address and account number; (2) Latest invoice.

Download the US LOA from the [Telnyx portal](https://portal.telnyx.com/downloads/other/Telnyx-LOA.pdf).

Porting timelines in the US typically range from 1 to 7 business days. For details, see [Porting Policy Procedure](porting-policy-procedure.md), [Porting Error Messages](porting-error-messages.md), and [Porting Requirements](porting-requirements.md).

### Canada

**Local / Mobile / Toll-free numbers** require: (1) LOA — must include local address and account number; (2) Latest invoice.

Download the Canadian LOA [here](https://www.dropbox.com/s/dxk8l9x73j4ufn8/Telnyx%20LOA.pdf?dl=0).

Canadian porting timelines range from 1 to 7 business days depending on carrier and number type.

### Puerto Rico

**Local / Mobile numbers** require: (1) LOA; (2) Latest invoice.

Download the Puerto Rico LOA [here](https://www.dropbox.com/s/dxk8l9x73j4ufn8/Telnyx%20LOA.pdf?dl=0).

## Latin America

### Argentina

**Local / National numbers** require: (1) LOA — must include a contact email; (2) CUIT / CUIL / VAT (if the number belongs to a company); (3) Attorney's authorization for the representative (if the number belongs to a company); (4) Copy of ID / Passport / DNI; (5) Latest invoice with the current provider.

Contact [porting.intl@telnyx.com](mailto:porting.intl@telnyx.com) for the Argentina LOA template.

### Brazil

**Local / National / Toll-free numbers** require: (1) LOA — CPF / CNPJ is required; (2) ID / Passport copy; (3) Company registration certificate; (4) Latest invoice.

Download the Brazil LOA [here](https://www.dropbox.com/s/fmli59wv3wfky7w/BrazilLOA.pdf?dl=0).

Once a port request reaches **FOC confirmed** status, at least 48 business hours before the FOC date are required to change the date.

Porting hours: Standard time after 5 PM local.

| Number Type | Expected Timeframe |
|---|---|
| National | 5+ business days |
| Toll-Free | 5+ business days |

### Chile

**Local / Toll-free numbers** require: (1) E-RUT; (2) LOA or POA; (3) Latest invoice; (4) Power of Attorney; (5) Certificate of Incorporation; (6) RUT of the individual requesting portability; (7) Proof of Payment — national carrier invoice only, no resellers.

Contact [porting.intl@telnyx.com](mailto:porting.intl@telnyx.com) for the Chile LOA template.

Porting hours: Standard time 00:00 AM to 4 AM local.

| Number Type | Expected Timeframe |
|---|---|
| Local | 5+ business days |
| National | 5+ business days |
| Toll-Free | 5+ business days |

### Dominican Republic

**Local / National numbers** require: (1) LOA — national address required; (2) Latest invoice with the current carrier; (3) TAX ID / VAT; (4) Passport / ID copy; (5) Proof of address.

**Important:** To keep the number active during porting, contact the losing provider to retain your subscription or inbound services throughout the process. Otherwise, expect an undefined service downtime of up to 7 business days. Additionally, once the losing carrier releases the numbers, there is an undefined downtime of at least 2 hours due to local porting procedures.

Download the Dominican Republic LOA [here](https://assets.ctfassets.net/taysl255dolk/3k7kEutWHK91DYuMoG4Xa/6fdf040bb9d9aff432133f4d72997ab2/LOA_-_TELNYX_-_DOMINICAN_REPUBLIC.pdf).

### El Salvador

**Local / National numbers** require: (1) Valid LOA — local address required; (2) Copy of Identification (DUI) or Passport of the authorized person; (3) Copy of the latest invoice with a local carrier; (4) Proof of payment of the latest invoice; (5) Company registration certificate (only if numbers belong to a company); (6) Power of Attorney (only if numbers belong to a company).

Contact [porting@telnyx.com](mailto:porting@telnyx.com) for the El Salvador LOA template.

**Submission tips:** Ensure all copies are clear and legible. Digital submissions should be in high-resolution PDF or JPEG format. Double-check document completeness before submission to avoid delays.
