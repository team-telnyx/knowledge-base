# DID Number Requirements by Country

*Part 1 of 3 — see also: [Part 2](did-number-requirements-by-country--part-2.md), [Part 3](did-number-requirements-by-country--part-3.md)*

Regulatory requirements for purchasing Direct Inward Dialing (DID) phone numbers through Telnyx, organized by country and number type. Requirements vary by jurisdiction and include identity verification (personal or business), address verification, and in some cases business-use-only restrictions or local presence mandates.

## Common Policies and Timelines

Across all countries, the following general policies apply unless otherwise noted:

- **Validation time:** Once documentation is received, it takes approximately **72 hours** to validate the information and activate the number.
- **Proof of address recency:** Most countries require proof of address dated within **3 months**. Exceptions are noted per country.
- **Physical presence:** Some countries require end-users to be physically present in the country when purchasing numbers from that country.
- **Business-use-only:** Certain number types are restricted to business use; private use is not allowed.

### EU Identity Verification

For most orders, proof of identity must be issued by the country in which the purchase is made. As an exception, customers within the **European Union** may use a valid passport or national identity card from **any EU member state**, even if it differs from the EU country associated with the order; in these cases, the document will be treated as local.

All identification is subject to review, and Telnyx reserves the right to request additional documentation or decline any document that cannot be verified or does not meet applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

### Requirement Groups

For certain countries (currently Portugal and Switzerland), **Requirement Groups** are mandatory for ordering phone numbers as of September 16, 2024. Requirement Groups let you manage regulatory requirements by pre-filling necessary information once, allowing for reuse across multiple orders. See the [Requirement Groups guide](https://support.telnyx.com/en/articles/9801714-requirement-groups-for-ordering-phone-numbers) for setup details.

## Bangladesh

### National and Mobile Numbers

Business use required (private use is not allowed).

**Business identity:**
- Name, last name of an authorized representative
- Company name
- Contact phone number
- Passport or ID copy of an authorized representative
- Business Registration Certificate
- Signed LOI (dated within 1 month)

**Address:**
- Worldwide address (street, building number, postal code, city, and country)
- Proof of address matching the address in the Business Registration Certificate (dated within 3 months)

## Belgium

### Local Numbers

**Personal identity:**
- Name, last name
- Contact phone number
- Local Passport or ID copy

**Business identity:**
- Name, last name of an authorized representative
- Company name
- Contact phone number
- Local Passport or ID copy of an authorized representative
- Local Company registration certificate
- VAT number

**Address:**
- Address matching the DID area code (street, building number, postal code, city, and country)
- Proof of address (dated within 3 months)

End-users must be physically present in Belgium when purchasing.

### Toll-Free Numbers

**Personal identity:**
- Name, last name
- Contact phone number
- Local Passport or ID copy

- Name, last name of an authorized representative
- Company name
- Contact phone number

- Local Company registration certificate

**Address:**
- Address in Belgium (street, building number, postal code, city, and country)
- Proof of address (dated within 3 months)

End-users must be physically present in Belgium when purchasing.

### Mobile Numbers

**Business identity:**
