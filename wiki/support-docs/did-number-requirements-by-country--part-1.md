# DID Number Requirements by Country

*Part 1 of 2 — see also: [Part 2](did-number-requirements-by-country--part-2.md)*

This page consolidates the documentation requirements for acquiring Telnyx DID numbers across multiple countries, including Uzbekistan, Pakistan, Kuwait, Bangladesh, Oman, Kazakhstan, Japan, Iraq, Uruguay, Bulgaria, Estonia, Austria, El Salvador, and Qatar. Requirements vary by country and number type, generally covering personal identity, business identity, and address verification, with most orders requiring a signed LOI and approximately 72 hours for validation and activation.

## Overview

This page consolidates the documentation requirements for acquiring Direct Inward Dialing (DID) numbers across a range of countries served by Telnyx. Requirements vary by country and by number type (local, national, mobile, or toll-free), and typically fall into three categories: personal identity verification, business identity verification, and address verification. In most cases, a signed Letter of Intent (LOI) is required, and Telnyx provides a standard [LOI Template](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/ltcafuzd/1974079146/f3eb9460203bfedda610a97379a8/LOI+Template.docx) for that purpose. Where business use is mandated, private use is not permitted. Once documentation is received, validation and activation typically take approximately 72 hours.

## Uzbekistan

For local numbers, personal identity verification requires name, last name, contact phone number, and a signed LOI. Business identity verification requires the name and last name of an authorized representative, company name, contact phone number, and a signed LOI. Address verification requires a worldwide address (street, building number, postal code, city, and country) and proof of address dated within 3 months.

## Pakistan

Local number requirements mirror those for Uzbekistan. Personal identity verification requires name, last name, contact phone number, and a signed LOI. Business identity verification requires the name and last name of an authorized representative, company name, contact phone number, and a signed LOI. Address verification requires a worldwide address and proof of address dated within 3 months.

## Kazakhstan

Local number requirements are identical to those for Uzbekistan and Pakistan. Personal identity verification requires name, last name, contact phone number, and a signed LOI. Business identity verification requires the name and last name of an authorized representative, company name, contact phone number, and a signed LOI. Address verification requires a worldwide address and proof of address dated within 3 months.

## Kuwait

Only business identity verification is supported for local numbers. Required documents include the name and last name of an authorized representative, contact phone number, company name, company website, Kuwaiti ID copy of an authorized representative, local company registration certificate, traffic forecast, a signed LOI dated within 1 month, and a signed Service Agreement Form. Every order is subject to local provider approval, and business use is required. Validation and activation take approximately 72 hours.

## Bangladesh

National and mobile numbers require business identity verification only. Required documents include the name and last name of an authorized representative, company name, contact phone number, passport or ID copy of an authorized representative, business registration certificate, and a signed LOI dated within 1 month. Address verification requires a worldwide address and proof of address matching the address in the business registration certificate, dated within 3 months. Business use is required, and validation takes approximately 72 hours.

## Oman

For local numbers, business identity verification requires the name and last name of an authorized representative, company name, contact phone number, local business registration certificate, and a signed LOI. Address verification requires an address in Oman and proof of address dated within 3 months.

For toll-free numbers, business identity verification requires the name and last name of an authorized representative, contact phone number, company name, passport or ID copy, establishment card, latest trade license, and a signed LOI. Address verification requires a national address. Additional information required includes a service usage description and a traffic forecast.

## Japan

For national numbers, personal identity verification requires name, last name, contact phone number, and a certificate of the person in charge. Acceptable certificates include a local driver's license with both sides of the driving history certificate, My Number Card (front only), Japanese passport, health insurance cards (with redacted basic pension and insurance numbers), residence card dated within 3 months, or a certificate of residence with redacted personal number.

Business identity verification requires the name and last name of an authorized representative, contact phone number, company name, a certificate of contractor (company registered matters or certificate of company seal registration dated within 3 months), and a certificate of authority of the person in charge (such as a power of attorney that includes the name and seal of the corporate representative).

Address verification requires an address in Japan and proof of address. If the certificate of contractor or person in charge shows a different address, supplementary documents are required, such as stamped receipts of national and local taxes dated within 3 months, certificate of tax payment, receipts of social insurance premiums, or utility bill receipts (electricity, city gas, water, or landline phone). Certificates with an expiration date must be within that date; certificates without an expiration date must be within 3 months of issue. A power of attorney is not required if the address and name of the person in charge match the registered representative in the company registered matters.

## Iraq

Local numbers require business identity verification only. Required documents include the name and last name of an authorized representative, contact phone number, company name, local company registration document, and a signed Service Acceptance Form provided by NumberOps. Address verification requires an address in Iraq and proof of address dated within 3 months. Business use is required.

## Uruguay

Toll-free numbers require personal identity verification (name, last name, contact phone number, and a signed LOI dated within 1 month), business identity verification (name and last name of an authorized representative, company name, contact phone number, and a signed LOI dated within 1 month), and address verification (worldwide address and proof of address dated within 3 months). Validation and activation take approximately 72 hours.

## Bulgaria

For local numbers, personal identity verification requires name, last name, contact phone number, and a passport or ID copy. Business identity verification requires the name and last name of an authorized representative, company name, contact phone number, and company registration certificate. Address verification requires an address matching the DID area code and proof of address dated within 3 months.

For toll-free numbers, personal identity verification requires name, last name, and contact phone number. Business identity verification requires the name and last name of an authorized representative, company name, and contact phone number. Address verification requires a worldwide address. Validation and activation take approximately 72 hours.

## Estonia

For local numbers, personal identity verification requires name, last name, contact phone number, and a copy of ID or passport. Business identity verification requires the name and last name of the legal representative, contact phone number, company name, copy of ID or passport of the legal representative, and copy of company registration. Address verification requires an address in Estonia and a copy of a utility bill no older than 3 months.

For toll-free numbers, personal identity verification requires name, last name, contact phone number, and a passport or ID copy. Business identity verification requires company name, contact phone number, and company registration certificate. Address verification requires a worldwide address. Validation and activation take approximately 72 hours.
