# DID Number Requirements by Country

*Part 1 of 2 — see also: [Part 2](did-number-requirements-by-country--part-2.md)*

This page consolidates Telnyx's documentation requirements for purchasing Direct Inward Dialing (DID) numbers across multiple countries, covering identity verification, address proof, business registration, and supporting documents such as Letters of Intent (LOI) and Service Acceptance Forms. It also outlines country-specific rules such as business-only use, local presence requirements, and standard processing times.

## Overview

Telnyx requires customers to provide specific documentation when purchasing DID (Direct Inward Dialing) numbers in different countries. Requirements typically fall into three categories: **personal identity verification**, **business identity verification**, and **address verification**. Some countries also require additional documents such as a Letter of Intent (LOI), Service Acceptance Form, traffic forecast, or service usage description. Once documentation is received, validation and activation generally takes approximately 72 hours, though some jurisdictions require local provider approval which may extend this timeline.

## Algeria

National numbers in Algeria require the following:

- **End-user name** — the company name for business usage or the surname for private usage.
- **Business use: Copy ID of an authorized representative.**
- **Business use: Copy of company registration.**
- **Name, telephone number, and email address of the legal representative.**

For custom number pricing, see the [Algeria number pricing](https://telnyx.com/pricing/numbers/dz) page.

## Bulgaria

### Local Numbers

- **Personal identity:** Name, last name; contact phone number; passport or ID copy.
- **Business identity:** Name and last name of an authorized representative; company name; contact phone number; company registration certificate.
- **Address:** Address matching the DID area code (street, building number, postal code, city, and country); proof of address dated within 3 months.

### Toll-Free Numbers

- **Personal identity:** Name, last name; contact phone number.
- **Business identity:** Name and last name of an authorized representative; company name; contact phone number.
- **Address:** Address worldwide (street, building number, postal code, city, and country).

Validation and activation take approximately 72 hours after documentation is received.

## Egypt

Local numbers in Egypt require the following:

- **Business identity:** Name and last name of an authorized representative; contact phone number; company name; local company registration document; signed Service Acceptance Form (provided by NumberOps).
- **Address:** Address worldwide (street, building number, postal code, city, and country); proof of address dated within 3 months.

Business use is required; private use is not allowed.

## Iraq

Local numbers in Iraq require the following:

- **Business identity:** Name and last name of an authorized representative; contact phone number; company name; local company registration document; signed Service Acceptance Form (provided by NumberOps).
- **Address:** Address in Iraq (street, building number, postal code, city, and country); proof of address dated within 3 months.

Business use is required; private use is not allowed.

## Kazakhstan

### Local Numbers

- **Personal identity:** Name, last name; contact phone number; LOI (provided by Telnyx — [LOI Template.docx](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/ltcafuzd/1974079146/f3eb9460203bfedda610a97379a8/LOI+Template.docx)).
- **Business identity:** Name and last name of an authorized representative; company name; contact phone number; LOI (provided by Telnyx).
- **Address:** Worldwide address (street, building number, postal code, city and country); proof of address dated within 3 months.

## Kuwait

Local numbers in Kuwait require the following:

- **Business identity:** Name and last name of an authorized representative; contact phone number; company name; company website; Kuwaiti ID copy of an authorized representative; local company registration certificate; traffic forecast; signed LOI dated within 1 month (provided by Telnyx — [LOI Template.docx](https://telnyx-48416ce2297b.intercom-attachments-7.com/i/o/ltcafuzd/1859021631/2598a28fce5b9e7143855f466ff7/LOI+Template+.docx)); signed Service Agreement Form (provided by Telnyx — [Service Acceptance Doc.xlsx](https://telnyx-48416ce2297b.intercom-attachments-2.com/i/o/ltcafuzd/1859022086/2008b1c4683d416d3fc4d35d23ef/Service+Acceptance+Doc.xlsx)).

Every order is subject to local provider approval. Business use is required; private use is not allowed. Validation and activation take approximately 72 hours after documentation is received.

## Malaysia

### Local Numbers

- **Business identity:** Name and last name of an authorized representative; contact phone number; company name; passport or ID copy of an authorized representative.
- **Address:** Address in Malaysia (street, building number, postal code, city and country); proof of address dated within 3 months.

Business use is required; private use is not allowed.

### National Numbers

- **Business identity:** Name and last name of an authorized representative; contact phone number; company name; passport or ID copy of an authorized representative; Malaysian company registration certificate; signed LOI dated within 1 month.
- **Address:** Address in Malaysia (street, building number, postal code, city and country); proof of address dated within 3 months.

Business use is required; private use is not allowed.

### Toll-Free Numbers

- **Personal identity:** Name, last name; contact phone number.
- **Business identity:** Name and last name of an authorized representative; contact phone number; company name.
- **Address:** Address in Malaysia (street, building number, postal code, city and country); proof of address dated within 3 months.

### Mobile Numbers

- **Business identity:** Name and last name of an authorized representative; contact phone number; passport or ID copy of an authorized representative; company name; company registration certificate; signed LOI dated within 1 month.
- **Address:** Worldwide address (street, building number, postal code, city and country); proof of address dated within 3 months.

Business use is required; private use is not allowed. Validation and activation take approximately 72 hours after documentation is received.

## Oman

- **Address:** Address in Oman (street, building number, postal code, city and country); proof of address dated within 3 months.

### Toll-Free Numbers

- **Business identity:** Name and last name of an authorized representative; contact phone number; company name; passport or ID copy; establishment card; latest trade license; LOI (provided by Telnyx).
- **Address:** National address (street, building number, postal code, city and country).

Additional information required: service usage description and traffic forecast.
