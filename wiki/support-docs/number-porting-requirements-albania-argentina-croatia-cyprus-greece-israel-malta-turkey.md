# Number Porting Requirements: Albania, Argentina, Croatia, Cyprus, Greece, Israel, Malta, Turkey

A consolidated checklist of country-specific documents, LOA templates, and special notes needed to port local, national, toll-free, and (where applicable) mobile numbers in Albania, Argentina, Croatia, Cyprus, Greece, Israel, Malta, and Turkey with Telnyx.

## Albania
- Number types: Local, National
- Required documents:
  - Letter of Authorization (LoA)
  - TAX ID / VAT or Company Registration Number
  - Latest invoice with the current provider
  - Copy of the authorized person’s ID or passport
- LOA template: Request via porting@telnyx.com

## Argentina
- Number types: Local, National
- Required documents:
  - LOA (must include a contact email)
  - CUIT / CUIL / VAT (if the number belongs to a company)
  - Attorney’s authorization for the representative (if the number belongs to a company)
  - Copy of ID / Passport / DNI
  - Latest invoice with the current provider
- LOA template: Request via porting.intl@telnyx.com

## Croatia
- Number types: Local, National; Toll-free
- Required documents (Local/National):
  - LOA
  - ID or passport copy
  - Company registration certificate
  - Latest invoice
- Required documents (Toll-free):
  - LOA
  - ID or passport copy and/or company registration certificate
  - Latest invoice
- LOA template: Download at https://www.dropbox.com/s/1kd2hs03tq8o0bg/Croatia_Porting_Form.pdf?dl=0

## Cyprus
- Number types: Local; National and Toll-free
- Required documents (Local):
  - LOA with a local address
  - Latest invoice with the current carrier
  - Company registration (if the number belongs to a company)
  - ID or passport number of the legal representative
  - Proof of address
- Required documents (National and Toll-free):
  - LOA with a national address
  - Latest invoice with the current carrier
  - Company registration (if the number belongs to a company)
  - ID or passport number of the legal representative
  - Proof of address
- LOA template: Download at https://assets.ctfassets.net/taysl255dolk/1Cq2IXxuZiFRu03aA3LXB6/8006b5ee40f97ac95f050be7013c8bcb/CYPRUS_LOA-_Telnyx.pdf

## Greece
- Number types: Local
- Required documents:
  - LOA
  - Greek TAX/VAT ID or company registration number
  - ID or passport copy
  - Company registration certificate
  - Latest invoice with the current carrier (numbers must be listed on the invoice)
- LOA template: Download at https://assets.ctfassets.net/taysl255dolk/6YYtHkPiDoOfhR8Vp3QxUz/3f7b77cf78ecdebe280c6c0226ac12ea/LoA_-_Telnyx_-_INTL.pdf

## Israel
- Number types: Local, National, Mobile
- Required documents:
  - LOA
  - VAT/TAX ID
  - Latest invoice
- Notes (Mobile under a business):
  - The invoice must be from the underlying carrier
  - An official proof of ownership signed by the underlying carrier is required
- LOA template: Download at https://assets.ctfassets.net/taysl255dolk/6YYtHkPiDoOfhR8Vp3QxUz/3f7b77cf78ecdebe280c6c0226ac12ea/LoA_-_Telnyx_-_INTL.pdf

## Malta
- Number types: Local, National
- Required documents:
  - LOA
  - VAT/TAX ID
  - Latest invoice with the current carrier
  - Company registration certificate
  - Copy of the authorized person’s ID or passport
- LOA template: Download at https://assets.ctfassets.net/taysl255dolk/4eNcFSEqPgAGcs7H5JsOZ3/b26904cf82b11c3d13f925fbf81972ae/LoA_-_Telnyx_-_NZ-malta.pdf

## Turkey
- Number types: Local, National
- Required documents:
  - Most recent invoice with the carrier
  - LOA including business name, address, and local company registration number
  - Copy of the authorized person’s ID
  - Copy of official TAX ID issued by the Turkish government
  - Copy of corporate signatories filed with the Turkish government
  - Description of the business activities
- LOA template: Download at https://assets.ctfassets.net/taysl255dolk/6YYtHkPiDoOfhR8Vp3QxUz/3f7b77cf78ecdebe280c6c0226ac12ea/LoA_-_Telnyx_-_INTL.pdf
