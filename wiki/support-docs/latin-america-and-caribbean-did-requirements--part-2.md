# Latin America and Caribbean DID Requirements

*Part 2 of 2 — see also: [Part 1](latin-america-and-caribbean-did-requirements--part-1.md)*

Requirements for purchasing Telnyx DID numbers across Latin American and Caribbean countries, including identity, business, and address verification documents needed for each number type.

## Panama

**Local Numbers**

- **Personal identity:** Name, last name; contact phone number; passport or ID copy
- **Business identity:** Company name; contact phone number; company registration certificate
- **Address:** Worldwide (street, building number, postal code, city, country)

**Toll-Free Numbers** (business use required; private use not allowed)

- **Business identity:** Name, last name of an authorized representative; company name; contact phone number; passport or ID copy of an authorized representative; Panama registration form
- **Address:** Worldwide (street, building number, postal code, city, country)
- **Additional information:** Service usage description

Activation takes approximately 72 hours after documentation is received.

## Paraguay

**Local Numbers**

- **Personal identity:** Name, last name; contact phone number; passport or ID copy
- **Business identity:** Company name; contact phone number; company registration certificate
- **Address:** Worldwide (street, building number, postal code, city, country)

**Toll-Free Numbers**

- **Personal identity:** Name, last name; contact phone number; passport or ID copy; signed LOI (dated within 1 month)
- **Business identity:** Company name; contact phone number; company registration certificate; signed LOI (dated within 1 month)
- **Address:** Worldwide (street, building number, postal code, city, country)

## Peru

**Local Numbers** (business use required; private use not allowed)

- **Business identity:** Name, last name of an authorized representative; company name; contact phone number; passport or ID copy of an authorized representative; Peruvian company registration certificate
- **Address:** Matching the DID area code (street, building number, postal code, city, country); proof of address (dated within 3 months)

**Toll-Free Numbers** (business use required; private use not allowed)

- **Business identity:** Name, last name of an authorized representative; company name; contact phone number; passport or ID copy of an authorized representative; Peruvian company registration certificate
- **Address:** In Peru (street, building number, postal code, city, country); proof of address (dated within 3 months)

**Shared Cost Numbers**

- **Personal identity:** Name, last name; contact phone number; passport or ID copy
- **Business identity:** Company name; contact phone number; company registration certificate
- **Address:** Worldwide (street, building number, postal code, city, country)

Activation takes approximately 72 hours after documentation is received.

## Saint Barthélemy

**Local Numbers**

- **Personal identity:** Name, last name; contact phone number; passport or ID copy
- **Business identity:** Company name; contact phone number; company registration certificate
- **Address:** Worldwide (street, building number, postal code, city, country)

## Saint Martin

**Local Numbers**

- **Personal identity:** Name, last name; contact phone number; passport or ID copy
- **Business identity:** Company name; contact phone number; company registration certificate
- **Address:** Worldwide (street, building number, postal code, city, country)

## Venezuela

**Local Numbers**

- **Personal identity:** Name, last name; contact phone number; Venezuelan passport or ID copy
- **Business identity:** Name, last name of an authorized representative; contact phone number; company name; Venezuelan company registration certificate
- **Address:** In Venezuela (street, building number, postal code, city, country); proof of address (dated within 3 months)
- **Additional information:** Service usage description

**Toll-Free Numbers**

- **Personal identity:** Name, last name; contact phone number
- **Business identity:** Name, last name of an authorized representative; company name; contact phone number; company registration certificate
- **Address:** Worldwide (street, building number, postal code, city, country); proof of address (dated within 3 months)
- **Additional information:** Service usage description

Activation takes approximately 72 hours after documentation is received.

## Common Requirements and Patterns

Several patterns emerge across the region:

- **Worldwide address accepted:** French Guiana, Guadeloupe, Martinique, Saint Barthélemy, Saint Martin, Bolivia, Dominican Republic, Argentina, Paraguay (local), Panama (local), and Peru (shared cost) all accept any worldwide address without requiring proof of local presence.
- **Local address required with proof:** Honduras, Guatemala, Venezuela (local), Nicaragua, and Peru (local and toll-free) require an address within the country along with proof of address dated within 3 months.
- **LOI required:** Honduras, Ecuador, Guatemala, Nicaragua, Paraguay (toll-free), Anguilla, and Antigua and Barbuda require a Letter of Intent. Some LOIs are provided by Telnyx (using the [LOI Template](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/ltcafuzd/1974079146/f3eb9460203bfedda610a97379a8/LOI+Template.docx?expires=1781298000&signature=f3e96dc97f88226513f9b0b7a1fe24cc282441040a634ffec298f3906f76d644&req=dSkgEsl5lIBbX%2FMW3Hu4gYkDxCMSx%2Fz4JWMNiC1aPckl9X1pUcxnLXrVCS62%0AkA%3D%3D%0A)), while others must be signed and dated within 1 month.
- **Business use only:** Costa Rica, Panama (toll-free), Peru (local and toll-free), Nicaragua, Anguilla, and Antigua and Barbuda restrict numbers to business use only; private use is not allowed.
- **Service usage description required:** Venezuela and Panama (toll-free) require a description of how the service will be used.
- **72-hour activation:** Guatemala, Ecuador, Venezuela, Panama, Peru, and Nicaragua note approximately 72 hours for validation and activation after documentation is received.
