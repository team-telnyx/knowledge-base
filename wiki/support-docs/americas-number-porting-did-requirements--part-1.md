# Americas Number Porting & DID Requirements

*Part 1 of 2 — see also: [Part 2](americas-number-porting-did-requirements--part-2.md)*

This page consolidates Telnyx's number porting and DID acquisition requirements for the Americas region, covering Brazil, Canada, Chile, Panama, Peru, Puerto Rico, the United States, the Dominican Republic, Malta, Anguilla, and Argentina. Each country section lists the required documentation, porting hours, expected timeframes, and links to downloadable LOA templates, with additional guidance on US/CA toll-free porting and SMS migration best practices.

## Overview

This page consolidates Telnyx's number porting and DID acquisition requirements for the Americas region, covering Brazil, Canada, Chile, Panama, Peru, Puerto Rico, the United States, the Dominican Republic, Malta, Anguilla, and Argentina. Each country has its own documentation requirements, porting hours, and expected timeframes. Use the country-specific sections below to identify the documents you need to gather before submitting a porting request or purchasing a new DID.

## Brazil

### Porting Requirements

For local, national, and toll-free numbers, Brazil requires:

- LOA (CPF / CNPJ required)
- ID / Passport copy
- Company Registration certificate
- Latest Invoice

Download the Brazil LOA from the [Brazil LOA PDF](https://www.dropbox.com/s/fmli59wv3wfky7w/BrazilLOA.pdf?dl=0).

Once a port request reaches *FOC confirmed* status, Telnyx requires at least 48 business hours prior to the FOC date to change the date. Porting hours are standard time after 5 PM local. Expected timeframes are 5+ business days for both national and toll-free numbers, though actual times may vary based on the losing carrier.

### DID Acquisition Requirements

For local/mobile numbers, Telnyx requires personal or business identity verification plus a Brazilian address with proof of address dated within 3 months. Personal identity requires name, last name, contact phone number, CPF number, and a passport or ID copy. Business identity requires name and last name of an authorized representative, company name, contact phone number, and Cartão CNPJ (Comprovante de Inscrição e Situação Cadastral).

For toll-free/national (3003/4004) numbers, personal identity requires name, last name, contact phone number, contact email, CPF number, and a passport or ID copy. Business identity requires name and last name of an authorized representative, company name, contact phone number, contact email, CNPJ number, Power of Attorney, passport or ID copy of an authorized representative, and Company Registration Certificate. Address verification requires a Brazilian address (street, building number, postal code, city, and country).

Brazil orders may take up to 3 business days to review and approve. See [Brazil DID Requirements](brazil-did-requirements.md) for the full acquisition guide.

## Canada

For local, mobile, and toll-free numbers, Canada requires:

- LOA (local address and account number required)
- Latest Invoice

Download the Canadian LOA from the [Telnyx LOA PDF](https://www.dropbox.com/s/dxk8l9x73j4ufn8/Telnyx%20LOA.pdf?dl=0). The porting process typically takes 1 to 7 business days. See [Canada Number Porting](canada-number-porting.md) for additional context.

## Chile

### Porting Requirements

For local and toll-free numbers, Chile requires:

- E-RUT
- LOA or POA
- Latest Invoice
- Chile Power of Attorney
- Chile Certificate of Incorporation
- RUT of the individual requesting portability
- Proof of Payment (national carrier invoice, no resellers)

Reach out to [porting.intl@telnyx.com](mailto:porting.intl@telnyx.com) for the template document. Porting hours are standard time 00:00 AM to 4 AM local. Expected timeframes are 5+ business days for local, national, and toll-free numbers, though actual times may vary based on the losing carrier.

### DID Acquisition Requirements

For local numbers, personal identity requires name, last name, and a passport or ID copy. Business identity requires name and last name of an authorized representative, company name, and a passport or ID copy of an authorized representative. Address verification requires a worldwide address (street, building number, postal code, city, and country).

For toll-free numbers, business use is required (private use is not allowed). Business identity requires name and last name of an authorized representative, company name, and a passport or ID copy of an authorized representative. Address verification requires a worldwide address.

For national and shared cost numbers, personal identity requires name, last name, contact phone number, email address, and a passport or ID copy. Business identity requires company name, contact phone number, email address, TAX/RUT number, and company registration certificate. Address verification requires a worldwide address.

See [Chile DID Requirements](chile-did-requirements.md) for the full acquisition guide.

## Peru

For local and national numbers, Peru requires:

- LOA
- Attorney's authorization for the representative (if the number belongs to a company)
- ID / Passport copy
- Company Registration certificate
- Latest Invoice

Download the Peruvian porting form from the [Peruvian Porting Form PDF](https://www.dropbox.com/s/0j3d2r4e5idm9o7/Peruvian_Porting_Form.pdf?dl=0). Porting hours are standard time 00:00 AM to 4 AM local. Expected timeframes are 2–7 business days for local numbers, though actual times may vary based on the losing carrier.

## Puerto Rico

For local and mobile numbers, Puerto Rico requires:

- LOA
- Latest Invoice

Download the Puerto Rico LOA from the [Telnyx LOA PDF](https://www.dropbox.com/s/dxk8l9x73j4ufn8/Telnyx%20LOA.pdf?dl=0). The porting process typically takes 1 to 7 business days.

## United States

For local numbers, the US requires:

- LOA (BTN, account number, and local address required)
- Latest Invoice

For toll-free numbers, the US requires:

- LOA (local address and account number required)
- Latest Invoice

Download the US LOA from the [Telnyx LOA PDF](https://portal.telnyx.com/downloads/other/Telnyx-LOA.pdf). The porting process typically takes 1 to 7 business days.

## Dominican Republic

### Porting Requirements

For local and national numbers, the Dominican Republic requires:

- LOA (national address required)
- Latest Invoice with the current carrier
- TAX ID / VAT
- Passport / ID copy
- Proof of address

Download the Dominican Republic LOA from the [Telnyx Dominican Republic LOA PDF](https://assets.ctfassets.net/taysl255dolk/3k7kEutWHK91DYuMoG4Xa/6fdf040bb9d9aff432133f4d72997ab2/LOA_-_TELNYX_-_DOMINICAN_REPUBLIC.pdf).

To keep numbers active during porting, contact the losing provider to retain the subscription or keep inbound services throughout the process. Otherwise, undefined service downtime of up to 7 business days is to be expected. Once the porting process has started and the numbers are released by the losing carrier, there is an undefined (at least 2 hours) service downtime that may be experienced due to local porting procedures.

### DID Acquisition Requirements

For local and toll-free numbers, personal identity requires name, last name, contact phone number, and a passport or ID copy. Business identity requires company name, contact phone number, and company registration certificate. Address verification requires a worldwide address (street, building number, postal code, city, and country).

See [Dominican Republic DID Requirements](dominican-republic-did-requirements.md) for the full acquisition guide.
