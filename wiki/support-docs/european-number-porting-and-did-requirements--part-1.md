## Overview

This page consolidates Telnyx's documentation for number porting and DID acquisition across a range of European countries, including Denmark, Finland, Ireland, the United Kingdom, Sweden, Georgia, Latvia, Russia, Ukraine, Poland, Lithuania, and Estonia. It covers the documentation required to port existing numbers into Telnyx, the identity and address verification needed to purchase new DIDs, and country-specific operational notes such as porting hours, SLAs, and rejection causes.

## Denmark Number Porting

For local, national, and toll-free numbers in Denmark, the following are required:

1. LOA — a local address is mandatory, and the signature must be a wet signature.
2. Local TAX ID / VAT.
3. Latest invoice with the current provider.

Download the Denmark LoA from the [Telnyx international LoA template](https://assets.ctfassets.net/taysl255dolk/6YYtHkPiDoOfhR8Vp3QxUz/3f7b77cf78ecdebe280c6c0226ac12ea/LoA_-_Telnyx_-_INTL.pdf).

## Finland Number Porting

For local and national numbers in Finland, the following are required:

1. LOA.
2. VAT/TAX ID or social security number.
3. Latest invoice.

For toll-free numbers in Finland, the following are required:

1. LOA.
2. VAT/TAX ID or social security number.
3. Attorney's authorization for representative (in case the number belongs to a company).
4. Latest invoice.

Download the Finland POA from the [Finland Telnyx POA template](https://assets.ctfassets.net/taysl255dolk/BjAY6YlvDP8UHiRtMAtSP/009510c32ea7832407ab6c525f69478a/FINLAND-TELNYX.pdf).

The most common rejections for portability are:

- Wrong end-user on document.
- Signature not readable or valid on the LOA.

## Ireland Number Porting

For local, national, and toll-free numbers in Ireland, the following are required:

1. LOA.
2. UAN / Account Number.
3. Latest invoice.

Download the Ireland LOA from the [Ireland Geo Porting Form](https://www.dropbox.com/s/9f8nesm8873og7d/IRELANDGEOPORTINGFORM-edit.pdf?dl=0).

A UAN is eight digits in length and can be found on the end-user's invoice. If it is not mentioned on the invoice, the end-user must contact their current provider and request it.

Customers porting away from EIR or Open EIR must ensure with the current provider prior to the port that numbers don't have special services, as these services may be cancelled as a result of the port to Telnyx.

Porting hours in Ireland are from 10:00 to 15:30 local time. Telnyx strongly advises against requesting a port to occur after 15:00. If an issue occurs with the port, there is a limited amount of time for escalation within business hours, which may result in the DIDs being down until the following business day.
