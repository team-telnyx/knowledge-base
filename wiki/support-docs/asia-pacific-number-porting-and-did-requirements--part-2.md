# Asia-Pacific Number Porting and DID Requirements

*Part 2 of 4 — see also: [Part 1](asia-pacific-number-porting-and-did-requirements--part-1.md), [Part 3](asia-pacific-number-porting-and-did-requirements--part-3.md), [Part 4](asia-pacific-number-porting-and-did-requirements--part-4.md)*

Consolidated reference for Telnyx number porting and DID acquisition across APAC and Finland, covering portable number types, expected timeframes, port categories, document requirements, LOA downloads, and country-specific rules for Australia, New Zealand, Singapore, China, Hong Kong, Vietnam, Malaysia, Thailand, Indonesia, the Philippines, and Finland.

## Singapore Number Porting

Port Singapore local (Level 6) numbers to Telnyx. National, toll-free, and mobile numbers are not available for porting.

### Portable Number Types

| Number Type | Portable | Port Type |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Local Numbers (Level 6) | Yes | DEL or DID/DDI |
| National Numbers | Not available | — |
| Toll-Free Numbers | Not available | — |
| Mobile Numbers | Not available | — |

### Expected Timeframes

| Port Type | Expected Timeframe |
| --- | --- |
| DEL (Simple) | 6–7 business days |
| DID/DDI (Complex) | 8–18 business days |

These are expected timeframes between carriers. Delays may occur if the losing carrier is slow to respond.

### Port Types

- **DEL (Direct Exchange Line) — Simple Port:** Single-line PSTN services, standard telephone services, numbers without complex features.
- **DID/DDI (Direct Inward Dialling) — Complex Port:** Number ranges or blocks, numbers with complex services, PABX or private system numbers, numbers that allow direct dialling without operator intervention.

If porting a number block, you must port the full range or have your current carrier disassociate the specific numbers you wish to port.

### Document Requirements

**Company Accounts**

- Country-specific LOA signed by the authorized person
- Business Registration Certificate or Certificate of Incorporation
- Work Permit or Employment Pass (for non-citizen applicants)
- Latest invoice from current carrier

**Personal Accounts**

- Country-specific LOA signed by the end-user
- NRIC or Passport
- Work Permit or Employment Pass (for non-citizen applicants)
- Latest invoice from current carrier

### LOA

📄 [Download the Singapore Letter of Authorization](https://images.telnyx.com/Telnyx-Singapore-LOA.pdf)

Before completing the LOA:

- Save in PDF format (non-editable)
- Fill in all designated fields correctly
- Ensure information matches your current carrier's records exactly
- Signature must be from the authorized person (company) or end-user (personal)

### Changing Your FOC Date

Once a porting date has been confirmed with the losing carrier, date changes require at least 72 business hours (SGT) notice before the scheduled cutover. Contact Telnyx porting support to request a change.

### Porting Process

1. Gather all required documents based on the account type.
2. Submit port request through [Mission Control](https://portal.telnyx.com).
3. Select the correct port type (DEL or DID/DDI).
4. Upload LOA and supporting documents.
5. Telnyx submits a request to the losing carrier.
6. Receive FOC confirmation with activation date.
7. Numbers activate on the scheduled FOC date.

## China DID Requirements

Business use is required for China local, toll-free, and shared cost numbers; private use is not allowed. A copy of the ID card of the business representative is required — passports or driver's licenses are not acceptable.

### Local Numbers in China

**Business identity verification:**

- Name and last name of an authorized representative
- Company name
- Contact phone number of an authorized representative
- Copy of the ID card of an authorized representative with the company stamp
- Picture of the authorized representative holding the ID card
- Chinese Business License with the company stamp (must contain "副本")

**Additional information:**

- Signed and stamped Customer Liability Authorization Letter (provided by NumberOps)
- Signed and stamped Network and Information Security Commitment Letter (provided by NumberOps)
- Signed and stamped Scenario & Script Declaration — at least 3 outbound script examples, or inbound script if no outbound is needed (provided by NumberOps)

### Shared Cost Numbers in China

**Business identity verification:**

- Name and last name of an authorized representative
- Contact phone number of an authorized representative
- Passport or ID copy of an authorized representative
- A photo of the authorized representative holding their passport/ID
- Company name
- Company incorporation certificate copy
- Recently signed LOI (signed within 1 month, provided by NumberOps)

**Address verification:**

- Address worldwide (street, building number, postal code, city, country)
- A copy of a utility bill (less than 3 months old)

**Additional information:**

- Service usage description

## Hong Kong DID Requirements

Business use is required for Hong Kong national and toll-free numbers; private use is not allowed. Once documentation is received, validation and activation take approximately 72 hours.

### National Numbers in Hong Kong

**Business identity verification:**

- Name and last name of an authorized representative
- Company name
- Contact phone number
- Company registration certificate
- Hong Kong Tripartite Agreement

**Address verification:**

- Address in Hong Kong (street, building number, postal code, city, country)
- Proof of address (dated within 3 months)

### Toll-Free Numbers in Hong Kong

**Business identity verification:**

- Name and last name of an authorized representative
- Company name
- Contact phone number

**Address verification:**

- Address worldwide (street, building number, postal code, city, country)
- Proof of address (dated within 3 months)

**Additional information:**

- Service usage description

## Vietnam DID Requirements

Once documentation is received, validation and activation take approximately 72 hours. Calling card usage is not permitted on Vietnam toll-free numbers.

### Local Numbers in Vietnam

**Personal identity verification:**

- Name, last name
- Contact phone number
- Passport or ID copy
- Utility bill, not older than 3 months

**Business identity verification:**

- Name and last name of an authorized representative
- Contact phone number
- Contact e-mail
- Company name
- Passport or ID copy of an authorized representative
- Company registration certificate
- Signed LOI (dated within 1 month)
- Utility bill, not older than 3 months

**Address verification:**

- Address worldwide (street, building number, postal code, city, country)

### Toll-Free Numbers in Vietnam

**Personal identity verification:**

- Name, last name
- Contact e-mail

**Business identity verification:**

- Company name
- Contact e-mail

**Address verification:**

- Address worldwide (street, building number, postal code, city, country)
