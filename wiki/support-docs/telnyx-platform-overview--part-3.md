# Telnyx Platform Overview

*Part 3 of 3 — see also: [Part 1](telnyx-platform-overview--part-1.md), [Part 2](telnyx-platform-overview--part-2.md)*

Telnyx is a Communications Platform as a Service (CPaaS) provider founded in 2009 and based in Austin, Texas, offering voice, messaging, networking, AI inference, storage, and workflow automation through a globally distributed private IP network and robust APIs.

## Portal and Support Resources

### Mission Control Portal

The Telnyx Mission Control Portal ([portal.telnyx.com](https://portal.telnyx.com)) is the central hub for managing all services, including numbers, SIP connections, messaging profiles, API keys, outbound profiles, and billing.

### AI Chat Support Assistant

An AI Assistant is available 24/7 in the portal's left navigation bar under "Ask our AI Assistant." It draws from the Support Center, Developer Docs, and main website, and has read-only access to your portal account resources for troubleshooting. If the assistant cannot resolve your issue, you can connect with a live agent from the widget. Conversation history is not retained between sessions. By using the AI Assistant, you consent to data processing by Telnyx and OpenAI (subprocessor) in the United States.

### Feature Requests

To submit a feature request, click the question mark icon in the portal's top navigation bar, select "Feature Request," then click "Submit Idea." Enter the feature request, importance level, and email address. You can also view planned, in-progress, and under-consideration features. Support cases tagged as feature requests are also reviewed by product managers.

### Bug Reports

Report bugs to [support@telnyx.com](mailto:support@telnyx.com) with detailed descriptions, screenshots, and videos. For portal issues, include browser console logs (front-end) or network logs/HAR files (back-end). For mission-critical service-affecting bugs, open a chat for immediate investigation.

**Security vulnerabilities** must be reported to [security@telnyx.com](mailto:security@telnyx.com) with the subject `[Vulnerability Report] <Brief Description>`. Reports must include a description, reproduction steps, proof of concept, and demonstrated impact. Telnyx operates a Vulnerability Disclosure Program with scope covering `*.telnyx.com` properties; out-of-scope items include third-party services, physical security, social engineering, and DoS testing. Telnyx may offer rewards for qualifying reports and provides safe harbor for good-faith researchers.

## Compliance

### Accessible Canada Act (ACA)

In compliance with the CRTC's accessibility requirements under the ACA, Telnyx enables customers to provide feedback regarding accessibility. Send feedback to [acafeedback@telnyx.com](mailto:acafeedback@telnyx.com) with the subject "ACA: Feedback" including your name, phone number, email, relation to Telnyx, and description.

### Google Verified Calls (Sunset)

Google Verified Calls, which displayed business name, logo, and reason for calling on Android devices, has been sunset by Google and is no longer available through Telnyx. See [Making Calls with Branded Calling](making-calls-with-branded-calling.md) for current alternatives.

### Call Center Support

Telnyx actively serves call centers as an ideal customer profile, offering HD Voice, scalable SIP trunking, and high-quality voice services optimized for high-volume operations.

## Contracts and Pricing

Telnyx operates on a pay-as-you-go model with no contracts required. Elastic SIP Trunking has no minimum commitments, and granular billing options allow efficient usage management. Channel Billing provides an alternative flat-fee model for unlimited inbound minutes. International termination pricing is available in the portal.
