# Asia-Pacific DID Requirements

*Part 1 of 3 — see also: [Part 2](asia-pacific-did-requirements--part-2.md), [Part 3](asia-pacific-did-requirements--part-3.md)*

Requirements for purchasing Telnyx DID numbers across Asia-Pacific countries, covering identity, business, and address verification documents needed for local, national, toll-free, mobile, and shared cost number types.

## China

### Local Numbers

Business use is required for China Local numbers; private use is not allowed.

**Business identity verification:**
- Name, last name of an authorized representative
- Company name
- Contact phone number of an authorized representative
- Copy of the ID card of an authorized representative with the company stamp
- Picture of the authorized representative holding the ID card
- Chinese Business License with the company stamp (must contain "副本")

**Additional documents (provided by NumberOps):**
- Signed and stamped Customer Liability Authorization Letter
- Signed and stamped Network and Information Security Commitment Letter
- Signed and stamped Scenario & Script Declaration (write at least 3 outbound script examples; if no outbound is needed, write the inbound script)

Only a copy of the ID card is acceptable for the business representative — passports or driver's licenses are not accepted.

### Shared Cost Numbers

Business use is required; private use is not allowed.

**Business identity verification:**
- Name, last name of an authorized representative
- Contact phone number of an authorized representative
- Passport or ID copy of an authorized representative
- A photo of the authorized representative holding their passport/ID
- Company name
- Company incorporation certificate copy
- Recently signed LOI (signed within 1 month; provided by NumberOps)

**Address verification:**
- Address worldwide (street, building number, postal code, city, and country)
- A copy of a utility bill (less than 3 months old)

A service usage description is also required.

## Japan

### National Numbers

**Personal identity verification:**
- Name, last name
- Contact phone number
- Certificate of the person in charge (one of the following):
  1. Local driver's license with both sides of driving history certificate
  2. My Number Card (front only)
  3. Japanese Passport
  4. Health insurance cards (redacted basic pension number and insurance number)
  5. National Pension Handbook
  6. Maternal and Child Health Handbook
  7. Seal Registration Certificate
  8. Basic Resident Registration Card
  9. Residence card (dated within 3 months)
  10. Special Permanent Resident Certificate
  11. Certificate of Residence (redacted personal number)

**Business identity verification:**
- Name, last name of an authorized representative
- Contact phone number
- Company name
- Certificate of contractor (one or both of the following):
  1. Company registered matters
  2. Certificate of company seal registration (dated within 3 months)
- Certificate of authority of the person in charge (supplemental):
  - Power of Attorney (must include name and seal of the representative of the corporation)

**Address verification:**
- Address in Japan (street, building number, postal code, city, and country)
- Proof of address — supplemental documents are required if the address on the Certificate of contractor or Certificate of the person in charge differs from the current address or place of use, or if the current address is not listed

**Supplementary documents for address (Category A):**
1. Receipts of national and local taxes (stamped and dated within 3 months)
2. Certificate of Tax Payment (stamped and dated within 3 months)
3. Receipts of social insurance premiums (stamped and dated within 3 months)
4. Certificate of receipt of utility bills (electricity, city gas, water, and landline phone invoices acceptable)

**Supplementary documents for address (Category B):**
1. Receipt of utility bills (electricity, city gas, water, and landline phone invoices acceptable)
2. Certificate of Tax Payment (stamped and dated within 3 months)
3. Receipt of social insurance (stamped and dated within 3 months)

Certificates with an expiration date must be within the expiration date; certificates without an expiration date must be within 3 months of the date of issue. Power of Attorney is not required if the address and name of the person in charge match the registered name of the representative shown in the Company registered matters.
