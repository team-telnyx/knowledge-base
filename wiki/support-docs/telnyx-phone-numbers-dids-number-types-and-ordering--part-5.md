# Telnyx Phone Numbers: DIDs, Number Types, and Ordering

*Part 5 of 5 — see also: [Part 1](telnyx-phone-numbers-dids-number-types-and-ordering--part-1.md), [Part 2](telnyx-phone-numbers-dids-number-types-and-ordering--part-2.md), [Part 3](telnyx-phone-numbers-dids-number-types-and-ordering--part-3.md), [Part 4](telnyx-phone-numbers-dids-number-types-and-ordering--part-4.md)*

A consolidated reference covering what DIDs are, the global number types Telnyx offers, how to search and buy numbers, how to request numbers that are unavailable, country-specific documentation requirements for international numbers, and best practices for working with the Telnyx numbering team.

## International Number Porting - Required Documents

To check the portability status of international numbers, submit your port request via your Telnyx Portal account along with the documentation required for the country. Upon submission, you will be required to provide an LOA and invoice. Once portability has been confirmed across coverage and carrier, the porting team will get back to you with the next steps required to port the number(s) along with any other documentation requirements needed.

## Finding GB Numbers on the Telnyx Portal

GB local, national, and toll-free numbers are not SMS supported; only mobile numbers support the SMS feature. Use the following search queries to find your desired numbers on the Telnyx portal:

- **GB National numbers:** Search using the National number type.
- **GB Toll-free numbers:** Search using the Toll-free number type.
- **SMS capable GB Mobile numbers:** Search using the Mobile number type.
- **GB Local numbers:** Put the region or city name in the region search to find numbers for a specific city.

If you are still facing issues or have questions regarding finding GB numbers on the portal, reach out to the numbering team by emailing [numbering@telnyx.com](mailto:numbering@telnyx.com) or by opening a numbering request ticket on the portal.

## Numbering Team Best Practices

### Before Contacting the Numbering Team

1. Search for the number you want in the portal at [https://portal.telnyx.com/#/voice/my-numbers/buy](https://portal.telnyx.com/#/voice/my-numbers/buy).
2. If the number is available, you may purchase it. If the number you searched for is unavailable, select the **Request Number** button.
3. When your order is placed, there are two possibilities:
   - **Scenario 1:** The phone numbers have no ordering requirements, so the order should complete momentarily. No further action is needed.
   - **Scenario 2:** The phone numbers have ordering requirements. Your order will transition into "pending" until you provide that additional information.
4. Find your "pending" order on the [Number Orders page](https://portal.telnyx.com/#/voice/orders) and fulfill any regulatory requirements. Upload your ordering requirements within 2 days to avoid auto-cancellation of your order.
5. Once the ordering requirements are fulfilled, your request will be sent directly to the numbering team for requirement review and approval. If any further information is needed, the numbering team will contact you directly. Lead times on number orders and availability checks vary by country and number type, so your request will be processed as soon as possible.
6. You will receive an update either by a comment on the order or to your email once the transaction is approved or denied.

### When Can I Contact the Numbering Team?

From Monday to Friday, Telnyx's numbering team can be reached between 9am - 5pm CT. If you contact them outside of these hours, your message will be attended to on the next business day.

### How to Contact Telnyx's Numbering Team

- Click the chat icon on the bottom right of the screen when signed into the Portal. This will open a chat window where you can talk to a member of the team.
- Send an email to [numbering@telnyx.com](mailto:numbering@telnyx.com). One of the team members will get back to you as soon as possible.

### Requesting New Numbers

If you are unable to locate the desired number on portal.telnyx.com, refer to the [Requesting Numbers](requesting-numbers.md) guide to request a number.

### Need an Update on an Existing Order?

If you have an existing order and need an update, click the chat icon on the bottom right of the screen when signed into the Portal. A member of the numbering team will be able to give you an update on your order status.

### What Support Method is Best?

- **To request a new number:** Use the numbering request form on the portal.
- **If you need an update on an existing order:** Use the chat function on the Portal.
- **If you have an urgent issue and need to expedite a ticket:** Contact the numbering team by opening a new chat or by sending an email to [numbering@telnyx.com](mailto:numbering@telnyx.com) during work hours.
- **Looking for document requirements for a specific country:** Search by country on support.telnyx.com.
