# SMS Guidelines: Permissive Destinations

Consolidated Telnyx SMS guidelines for 15 destinations (American Samoa, Estonia, French Polynesia, Germany, Kiribati, Latvia, Liechtenstein, Lithuania, Luxembourg, Samoa, Slovakia, Slovenia, Solomon Islands, Switzerland, and Tuvalu). All listed destinations support alphanumeric Sender IDs without registration and impose no content restrictions, though senders must still comply with the Telnyx Acceptable Use Policy for Messaging.

## Overview

This page consolidates Telnyx SMS guidelines for a set of destinations that share a common, permissive policy profile. For every destination listed below, the following rules apply:

- **Alphanumeric Sender IDs** are supported and will be maintained. No registration is required.
- There are **no restrictions** with regards to content sent to these destinations.
- All senders must continue to comply with the Telnyx [Acceptable Use Policy for Messaging](acceptable-use-policy-for-messaging.md).

## Compliance

Regardless of the permissive destination rules above, all SMS traffic must comply with the Telnyx [Acceptable Use Policy for Messaging](acceptable-use-policy-for-messaging.md). Review that policy before sending to ensure your use case is permitted.
