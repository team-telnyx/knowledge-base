*Part 3 of 3 — see also: [Part 1](telnyx-sms-guide--part-1.md), [Part 2](telnyx-sms-guide--part-2.md)*

It is organized into topical collections covering 10DLC and toll-free compliance, getting started with messaging, third-party integrations, miscellaneous features, and country-specific SMS guidelines.
