# Middle East and Central Asia DID Requirements

Requirements for purchasing DID numbers across Middle Eastern and Central Asian countries, including identity, business, and address verification documents needed for each number type.

## Algeria

### National Numbers

**Business identity verification:**
- Name, telephone number, and email address of legal representative
- Copy of ID of an authorized representative
- Copy of company registration

**Registration:** The company name (for business usage) or surname (for private usage) is needed when ordering.

## Egypt

### Local Numbers

**Business identity verification:**
- Name, last name of an authorized representative
- Contact phone number
- Company name
- Local Company Registration Document
- Signed Service Acceptance Form (provided by NumberOps)

**Address verification:**
- Address worldwide (street, building number, postal code, city, and country)
- Proof of address (dated within 3 months)

Business use is required; private use is not allowed.

## Iraq

### Local Numbers

**Business identity verification:**
- Name, last name of an authorized representative
- Contact phone number
- Company name
- Local Company Registration Document
- Signed Service Acceptance Form (provided by NumberOps)

**Address verification:**
- Address in Iraq (street, building number, postal code, city, and country)
- Proof of address (dated within 3 months)

Business use is required; private use is not allowed.

## Israel

### Mobile Numbers

**Personal identity verification:**
- Name, last name
- Contact email

**Business identity verification:**
- Company name
- Contact email

**Address verification:**
- Address worldwide (street, building number, postal code, city, and country)

### Local and National Numbers

**Personal identity verification:**
- Name, last name
- Contact phone number

**Business identity verification:**
- Name, last name of the legal representative
- Contact phone number
- Company name
- Business Registration Certificate
- Business Use Case

**Address verification:**
- Worldwide address (street, building number, postal code, city, and country)
- Proof of address (dated within 3 months)

Local and National numbers may be ordered using an address from any country. However, emergency services are only supported for addresses located within Israel.

### Toll-Free Numbers

**Personal identity verification:**
- Name, last name
- Contact phone number
- Passport or ID copy

**Business identity verification:**
- Company name
- Contact phone number
- Company registration certificate

**Address verification:**
- Address worldwide (street, building number, postal code, city, and country)

## Kazakhstan

### Local Numbers

**Personal identity verification:**
- Name, last name
- Contact phone number
- LOI (provided by Telnyx)

**Business identity verification:**
- Name, last name of an authorized representative
- Company name
- Contact phone number
- LOI (provided by Telnyx)

**Address verification:**
- Worldwide address (street, building number, postal code, city, and country)
- Proof of address (dated within 3 months)

## Kuwait

### Local Numbers

**Business identity verification:**
- Name, last name of an authorized representative
- Contact phone number
- Company name
- Company website
- Kuwaiti ID copy of an authorized representative
- Local Company registration certificate
- Traffic forecast
- Signed LOI (dated within 1 month, provided by Telnyx)
- Signed Service Agreement Form (provided by Telnyx)

Every order is subject to the local provider's approval. Business use is required; private use is not allowed. Once documentation is received, it takes approximately 72 hours to validate the information and activate the number.

## Oman

### Local Numbers

**Business identity verification:**
- Name, last name of an authorized representative
- Company name
- Contact phone number
- Local Business Registration Certificate
- LOI (provided by Telnyx)

**Address verification:**
- Address in Oman (street, building number, postal code, city, and country)
- Proof of address (dated within 3 months)

### Toll-Free Numbers

**Business identity verification:**
- Name, last name of an authorized representative
- Contact phone number
- Company name
- Passport or ID copy
- Establishment card
- Latest trade license
- LOI (provided by Telnyx)

**Address verification:**
- National address (street, building number, postal code, city, and country)

**Additional information for Toll-Free:** Service usage description and traffic forecast are required.

## Qatar

### National Numbers

**Business identity verification:**
- Name, last name of an authorized representative
- Contact phone number
- Company name
- Local Company Registration Document
- Signed Service Acceptance Form (provided by NumberOps)

**Address verification:**
- Address worldwide (street, building number, postal code, city, and country)
- Proof of address (dated within 3 months)

Business use is required; private use is not allowed.

## Turkey

### Local and National Numbers

**Business identity verification:**
- Name, last name of an authorized representative
- Company name
- Contact phone number
- Circular of Signatures (İmza Sirküleri)
- Tax Registration Certificate (Vergi Levhası)
- Chamber of Commerce Registration Document (Faaliyet Belgesi)
- Trade Registry Gazette (Ticari Sicil Gazetesi)
- Passport or ID copy of an authorized representative (Yetkili Kimlik Fotokopisi)

**Address verification:**
- Address in Turkey (street, building number, postal code, city, and country)
- Address confirmation from Turkish government portal (Adres Beyani), available at [turkiye.gov.tr](https://www.turkiye.gov.tr/nvi-yerlesim-yeri-ve-diger-adres-belgesi-sorgulama)

## Common Patterns

Several requirements appear across multiple countries in this region:

- **LOI (Letter of Intent):** Required for Kuwait, Oman, and Kazakhstan; template provided by Telnyx.
- **Service Acceptance Form:** Required for Kuwait, Iraq, Qatar, and Egypt; provided by NumberOps or Telnyx.
- **Local company registration:** Required for Kuwait, Oman, Iraq, Qatar, Egypt, and Algeria.
- **Proof of address (within 3 months):** Required for Oman, Kazakhstan, Iraq, Qatar, Egypt, and Israel (local/national).
- **Business use only:** Enforced for Kuwait, Iraq, Qatar, and Egypt.
- **Local address requirement:** Turkey and Oman require an in-country address; Iraq requires an address in Iraq. Kazakhstan, Qatar, Egypt, and Israel accept a worldwide address.
- **Passport or ID copy:** Required for Turkey, Oman (toll-free), and Israel (toll-free personal).

For general information about DID numbers, see [DID Number Requirements](did-number-requirements.md).
