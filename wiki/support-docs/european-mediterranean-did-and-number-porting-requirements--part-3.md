# European & Mediterranean DID and Number Porting Requirements

*Part 3 of 3 — see also: [Part 1](european-mediterranean-did-and-number-porting-requirements--part-1.md), [Part 2](european-mediterranean-did-and-number-porting-requirements--part-2.md)*

This page consolidates Telnyx's documentation, identity verification, and porting requirements for acquiring and porting DID numbers across the United Kingdom, Ireland, Turkey, Croatia, Germany, Greece, Italy, Cyprus, Switzerland, Malta, and Albania. It covers the documents required for new number purchases (personal/business identity and address verification) and for porting existing numbers (LOA, invoices, IDs, and country-specific forms), along with EU rules on what counts as a "local" identity document.

## Turkey Number Porting

**Local and National Numbers** require:
1. The most recent invoice with the carrier
2. LoA with name and address of the business, local company registration number
3. Copy of the ID of the authorized person
4. Copy of official TAX ID issued by the Turkish government
5. Copy of corporate signatories filed with the Turkish government
6. A description of the activities of the business

The Turkey LOA can be downloaded from the [Telnyx INTL LoA](https://assets.ctfassets.net/taysl255dolk/6YYtHkPiDoOfhR8Vp3QxUz/3f7b77cf78ecdebe280c6c0226ac12ea/LoA_-_Telnyx_-_INTL.pdf).

## Benefits of Telnyx DIDs

Across all covered countries, Telnyx DIDs offer the following benefits:
- Potential customers are 400% more likely to answer calls from local numbers (per [GTM Now](https://gtmnow.com/5-ways-to-get-your-prospects-to-pick-up-their-phones/))
- Improved call routing where individual DIDs can be assigned to specific business functions
- Scalability as the business grows
- Enhanced privacy for individuals
- Advanced call tracking and analytical capabilities to measure volume, duration, and more

Telnyx owns and operates its own IPs, so calls bypass congestion on the public internet, making them more secure and reliable. Global DID numbers start at $1 and can be assigned or reassigned to any phone or device, with unlimited DIDs creatable on a single SIP trunk.
