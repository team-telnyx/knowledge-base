# DID Requirements: French Guiana, Guadeloupe, Martinique, Saint Barthélemy, Saint Martin, and Honduras

This page consolidates Telnyx DID document requirements for acquiring local numbers in French Guiana, Guadeloupe, Martinique, Saint Barthélemy, Saint Martin, and Honduras, detailing personal, business, and address verification. French overseas territories accept worldwide addresses; Honduras requires a Telnyx-provided LOI and recent proof of local address.

## French Overseas Territories: French Guiana, Guadeloupe, Martinique, Saint Barthélemy, Saint Martin
Number type: Local numbers

For personal identity verification:
- Name and last name
- Contact phone number
- Passport or national ID copy

For business identity verification:
- Company name
- Contact phone number
- Company registration certificate

For address verification:
- Address worldwide (street, building number, postal code, city, and country)

## Honduras
Number type: Local numbers

For personal identity verification:
- Name and last name
- Contact phone number
- LOI (provided by Telnyx)

For business identity verification:
- Authorized representative’s name and last name
- Company name
- Contact phone number
- LOI (provided by Telnyx)

For address verification:
- Address in Honduras (street, building number, postal code, city, and country)
- Proof of address dated within 3 months

## Notes on documents and address rules
- “Address worldwide” means no local address is required for the listed French overseas territories; a valid address from any country is acceptable.
- In Honduras, a Telnyx-provided LOI is mandatory for both personal and business use cases, and proof of address must be recent (within 3 months).
