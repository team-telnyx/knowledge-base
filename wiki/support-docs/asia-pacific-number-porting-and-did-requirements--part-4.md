Consolidated reference for Telnyx number porting and DID acquisition across APAC and Finland, covering portable number types, expected timeframes, port categories, document requirements, LOA downloads, and country-specific rules for Australia, New Zealand, Singapore, China, Hong Kong, Vietnam, Malaysia, Thailand, Indonesia, the Philippines, and Finland.

## General DID Notes

Telnyx [Global DID Numbers](https://telnyx.com/products/phone-numbers) are virtual and can be assigned or reassigned to any phone or device. Businesses can create unlimited DIDs on a single [SIP trunk](https://telnyx.com/products/sip-trunks), with few limitations. Telnyx owns and operates its own IPs, so calls bypass congestion on the public internet. See [Number Feature Availability by Country](https://telnyx.com/global-coverage) for full coverage.

## Need Help?

For porting questions, contact [porting@telnyx.com](mailto:porting@telnyx.com). See also [Porting Policy & Procedure](porting-policy-procedure.md) and [How to Fill Out an LOA](how-to-fill-out-an-loa.md).
