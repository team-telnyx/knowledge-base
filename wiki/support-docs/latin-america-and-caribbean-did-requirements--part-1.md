# Latin America and Caribbean DID Requirements

*Part 1 of 2 — see also: [Part 2](latin-america-and-caribbean-did-requirements--part-2.md)*

Requirements for purchasing Telnyx DID numbers across Latin American and Caribbean countries, including identity, business, and address verification documents needed for each number type.

This page consolidates Telnyx DID number requirements for countries in Latin America and the Caribbean. Requirements vary by country and number type (local, toll-free, mobile, national, or shared cost). Some countries accept worldwide addresses while others require a local address with proof. Several countries mandate business use only and require a Letter of Intent (LOI). Activation typically takes approximately 72 hours after documentation is received where noted. For countries not listed here, see [DID Requirements by Country](did-requirements-by-country.md) and the [Telnyx Number Pricing](https://telnyx.com/pricing/numbers) page for availability and custom pricing details.

## Argentina

**Local and Shared Cost Numbers**

- **Personal identity:** Name, last name; contact phone number; passport or ID copy
- **Business identity:** Company name; contact phone number; company registration certificate
- **Address:** Worldwide (street, building number, postal code, city, country)

**Toll-Free Numbers**

- **Personal identity:** Name, last name
- **Business identity:** Company name
- **Address:** Worldwide (street, building number, postal code, city, country)

## Anguilla

**Toll-Free Numbers** (business use required; private use not allowed)

- **Business identity:** Company name; signed LOI (dated within 1 month)
- **Address:** Worldwide (street, building number, postal code, city, country)

## Antigua and Barbuda

**Toll-Free Numbers** (business use required; private use not allowed)

- **Business identity:** Company name; signed LOI (dated within 1 month)
- **Address:** Worldwide (street, building number, postal code, city, country)

## Bolivia

**Toll-Free and National Numbers**

- **Personal identity:** Name, last name; contact phone number; passport or ID copy
- **Business identity:** Company name; contact phone number; company registration certificate
- **Address:** Worldwide (street, building number, postal code, city, country)

## Costa Rica

**National Numbers** (business use required; private use not allowed)

- **Business identity:** Name, last name; contact phone number; company name; company incorporation certificate copy
- **Address:** Worldwide (street, building number, postal code, city, country)

**Toll-Free Numbers** (business use required; private use not allowed)

- **Business identity:** Name, last name of an authorized representative; company name; contact phone number; passport or ID copy of an authorized representative; power of attorney; extract from the Commercial Register (Chamber of Commerce)
- **Address:** Worldwide (street, building number, postal code, city, country); proof of address (dated within 3 months)

## Dominican Republic

**Local and Toll-Free Numbers**

- **Personal identity:** Name, last name; contact phone number; passport or ID copy
- **Business identity:** Company name; contact phone number; company registration certificate
- **Address:** Worldwide (street, building number, postal code, city, country)

## Ecuador

**Toll-Free Numbers**

- **Personal identity:** Name, last name; contact phone number; signed LOI (dated within 1 month)
- **Business identity:** Name, last name of an authorized representative; company name; contact phone number; signed LOI (dated within 1 month)

## El Salvador

**Local Numbers**

- **Additional requirement:** Traffic forecast (a forecast of expected traffic must be provided)

No standard identity or address verification documents are specified beyond the traffic forecast.

**Local Numbers** (business only)

- **Business identity:** Name, last name of an authorized representative; company name; contact phone number; ID/passport of authorized representative; local company registration certificate; NIT (Tax ID); LOI (provided by NumberOps)

Activation takes approximately 72 hours after documentation is received.
