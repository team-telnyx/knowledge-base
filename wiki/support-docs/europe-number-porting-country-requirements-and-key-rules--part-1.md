# Europe Number Porting: Country Requirements and Key Rules

*Part 1 of 2 — see also: [Part 2](europe-number-porting-country-requirements-and-key-rules--part-2.md)*

This guide consolidates Telnyx’s public requirements and special rules for porting Local, National, Toll-Free, and select mobile numbers across key European countries, with links to the correct Letters of Authorization (LoA) and important timing, address, and documentation caveats.

## Using this guide
- Always gather a signed LoA, a recent invoice from the current provider, and identity/company documentation before submitting.
- Geographic numbers typically require a local address that matches the number’s area code; national or non-geographic numbers usually require a national address.
- Some markets require special codes (e.g., RIO in France, CVP in Portugal, UAN in Ireland, PAC for UK mobile). Obtain these from the current provider in advance.
- Where number ranges or extensions exist, verify whether partial porting is allowed and whether the entire range must move.
- Observe each country’s porting windows, cancellation rules, and any contract constraints with the losing carrier to avoid delays or outages.

## Europe-wide essentials
Commonly requested items (varies by country and number type):
- LoA with correct address (local vs. national), signed as required (wet signature in some markets).
- Latest invoice from current provider (sometimes only if the current provider is a reseller).
- Company registration certificate (business) or ID/passport (personal).
- Proof of address (often dated within the last 3 months) and local tax/VAT ID where applicable.
- Special portability codes where mandated (e.g., RIO, CVP, UAN, PAC).

## Austria
- Local/National: LoA (local address required), account number, ID/passport copy, company registration certificate, latest invoice, proof of address.
- Toll-Free: LoA, current underlying carrier’s name, porting history (if previously ported), account number, latest invoice.
- LoA: https://assets.ctfassets.net/taysl255dolk/5bvRirKjh7u8TWy0LkYiSc/c3d9da4269c2158f0e67496068a895e4/Rufnummernmitnahme_KDB_TELNYX.pdf

## Belgium
- Local (geographic): LoA (local address required; number’s area code must match the address), VAT/TAX ID, latest invoice, proof of local address.
- National/Toll-Free: LoA (national address required), VAT/TAX ID, latest invoice, proof of local address.
- LoA: https://assets.ctfassets.net/taysl255dolk/6YYtHkPiDoOfhR8Vp3QxUz/3f7b77cf78ecdebe280c6c0226ac12ea/LoA_-_Telnyx_-_INTL.pdf

## France
- Local/National/Toll-Free: LoA (national address mandatory), SIRET (business; 14 digits), RIO (12 characters), latest invoice, proof of address (dated within 3 months).
- LoA: https://assets.ctfassets.net/taysl255dolk/4pv4cqjnsUfEGbyexn1Ycy/536af1c7e37734422a9556ea38582ceb/Telnyx_LoA_-_France.pdf
- RIO retrieval: Dial 3179 or 0 805 92 3179; if special numbers are blocked, request RIO directly from the current carrier.
- Port types (all take at least 8 business days): Isolated (single number), Full (BTN plus all associated), Partial (some associated only), Complex (BTN without associated; specify new BTN).
- Time slots: Morning trigger 10:00–13:00; Afternoon trigger 14:00–17:00 (local).
- Validate info before submission; correcting details typically requires waiting for a rejection (can take up to 3 business days).
- Common rejections: Address or business name mismatch, invalid SIRET, pending port with another carrier.

## Germany
- Number length: Major cities (Berlin/Frankfurt/Hamburg/Munich) standard length 10 digits (excluding leading 0); other municipalities 11 digits (excluding leading 0).
- Extensions/blocks: Extra digits often indicate 10/100 extension blocks. Only the base (standard) number ports, not the extended block. You must be assigned the full 10/100 block; otherwise the port of the standard number may be blocked.
- Local/National requirements: Porting form (local address from the same city as the number), latest invoice, proof of address, ID/passport/company registration.
  - Form: https://www.dropbox.com/s/4d6jx7izmgaiun4/german-porting-document-and-address%20confirmation.pdf?dl=0
- Toll-Free requirements: Porting form (local German address), latest invoice (if current provider is a reseller), number allocation document (Zuteilungsbescheid), ID/passport/company registration.
  - Form: https://assets.ctfassets.net/taysl255dolk/1tsn59edoZYrYtwIzaKYd0/f4563a1894c76e07a588eb32e364999e/TFN_LOA.pdf
- Porting window: 06:00–08:00 local; trigger at 06:00; carrier has 2 hours to release. No off-window scheduling. Date changes possible but must be at least 5 business days out; fees apply after submission/confirmation.
- Important form notes: Max 10 numbers per form or 1 range; separate forms per city and per donor carrier; confirm number details and any active connections.
- Contracts: Numbers can’t port before contract end with current operator. Notify the operator of intent to port and any line cancellations; confirmed port date typically aligns with the contract end if not prearranged. Early termination fees may apply.
- Connections: After numbers port, any remaining connections with the old provider are terminated—plan accordingly.
- Porting out of Telnyx: Gaining provider emails porting.intl@telnyx.com with the standard German form; Telnyx creates a Port Out in the end-user Portal for authorization within 4 business days, then approves.

## Ireland
- Local/National/Toll-Free: LoA, UAN/account number (8 digits), latest invoice.
  - LoA: https://www.dropbox.com/s/9f8nesm8873og7d/IRELANDGEOPORTINGFORM-edit.pdf?dl=0
- UAN is on the end-user’s invoice; if absent, request from current provider.
- If porting from EIR/OEIR (Open Eir), verify that special services are removed or will be cancelled as part of the port.
- Porting hours: 10:00–15:30 local. Avoid ports after 15:00 due to limited escalation time if issues occur.

## Italy
- Local/National: LoA (local address required), local TAX/VAT or company registration number, migration code, latest invoice, passport/ID of authorized person, fiscal code (authorized person; 16 alphanumeric), proof of local address. A local Italian address and legal presence (valid fiscal code) are mandatory.
- Toll-Free: LoA (local address required), local TAX/VAT or company registration, latest invoice, passport/ID, proof of local address.
- LoA: https://assets.ctfassets.net/taysl255dolk/45LOLnr1ZO8PfWfHpAID9z/cf24bfaa49a0fc3d04900d5c0f9dfac5/LoA_TELNYX_Italy.pdf
- After losing-carrier confirmation: Cancellation/postponement deadline is 5 working days before port date for Standard ports; 7 working days for Mixed Operator/Complex ports. After these windows, the port proceeds with no fallback. Temporary outage may occur during porting.

## Luxembourg
- Local/National/Toll-Free: LoA, VAT/TAX ID, local contact phone number, latest invoice.
- LoA: https://assets.ctfassets.net/taysl255dolk/6YYtHkPiDoOfhR8Vp3QxUz/3f7b77cf78ecdebe280c6c0226ac12ea/LoA_-_Telnyx_-_INTL.pdf

## Netherlands
- Local (including smart mobile +31 97): LoA (user’s local address from the same city), latest invoice, company registration (business) or ID (personal), proof of local address.
- National: LoA (national address), latest invoice, company registration/ID, proof of local address, number allocation doc (OPTA/ACM Beschikking) required only for +31 88 numbers.
- Toll-Free: LoA, latest invoice, ID/passport, company registration certificate.
- LoA: https://assets.ctfassets.net/taysl255dolk/6YYtHkPiDoOfhR8Vp3QxUz/3f7b77cf78ecdebe280c6c0226ac12ea/LoA_-_Telnyx_-_INTL.pdf
- Partial porting: Many Dutch carriers will not split blocks. If your number is part of a full range, either port the full range or attempt a partial port at risk: numbers left behind may experience downtime of indeterminate length on FOC.
- Porting out of Telnyx: Gaining provider submits a request; Telnyx creates a Port Out in the end-user Portal. Authorization required within 2 business days; Telnyx then approves.
