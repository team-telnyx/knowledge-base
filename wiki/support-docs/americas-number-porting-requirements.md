# Americas Number Porting Requirements

A consolidated guide to Telnyx number porting across selected countries in the Americas, covering required documents by number type, porting windows, typical timeframes, LOA templates, and country-specific notes to help you prepare clean, complete submissions and plan cutovers effectively.

## How to use this guide
- Requirements vary by country and number type (local, national, toll-free, mobile). Submit clear, legible documents; incomplete or unclear copies can delay ports.
- Typical processing times can range from 1–7+ business days and may vary based on the losing carrier.
- Porting windows are generally outside business hours and differ by country; plan for potential downtime, especially during the cutover window.

## Brazil
- Number types: Local, National, Toll-Free
- Requirements:
  - LOA (CPF/CNPJ required)
  - ID or Passport copy
  - Company Registration certificate
  - Latest Invoice
- LOA template: https://www.dropbox.com/s/fmli59wv3wfky7w/BrazilLOA.pdf?dl=0
- Porting window: After 5:00 PM local time
- Typical timeframes: National 5+ business days; Toll-Free 5+ business days (actuals vary by losing carrier)
- Notes: Once a port request is FOC-confirmed, at least 48 business hours prior to the FOC date are required to change the date.

## Canada
- Number types: Local, Mobile, Toll-Free
- Requirements:
  - LOA (local address and account number required)
  - Latest Invoice
- LOA template: https://www.dropbox.com/s/dxk8l9x73j4ufn8/Telnyx%20LOA.pdf?dl=0
- Typical timeframes: Often 1–7 business days depending on carrier and order specifics
- Helpful resources:

  - Porting policy & procedure: https://support.telnyx.com/en/articles/1130630-porting-policy-procedure

## Chile
- Number types: Local, National, Toll-Free
- Requirements:
  - E-RUT
  - LOA or POA
  - Latest Invoice
  - Power of Attorney (Chile-specific)
  - Certificate of Incorporation (Chile-specific)
  - RUT of the individual requesting portability
  - Proof of Payment (national carrier invoice; no resellers)
- LOA template: request via porting.intl@telnyx.com
- Porting window: 00:00–4:00 AM local time
- Typical timeframes: 5+ business days for Local, National, and Toll-Free (actuals vary by losing carrier)

## Dominican Republic
- Number types: Local, National
- Requirements:
  - LOA (national address required)
  - Latest invoice with current carrier
  - Tax ID/VAT
  - Passport or ID copy
  - Proof of address
- LOA template: https://assets.ctfassets.net/taysl255dolk/3k7kEutWHK91DYuMoG4Xa/6fdf040bb9d9aff432133f4d72997ab2/LOA_-_TELNYX_-_DOMINICAN_REPUBLIC.pdf
- Notes:
  - To keep numbers active during porting, coordinate with the losing provider to retain subscription/inbound services; otherwise, downtime up to 7 business days may occur.
  - After numbers are released by the losing carrier, expect at least 2 hours of service downtime due to local procedures.

## El Salvador
- Number types: Local, National
- Requirements:
  - Valid LOA (local address required)
  - DUI (ID) or Passport of authorized person
  - Latest invoice with local carrier
  - Proof of payment of the latest invoice
  - Company Registration certificate (if numbers belong to a company)
  - Power of Attorney (if numbers belong to a company)
- LOA template: request via porting@telnyx.com
- Submission tips:
  - Provide clear, high-resolution copies (PDF/JPEG) and verify completeness before submission.
  - Keep copies of all documents and obtain confirmation of receipt when submitting digitally.

## Mexico
- Number types and requirements:
  - Local/National:
    - LOA signed by the authorized person; for individuals, include NIP; signature should match ID
    - Authorized person’s National ID/Passport copy
    - Latest Invoice
    - For companies: Company Registration Certificate (Acta Constitutiva)
    - If the authorized person is not named in the registration certificate: Attorney’s Authorization (Poder notarial)
  - Toll-Free:
    - LOA signed by the authorized person (signature should match ID)
    - ID/Passport copy
    - Latest Invoice
    - Company documents as above (Acta Constitutiva and, if needed, Poder notarial)
  - Mobile:
    - LOA signed by the authorized person
    - National ID/Passport copy
    - Assignment Letter completed and signed by the authorized person
    - For companies: Notarial Deed (POA) granting general administrative powers to the representative who signs the LOA
- LOA templates:
  - Spanish: https://www.dropbox.com/sh/8f6a7w785g89au6/AABhEVcAvcSAZPfgHk94sUZja/Mexico?dl=0&preview=Telnyx_Mexican_PortabilityForm_Spanish-edit.pdf&subfolder_nav_tracking=1
  - English: https://www.dropbox.com/sh/8f6a7w785g89au6/AABhEVcAvcSAZPfgHk94sUZja/Mexico?dl=0&preview=Telnyx_portabilityForm-edit.pdf&subfolder_nav_tracking=1
- Porting windows and FOC:
  - Local/National/Toll-Free: Monday–Friday at 2:00 AM local; auto-triggered and can take up to 2 hours to complete; earliest FOC date is 2 business days from submission
  - Mobile: Monday–Friday 2:00–7:30 AM local; earliest FOC date is 7 business days from submission; expect loss of mobile data service and service interruption during the window when moving to VoIP
