# Number Porting with Telnyx: Policies, Requirements, FastPort, and Best Practices

*Part 2 of 2 — see also: [Part 1](number-porting-with-telnyx-policies-requirements-fastport-and-best-practices--part-1.md)*

A single, end-to-end guide to porting phone numbers with Telnyx, covering prerequisites, portal and API submission, automated validation, FastPort activation, timelines and holidays, status tracking and communications, international and toll-free nuances, port-outs, and escalation paths.

## Porting Out from Telnyx
- For customers
  - Initiate a port with your new (winning) carrier; they must contact Telnyx at lnp@telnyx.com to process the port-out
- For carriers (porting in from Telnyx)
  - Send LSRs to lnp@telnyx.com (no phone submissions; no handwritten/faxed LSRs)
  - Include: CCNA, PON, Account Number, Desired Due Date, Company Code, NNSP, AGAUTH, Initiator TN, ZIP, LOA attachment, and a clean 10-digit number list
  - Acknowledgment within 3 business days
  - Simple port-outs typically complete in ~2 business days; non-simple port-outs are treated as projects with timelines provided by Telnyx
  - Status requests: allow 48 hours before asking; we respond within 48 hours. Early status pings may push requests back in queue. Expedites are at Telnyx’s discretion. Email lnp@telnyx.com for LSR changes
  - Telnyx does not provide CSRs

## Holidays, Cutoffs, and Order Validity
- Processing hours: Mon–Fri, 9:00 AM–5:00 PM CT. LSRs received after 2:00 PM CT count as received next business day
- Holiday observance (no porting on observed holidays): New Year’s Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving Day, Day after Thanksgiving, Christmas Eve Day, Christmas Day. If a holiday falls on Saturday, the prior Friday is observed; if Sunday, the following Monday is observed. Other providers may observe additional holidays, which can delay inter-carrier work
- FOC policies (port-out/LSR context)
  - No same-day FOCs
  - FOC validity: 10 business days
  - If not activated in NPAC by end of the third business day, the order is canceled
  - Cancellations/reschedules must be received by 2:00 PM CST the business day before the scheduled port date
