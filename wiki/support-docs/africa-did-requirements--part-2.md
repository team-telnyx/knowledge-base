# Africa DID Requirements

*Part 2 of 2 — see also: [Part 1](africa-did-requirements--part-1.md)*

A consolidated guide to the identity, address, and letter-of-intent (LOI) documentation needed to purchase Telnyx DIDs across select African countries, including any business-use restrictions and address constraints by number type.

## Country: Uganda
Number types offered: Local, National, Toll-Free
- Local — Personal identity: Name, last name.
- Local — Business identity: Authorized representative’s name and last name; company name.
- Local — Address: Worldwide address.
- National — Personal identity: Name, last name; contact phone; passport or ID copy.
- National — Business identity: Name, last name; contact phone; company name; company incorporation certificate copy.
- National — Address: Address in Uganda; utility bill (less than 3 months old).
- Toll-Free — Personal identity: Name, last name; contact email; passport or ID copy; signed LOI (dated within 1 month).
- Toll-Free — Business identity: Authorized representative’s name and last name; contact email; company name; copy of Business Extract (dated within 3 months); signed LOI (dated within 1 month).
- Toll-Free — Address: Worldwide address; proof of address (dated within 3 months).

## Country: Zambia
Number type offered: Mobile
- Personal identity: Name, last name; contact phone; contact email; passport or ID copy.
- Business identity: Authorized representative’s name and last name; contact phone; contact email; company name; company registration certificate.
- Address: Address in Zambia (street, building number, postal code, city, country); proof of address (dated within 3 months).
- Additional: Service usage description.
- Timeline: Validation and activation typically take ~72 hours after documents are received.

## Notes on business-use-only markets
- Business use required (private use not allowed): Kenya (Local, Toll-Free, Mobile), Rwanda (Local), Nigeria (National).

## LOI guidance (where required)
- Required with specific timing: South Africa (Toll-Free, signed within 1 month), Uganda (Toll-Free, signed within 1 month), Nigeria (National, signed within 1 month).
- Required without specific timing stated: Ghana (National), Mozambique (Mobile). Telnyx provides an LOI template via support resources.

## Address constraints to note
- Area-code match: South Africa Local requires an address matching the DID area code.
- In-country address: South Africa National (address within South Africa) and Uganda National (address in Uganda with recent utility bill).
- Country-specific: Zambia Mobile requires a Zambian address with recent proof.
- Worldwide allowed: Often permitted for Mobile, Toll-Free, and many Local/National numbers elsewhere, frequently with proof of address dated within 3 months.

## Purchasing and pricing
- For availability and rates, see Telnyx Number Pricing: https://telnyx.com/pricing/numbers
- Country-specific pricing pages exist for some markets (e.g., Algeria: https://telnyx.com/pricing/numbers/dz, Ghana: https://telnyx.com/pricing/numbers/gh).

## Validation timelines
- Where stated (e.g., Benin, Cameroon, DRC, Rwanda, South Africa, Zambia), document review and activation typically take approximately 72 hours after Telnyx receives complete documentation.

## Need help?
- If requirements are unclear or your use case is atypical, contact Telnyx support for guidance and the latest LOI templates and forms.
