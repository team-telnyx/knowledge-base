# Telnyx Phone Numbers: DIDs, Number Types, and Ordering

*Part 1 of 5 — see also: [Part 2](telnyx-phone-numbers-dids-number-types-and-ordering--part-2.md), [Part 3](telnyx-phone-numbers-dids-number-types-and-ordering--part-3.md), [Part 4](telnyx-phone-numbers-dids-number-types-and-ordering--part-4.md), [Part 5](telnyx-phone-numbers-dids-number-types-and-ordering--part-5.md)*

A consolidated reference covering what DIDs are, the global number types Telnyx offers, how to search and buy numbers, how to request numbers that are unavailable, country-specific documentation requirements for international numbers, and best practices for working with the Telnyx numbering team.

## What is a DID?

A Direct Inward Dial number (DID) is a virtual number that functions as a regular phone number; however, it is not connected to any POTS line (landline). Once you have completed your account configuration through the Telnyx portal, your DID becomes the phone number at which anyone around the world can reach you.

## DID Numbers Telnyx Offers

Telnyx offers domestic DIDs in the United States and Canada as well as toll-free DIDs. International DIDs are also available. You can search for available numbers in the portal or contact Telnyx for assistance.

For US and Canada specifically, Telnyx has access to over 15,000 rate centers, so DIDs can be purchased from virtually any state or Canadian province. To begin, review the [Numbers Pricing](https://telnyx.com/pricing/numbers) page, create a Telnyx account, and start searching for numbers.

## Global Number Types

Telnyx supports a range of number types globally. Searching and purchasing these number types is done through the Mission Control Portal.

### Domestic Long-Code

These are typical local numbers used for a local business. Telnyx has coverage in over 20,000 rate centers across the United States and Canada. Domestic long codes support instant provisioning for fax, voice, SMS, and MMS. See the [domestic coverage map](https://telnyx.com/resources/telnyx-domestic-coverage-map) for details.

### Domestic Toll Free

Telnyx is a managing Responsible Organization (RespOrg QIT01) and can acquire, manage, and route toll-free calls to your business, as well as quickly reroute traffic in the event of service impairments.

### Toll Free Voice

Telnyx allows users to purchase pre-provisioned voice toll-free numbers with their routing already set for instant use. Select **Quickship** on the number search interface to receive numbers that are ready to use for voice calling. If Quickship is not selected, routing provisioning can take up to one hour after order completion. Quickship only applies to the voice portion of toll-free numbers so they can be instantly provisioned for receiving inbound calls. Learn more about Quickship through the [API documentation](https://developers.telnyx.com/api/numbers/list-available-phone-number-blocks).

### Toll Free Messaging

Telnyx supports messaging on toll-free numbers to support large campaigns. Both toll-free SMS and MMS are available for the USA and Canada. The messaging portion is not instantly provisioned; you are recommended to submit your use case first to avoid spam blocks. See the dedicated [Toll-Free Messaging](toll-free-messaging.md) article for acceptable use cases.

### International Number Types

Telnyx's [international coverage map](https://telnyx.com/global-coverage) continues to grow, covering local, national, toll-free, shared cost, and mobile numbers across select countries. When ordering international numbers, Telnyx may require documentation to comply with local regulators. The quicker documentation is received, the quicker the number can be provisioned.

At checkout, if an international number requires documentation, the user interface will make this clear. Once ordered, you will be brought to the number orders page where you can upload the required documentation. You will also receive an email from Telnyx indicating the time-frame for providing documentation; if not provided within 10 days, the number may expire and be returned to inventory.
