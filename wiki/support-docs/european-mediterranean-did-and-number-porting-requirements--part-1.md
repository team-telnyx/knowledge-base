# European & Mediterranean DID and Number Porting Requirements

*Part 1 of 3 — see also: [Part 2](european-mediterranean-did-and-number-porting-requirements--part-2.md), [Part 3](european-mediterranean-did-and-number-porting-requirements--part-3.md)*

This page consolidates Telnyx's documentation, identity verification, and porting requirements for acquiring and porting DID numbers across the United Kingdom, Ireland, Turkey, Croatia, Germany, Greece, Italy, Cyprus, Switzerland, Malta, and Albania. It covers the documents required for new number purchases (personal/business identity and address verification) and for porting existing numbers (LOA, invoices, IDs, and country-specific forms), along with EU rules on what counts as a "local" identity document.

## Overview

This page consolidates the documentation, identity, and porting requirements for acquiring and porting Direct Inward Dialing (DID) numbers across several European and Mediterranean countries served by Telnyx. The countries covered include the United Kingdom, Ireland, Turkey, Croatia, Germany, Greece, Italy, Cyprus, Switzerland, Malta, and Albania. Requirements are grouped into two main workflows: purchasing new DID numbers (which requires identity, business, and address verification) and porting existing numbers from another carrier (which requires a Letter of Authorization, proof of ownership, and supporting documentation).

## United Kingdom (UK) DID Requirements

To purchase a UK number, the following must be provided.

**Local, National, and Toll-Free & Mobile Numbers** require the same set of documents, with one addition for toll-free and mobile: the business use case must indicate whether the customer is sub-allocating.

For **personal identity verification**:
- Name, last name
- Contact phone number
- Local passport or ID copy

For **business identity verification**:
- Name, last name of an authorized representative
- Company name
- Contact phone number
- Company website
- Local company registration certificate
- Business use case

For **address verification**:
- Address within the UK (street, building number, postal code, city, and country)
- A copy of a utility bill (less than 3 months old)

End-users must be physically present in the country when purchasing numbers from that country. Once documentation is received, validation and activation take approximately 72 hours.

## Ireland DID Requirements

To purchase an Ireland number, the following must be provided.

**Local Numbers** require:

For **personal identity verification**:
- Name, last name
- Contact phone number
- Contact e-mail
- Local copy of ID/passport

For **business identity verification**:
- Name, last name
- Contact phone number
- Company name
- Contact e-mail
- Local business registration certificate

For **address verification**:
- Address matching DID area code (street, building number, postal code, city and country)
- Proof of address (dated within 3 months)

Additional information: a service usage description is required.

**National and Toll-Free Numbers** follow the same requirements as local numbers, except the address does not need to match the DID area code — only an address in Ireland is required.

**Mobile Numbers** require business identity verification only (no personal identity option). Business use is required; private use is not allowed. The same address verification and service usage description apply.

Once documentation is received, validation and activation take approximately 72 hours.

## Turkey DID Requirements

To purchase a Turkey number, the following must be provided.

**Local and National Numbers** require:

For **business identity verification**:
- Name, last name of an authorized representative
- Company name
- Contact phone number
- Circular of Signatures (İmza Sirküleri)
- Tax Registration Certificate (Vergi Levhası)
- Chamber of Commerce Registration Document (Faaliyet Belgesi)
- Trade Registry Gazette (Ticari Sicil Gazetesi)
- Passport or ID copy of an authorized representative (Yetkili Kimlik Fotokopisi)

For **address verification**:
- Address in Turkey (street, building number, postal code, city, and country)
- Address confirmation from the Turkish government portal (Adres Beyani), available at [turkiye.gov.tr](https://www.turkiye.gov.tr/nvi-yerlesim-yeri-ve-diger-adres-belgesi-sorgulama)

Istanbul Asia (216) and Istanbul Europe (212) phone numbers must be strictly separated. Companies registered in the European region are eligible only for the 212 prefix, while companies registered in the Asian region are restricted to the 216 prefix.

## Cyprus DID Requirements

To purchase a Cyprus number, the following must be provided.

**Local Numbers** require:

For **personal identity verification**:
- Name, last name
- Contact phone number
- Passport or ID copy

For **business identity verification**:
- Name, last name of an authorized representative
- Company name
- Contact phone number
- Cypriot company registration certificate

For **address verification**:
- Address worldwide (street, building number, postal code, city, and country)
- Proof of address (dated within 3 months)

**National Numbers** require the same personal and business identity documents, plus a Cypriot company incorporation certificate copy and a utility bill less than 6 months old.

**Toll-Free Numbers** require the same personal identity documents, plus for business identity: name, last name of an authorized representative, company name, contact phone number, and passport or ID copy of an authorized representative. Address verification requires a worldwide address and proof of address dated within 3 months.

Once documentation is received, validation and activation take approximately 72 hours.

## Greece DID Requirements

To purchase a Greece number, the following must be provided.

**Local Numbers** require:

For **personal identity verification**:
- Name, last name
- Contact phone number
- Local passport or ID copy
- VAT number

For **business identity verification**:
- Name, last name of an authorized representative
- Company name
- Contact phone number
- Local passport or ID copy of an authorized representative
- Local company registration certificate
- VAT number of an authorized representative
- LOA or POA (Letter of Authorization or Power of Attorney) — filled in and signed; document provided by Telnyx

For **address verification**:
- Address matching the DID area code (street, building number, postal code, city, and country)
- Proof of address (dated within 3 months)

End-users must be physically present in the country when purchasing numbers from that country.

## Malta DID Requirements

To purchase a Malta number, the following must be provided.

**National Numbers** require:

For **personal identity verification**:
- Name, last name
- Contact phone number
- Local passport or ID copy

For **business identity verification**:
- Name, last name of an authorized representative
- Company name
- Contact phone number
- Local company registration certificate

For **address verification**:
- Address in Malta (street, building number, postal code, city and country)
- Proof of address (dated within 3 months)

**Toll-Free Numbers** follow the same requirements, except business identity verification does not require the name and last name of an authorized representative.

End-users must be physically present in the country when purchasing numbers from that country. Once documentation is received, validation and activation take approximately 72 hours.

## Albania DID Requirements

To purchase an Albanian number, the following must be provided.

**Local Numbers** require:

For **business identity verification**:
- Name, last name of an authorized representative
- Company name
- Contact phone number
- Passport or ID copy of an authorized representative
- Company registration certificate

For **address verification**:
- Address worldwide (street, building number, postal code, city, and country)

Business use is required; private use is not allowed.

**Toll-Free Numbers** require the same business identity and address verification documents, except the name, last name, and contact phone number of an authorized representative are not required. Business use is required; private use is not allowed.
