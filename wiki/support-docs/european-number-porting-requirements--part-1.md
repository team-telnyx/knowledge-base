# European Number Porting Requirements

*Part 1 of 3 — see also: [Part 2](european-number-porting-requirements--part-2.md), [Part 3](european-number-porting-requirements--part-3.md)*

A comprehensive reference for number porting requirements across European countries supported by Telnyx, covering required documentation, letter of authorization (LOA) templates, porting procedures, and country-specific rules.

## Overview

Number porting requirements vary significantly across European countries. Most countries require a Letter of Authorization (LOA) and a latest invoice from the current provider as baseline documentation. Several countries share a common international LOA template, while others require country-specific forms. Local address requirements, tax identification, and identity documents are commonly needed for local and national number types. This page details the specific requirements for each supported European country.

## Austria

**Local and National Numbers**

1. LOA (local address required)
1. Account Number
1. ID / Passport copy
1. Company Registration certificate
1. Latest Invoice
1. Proof of address

**Toll-Free Numbers**

1. LOA
1. Current underlying carrier's name
1. Porting history (if the number(s) were previously ported)
1. Account Number
1. Latest Invoice

## Belgium

**Local Numbers**

1. LOA (local address required; the area code of the number must match the address on file)
1. VAT / TAX ID
1. Latest Invoice
1. Proof of local address

**National and Toll-Free Numbers**

1. LOA (national address required)
1. VAT / TAX ID
1. Latest Invoice
1. Proof of local address

Download the Belgium LOA [here](https://assets.ctfassets.net/taysl255dolk/6YYtHkPiDoOfhR8Vp3QxUz/3f7b77cf78ecdebe280c6c0226ac12ea/LoA_-_Telnyx_-_INTL.pdf).

## Cyprus

**Local Numbers**

1. LOA (with local address)
1. Latest Invoice with the current carrier
1. Company Registration (if the number belongs to a company)
1. ID or Passport Number of the legal representative
1. Proof of address

**National and Toll-Free Numbers**

1. LOA (with national address)
1. Latest Invoice with the current carrier
1. Company Registration (if the number belongs to a company)
1. ID or Passport Number of the legal representative
1. Proof of address

Download the Cyprus LOA [here](https://assets.ctfassets.net/taysl255dolk/1Cq2IXxuZiFRu03aA3LXB6/8006b5ee40f97ac95f050be7013c8bcb/CYPRUS_LOA-_Telnyx.pdf).

## Denmark

**Local, National, and Toll-Free Numbers**

1. LOA (a local address is mandatory; the signature must be a wet signature)
1. Local TAX ID / VAT
1. Latest Invoice with the current provider

Download the Denmark LOA [here](https://assets.ctfassets.net/taysl255dolk/6YYtHkPiDoOfhR8Vp3QxUz/3f7b77cf78ecdebe280c6c0226ac12ea/LoA_-_Telnyx_-_INTL.pdf).

## Finland

**Local / National Numbers**

1. LOA
1. VAT / TAX ID or social security number
1. Latest Invoice

**Toll-Free Numbers**

1. LOA
1. VAT / TAX ID or social security number
1. Attorney's authorization for representative (if the number belongs to a company)
1. Latest Invoice

**Common rejections:**

- Wrong end-user on document
- Signature not readable/valid on the LOA

Download the Finland POA [here](https://assets.ctfassets.net/taysl255dolk/BjAY6YlvDP8UHiRtMAtSP/009510c32ea7832407ab6c525f69478a/FINLAND-TELNYX.pdf).

## France

**Local, National, and Toll-Free Numbers**

1. LOA (national address mandatory)
1. SIRET code (for business accounts only; must be 14 digits long)
1. RIO code (must be 12 characters long)
1. Latest Invoice with the current provider
1. Proof of address (dated within 3 months of the request)

The **RIO code** can be obtained by dialing 3179 or 0 805 92 3179. If the current carrier does not allow calls to special numbers, contact the carrier directly.

The **SIRET code** is an INSEE code that identifies any French establishment or business; it is always 14 digits.

**Port types in France:**

All port types take at least **8 business days** to complete.

| Port Type | Description |
|---|---|
| **Isolated** | Porting only one number |
| **Full Portability** | Migrating a main number along with ALL associated numbers |
| **Partial Portability** | Migrating some associated numbers to a BTN without porting the actual BTN |
| **Complex Portability** | Migrating a main phone number without the associated numbers; you must specify which number becomes the new BTN |

**Time of port:** There are two available time slots — a morning trigger (10 AM to 1 PM) and an afternoon trigger (2 PM to 5 PM).

**Important:** Verify all information before Telnyx submits a port request. If a request is rejected, Telnyx must wait for the losing carrier to formally reject it before a new request can be submitted, which can take up to 3 business days.

**Common rejections:**

- Business address mismatch
- SIRET code invalid
- Business name mismatch
- Pending porting request with another carrier

Download the France LOA [here](https://assets.ctfassets.net/taysl255dolk/4pv4cqjnsUfEGbyexn1Ycy/536af1c7e37734422a9556ea38582ceb/Telnyx_LoA_-_France.pdf).

## Germany

Number porting in Germany follows strict rules that must be adhered to.

**Number length:**

- Numbers in Berlin, Frankfurt, Hamburg, and Munich have a standard length of 10 digits (excluding leading 0).
- Numbers in other municipalities have a standard length of 11 digits (excluding leading 0).

**Extensions:** If a number is longer than the standard 10/11 digits, it likely uses an extension (typically a 10-block or 100-block). Only the standard number itself is ported — the block extension will **not** be ported. Check with the current operator to confirm whether extension numbers can be ported; you must be assigned the whole 10/100 block or the port of the standard number will be blocked.

**Local / National Numbers**

1. Porting Form (local address from the same city as the number to be ported)
1. Latest Invoice from the current provider(s)
1. Proof of address
1. ID / Passport copy / Company Registration certificate

**Toll-Free Numbers**

1. Porting Form (local address required within Germany)
1. Latest Invoice from the current provider (only if the current provider is a reseller)
1. Number allocation document ("Zuteilungsbescheid")
1. ID / Passport copy / Company Registration certificate

**Porting hours:** Requests are scheduled during the standard porting window of **6 AM to 8 AM local time**. On FOC, the port is triggered at 6 AM local and the carrier has 2 hours to release the number(s). Porting outside this window is not available.

**Date changes:** Once a port date is confirmed, it can be changed (the new date must be at least 5 business days away from the change request). A date change fee applies.

**Additional rules:**

- Maximum of 10 numbers per porting form and/or 1 range.
- A separate form is required for each city being ported from, including the address within that city.
- A separate form is required for each donor carrier.
- Confirm number details and any assigned connections with the current provider before submitting.
- Numbers cannot be ported until the end of the contract period with the current operator. Notify your carrier of intent to port before submitting to Telnyx; early termination fees may apply. If not done beforehand, the confirmed porting date will default to the last day of the contract.
- If a connection is assigned to the DID(s), discuss with the current provider beforehand. Once the DID(s) port to Telnyx, any remaining connection will be terminated.

**Porting out of Telnyx:** The gaining provider must submit a request by email to [porting.intl@telnyx.com](mailto:porting.intl@telnyx.com) with the Standard German Porting Form. Telnyx will create a port-out request in the end-user's Portal, which must be authorized within **4 business days**. Once authorized, Telnyx will approve the port out.

Download the Local/National porting form [here](https://www.dropbox.com/s/4d6jx7izmgaiun4/german-porting-document-and-address%20confirmation.pdf?dl=0) and the Toll-Free form [here](https://assets.ctfassets.net/taysl255dolk/1tsn59edoZYrYtwIzaKYd0/f4563a1894c76e07a588eb32e364999e/TFN_LOA.pdf).
