# Europe DID Requirements: Belgium, France, Germany, Ireland, Italy, Netherlands, Spain, Sweden, United Kingdom

*Part 1 of 2 — see also: [Part 2](europe-did-requirements-belgium-france-germany-ireland-italy-netherlands-spain-sweden-united-kingdom--part-2.md)*

Consolidated Telnyx documentation for purchasing European DIDs, detailing per-country identity and address verification, number-type nuances (local, national, mobile, toll-free, shared-cost), special forms and formats, presence and business-use rules, and typical validation timelines.

## Overview and common notes
- Scope: Requirements to acquire Telnyx phone numbers (DIDs) in Belgium, France, Germany, Ireland, Italy, Netherlands, Spain, Sweden, and the United Kingdom (UK), organized by number type.
- Typical activation timeline: Where noted, validation and activation occur approximately 72 hours after Telnyx receives complete documentation.
- Proof of address (PoA): Where required, PoA must generally be dated within the last 3 months.
- Physical presence: Many markets require end-users to be physically present in the country when purchasing numbers; see the country sections for applicability.
- Requirement Groups: Mandatory for Italy (IT) and Sweden (SE) beginning September 16, 2024. See the Telnyx guide: https://support.telnyx.com/en/articles/9801714-requirement-groups-for-ordering-phone-numbers
- Document format/extra forms:
  - Germany: Only scanned documents are accepted. A Germany registration form is required for many number types. Form: https://telnyx-48416ce2297b.intercom-attachments-1.com/i/o/ltcafuzd/1236805126/b02589a58f094051e75b2f759d42/Telnyx+Germany+Registration+Form.pdf
  - Italy: All documents must be submitted in PDF format; regulators may request additional validating details.

## Belgium
### Local numbers
- Personal identity: Name, last name; contact phone; local passport or ID copy.
- Business identity: Authorized representative’s name and last name; company name; contact phone; local passport/ID copy of authorized representative; local company registration certificate; VAT number.
- Address: Address matching the DID area code; PoA (≤3 months).
- Additional: End-users must be physically present in Belgium when purchasing numbers.

### Toll-free numbers
- Personal identity: Name, last name; contact phone; local passport or ID copy.
- Business identity: Authorized representative’s name and last name; company name; contact phone; local passport/ID copy of authorized representative; local company registration certificate.
- Address: Address in Belgium; PoA (≤3 months).
- Additional: End-users must be physically present. Typical activation ≈72 hours after complete docs.

### Mobile numbers
- Personal identity: Name, last name; contact phone; contact email; local passport or ID copy.
- Business identity: Authorized representative’s name and last name; company name; contact phone; contact email; VAT number; local company registration certificate; local passport/ID copy of authorized representative.
- Address: Address in Belgium; PoA (≤3 months).
- Additional: End-users must be physically present.

## France
### Local numbers
- Personal identity: Name, last name; contact phone; local copy of ID or passport.
- Business identity: Authorized representative’s name and last name; company name; contact phone; local company registration certificate.
- Address: Address in France; PoA (≤3 months).
- Additional: End-users must be physically present.

### National numbers
- Requirements mirror Local numbers (personal/business identity and address in France; PoA ≤3 months; physical presence required).

### Toll-free numbers
- Business identity: Authorized representative’s name and last name; company name; contact phone; local company registration certificate; SIREN (company registration) number.
- Address: Address in France; PoA (≤3 months).
- Additional: Physical presence required. Business use required (private use not allowed). Typical activation ≈72 hours after complete docs.

## Germany
### Local numbers
- Personal identity: Name, last name; contact phone; local ID or passport; Germany registration form (signed; include representative’s name, surname, date, and company stamp or handwritten company name if no stamp).
- Business identity: Authorized representative’s name and last name; company name; contact phone; local company registration certificate; Germany registration form (signed as above).
- Address: Address matching the DID area code; utility bill copy (≤3 months).
- Additional: Physical presence required. Only scanned documents are accepted.

### National numbers
- Personal identity: Name, last name; contact phone; local ID or passport; Germany registration form.
- Business identity: Authorized representative’s name and last name; company name; contact phone; local passport/ID copy of authorized representative; local company registration certificate; Germany registration form.
- Address: Address in Germany matching the address in the PoA/ID; PoA (≤3 months).
- Additional: Physical presence required. Only scanned documents accepted.

### Toll-free numbers
- Business identity: Authorized representative’s name and last name; company name; contact phone; place of birth (authorized representative); local passport/ID copy of authorized representative; signed LOI (≤1 month old); power of attorney (provided by Telnyx); local company registration certificate.
- Address: Address in Germany matching the local company registration certificate; PoA (≤3 months).
- Additional: Physical presence required. Only scanned documents accepted. Service usage description in German and marketing details in German. Business use required. Typical activation ≈72 hours after complete docs.

## Ireland
### Local numbers
- Personal identity: Name, last name; contact phone; contact email; local copy of ID/passport.
- Business identity: Name, last name; contact phone; company name; contact email; local business registration certificate.
- Address: Address matching the DID area code; PoA (≤3 months).
- Additional: Service usage description.

### National and Toll-free numbers
- Personal identity: Name, last name; contact phone; contact email; local copy of ID/passport.
- Business identity: Name, last name; contact phone; company name; contact email; local business registration certificate.
- Address: Address in Ireland; PoA (≤3 months).
- Additional: Service usage description. Typical activation ≈72 hours after complete docs.

### Mobile numbers
- Business identity: Name, last name; contact phone; company name; contact email; local business registration certificate.
- Address: Address in Ireland; PoA (≤3 months).
- Additional: Service usage description. Business use required (private use not allowed). Typical activation ≈72 hours after complete docs.

## Italy
- Requirement Groups: Mandatory from September 16, 2024 (see guide link in Overview).

### Local numbers
- Customer types: natural person (personal use, no VAT); legal entity (registered entity, VAT); sole proprietorship (individual business, VAT).
- Personal identity (natural persons and sole proprietorships where applicable): Name, last name; date of birth; place of birth; personal tax code (natural persons and sole proprietorships); local ID/passport (front and back); identity document type, issuer, number, expiration date.
- Business identity (legal entities and sole proprietorships): Authorized representative’s name and last name; representative’s date and place of birth; local ID/passport of representative (front and back); identity document type, issuer, number, expiration date; local company registration certificate; VAT number; supplemental address of legal representative’s residence in Italy (legal entities only).
- Address: Address in Italy; PoA (≤3 months).
- Additional: Physical presence required. All documents must be submitted in PDF format. Regulators may request additional validating details.

### Toll-free numbers
- Business identity: Business registration certificate; VAT number; local passport/ID copy of an authorized representative (front and back); company representative’s Tax ID.
- Address: Address in Italy; PoA (≤3 months).
- Additional: Physical presence required. Additional validating details may be requested upon review.
