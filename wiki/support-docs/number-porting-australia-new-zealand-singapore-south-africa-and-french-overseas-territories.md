# Number Porting: Australia, New Zealand, Singapore, South Africa, and French Overseas Territories

Consolidated requirements, timelines, documents, and key process notes for porting numbers to Telnyx in Australia, New Zealand, Singapore, South Africa, and the French overseas territories (Guadeloupe, Martinique, French Guiana, Mayotte, Réunion, Saint Barthélemy & Saint Martin).

## Scope and quick facts
- This page covers local/national (and where available, toll-free and mobile) porting for: Australia (AU), New Zealand (NZ), Singapore (SG), South Africa (ZA), and the French overseas territories listed below.
- Mobile portability: Available in Australia; not available in New Zealand and Singapore. Other regions here list only local/national/toll-free.
- Always keep service active with the losing carrier until cutover completes. Ensure all submitted details match the losing carrier’s records exactly to avoid rejections.

## Australia
### Portable number types
- Local: yes (CAT A or CAT C)
- National: yes (CAT A or CAT C)
- Toll-free (1800): yes (CAT Special)
- Mobile (+614): yes (Mobile)

### Timeframes (typical to FOC)
- CAT A (simple): 3–6 business days
- CAT C (complex): 5+ weeks
- CAT Special (toll-free): 3–6 business days
- Mobile: 1–5 business days

### Port category guidance
- CAT A (simple): single TNs/home-phone style services, no complex features
- CAT C (complex): ISDN, hunt groups, PBX services, bundled packages, or multiple numbers on one service
- CAT Special: toll-free/shared-cost numbers
- Important: Complex services on the line will be canceled during porting; plan dependencies accordingly.

### Documents
- Local/National/Toll-free:
  - LOA with a local Australian address
  - ABN (for business accounts)
  - Latest invoice from current carrier (within 30 days)
- Mobile:
  - LOA with local AU address
  - Latest invoice
  - State whether service is Prepaid or Postpaid
  - Identity verification: 2 government-issued IDs (see list)
  - For prepaid: end-user full name, date of birth, account number

#### Accepted identity documents (provide any 2)
- State/Territory driver’s licence
- Australian passport
- Australian citizenship certificate
- Proof-of-age card
- Australian birth certificate
- Foreign military ID card
- Current foreign passport with valid entry stamp/visa
- Australian Government-issued photo ID card
- Trade/work/business licence

### Mobile verification (required)
- During the port request, a verification step sends a unique code to each mobile number (SMS or call).
- Enter each code to verify ownership. All numbers must be verified or the port remains in draft.

### LOA and preparation
- Download: Australia LOA (PDF) — https://assets.ctfassets.net/taysl255dolk/72Cl4rcgvAl9N3Lt4iUalk/34cad211371dd434f52088f573a5f6b4/LoA_-_TELNYX_-_australia.pdf
- Before completing: save as non-editable PDF, fill all fields, match losing carrier records, ensure a local AU address (used for emergency services).

### Common rejection reasons and fixes
- Company information mismatch: confirm exact legal name/details with current carrier.
- Complex services not declared: if ISDN/hunt/PBX/bundles exist, submit as CAT C.
- Associated numbers: disassociate with current carrier or port linked numbers together.
- Disconnected number: ensure numbers are active until after cutover.

### FOC changes and porting hours
- FOC date changes: provide at least 48 business hours’ notice before the scheduled cutover.
- Porting window: standard time 8 AM–12 PM local.

## New Zealand
### Portable number types
- Local: yes
- National: yes
- Toll-free: yes
- Mobile: not available

### Timeframes (typical to FOC)
- Local/National/Toll-free: typically 5–10 business days; may extend to 10+ business days depending on the losing carrier.

### Documents
- LOA signed by the authorized person
- Latest invoice from current carrier (within 30 days)
- Full contact details for the authorized person
- Local address in New Zealand
- Personal accounts: full name, contact phone, NZ address
- Business accounts: authorized person’s name, contact phone, company name, NZ address

### LOA and preparation
- Download: New Zealand LOA (INTL format) — https://assets.ctfassets.net/taysl255dolk/6YYtHkPiDoOfhR8Vp3QxUz/c9e0cb60d5051829eb44349be299df79/LoA_-_Telnyx_-_INTL.pdf
- Before completing: save as non-editable PDF, fill all fields, match losing carrier records, include a valid NZ address.

### Porting process
1) Gather documents. 2) Submit request in Mission Control — https://portal.telnyx.com. 3) Upload LOA and invoice. 4) Telnyx submits to losing carrier. 5) Receive FOC date. 6) Numbers activate on FOC.

### Porting hours
- Cutover slots: 8 AM or 12 PM local time, Monday–Friday.

## Singapore
### Portable number types and port types
- Local (Level 6): yes — use DEL (simple) or DID/DDI (complex)
- National: not available
- Toll-free: not available
- Mobile: not available

### Port type guidance
- DEL (Direct Exchange Line): single-line PSTN/standard services, no complex features
- DID/DDI (Direct Inward Dialling): number ranges/blocks, complex services, PABX/private systems
- Number blocks: port the full range or have the current carrier disassociate desired numbers.

### Timeframes (typical to FOC)
- DEL: 6–7 business days
- DID/DDI: 8–18 business days

### Documents
- Company accounts: country-specific LOA (authorized signatory), Business Registration/Certificate of Incorporation, Work Permit/Employment Pass for non-citizen applicants, latest carrier invoice
- Personal accounts: country-specific LOA (end-user), NRIC/Passport, Work Permit/Employment Pass for non-citizen applicants, latest carrier invoice

### LOA and preparation
- Download: Singapore LOA (PDF) — https://images.telnyx.com/Telnyx-Singapore-LOA.pdf
- Before completing: save as non-editable PDF, fill all designated fields, ensure exact match to losing carrier records, signature by the authorized person/end-user.

### FOC changes and process
- FOC date changes: provide at least 72 business hours (SGT) notice before cutover.
- Process: gather docs → submit in Mission Control — https://portal.telnyx.com → select DEL or DID/DDI → upload LOA/supporting docs → await FOC → activate on FOC.

## South Africa
### Documents by number type
- Local numbers:
  - LOA (local ZA address required)
  - Latest invoice
  - ID/Passport copy
  - Company registration certificate
  - Proof of address
- National/Toll-free numbers:
  - LOA (national address required); for company requests, LOA must be signed by an Authorized Person (wet signature only; no e-signatures) and be on company letterhead or include a company stamp
  - Latest invoice
  - ID/Passport copy
  - Company registration certificate
  - Proof of address

### Linked services warning
- Declare any DSL functionality or other services linked to the line. Undeclared services will be lost and unrecoverable after the port. For special services, provide a number that will remain with the losing carrier to keep those services linked.

### LOA
- Download: South Africa porting form — https://www.dropbox.com/s/fvfhmlarpen9ucb/SouthAfrica-portingform.pdf?dl=0

### Documents (Local/National/Toll-free)

- Latest invoice
