# Number Porting with Telnyx

*Part 1 of 3 — see also: [Part 2](number-porting-with-telnyx--part-2.md), [Part 3](number-porting-with-telnyx--part-3.md)*

A consolidated reference for porting phone numbers into and out of Telnyx, covering port-in procedures, SLA timelines, automated validation, status tracking, port-out handling, reseller and carrier-specific guidance, and programmatic API automation.

## Overview

Telnyx supports Local Number Portability (LNP) for moving telephone numbers between carriers. Numbers can be ported into Telnyx through Mission Control or the Porting API, and ported away from Telnyx by initiating a request with a gaining carrier. Telnyx ports telephone numbers in accordance with applicable regulatory rules and industry guidelines (SPID: 073H).
