# Europe DID Requirements: Belgium, France, Germany, Ireland, Italy, Netherlands, Spain, Sweden, United Kingdom

*Part 2 of 2 — see also: [Part 1](europe-did-requirements-belgium-france-germany-ireland-italy-netherlands-spain-sweden-united-kingdom--part-1.md)*

Consolidated Telnyx documentation for purchasing European DIDs, detailing per-country identity and address verification, number-type nuances (local, national, mobile, toll-free, shared-cost), special forms and formats, presence and business-use rules, and typical validation timelines.

## Netherlands
### Local numbers
- Personal identity: Name, last name; contact phone; passport or ID copy.
- Business identity: Authorized representative’s name and last name; company name; contact phone; passport/ID copy of authorized representative; company registration certificate.
- Address: Address matching the DID area code; PoA (≤3 months).

### National numbers
- Business identity: Authorized representative’s name and last name; company name; contact phone.
- Address: Address in the Netherlands.
- Additional: Business use required (personal use not allowed).

### Mobile numbers
- Personal identity: Name, last name.
- Business identity: Company name.
- Address: Address worldwide.
- Additional: A2A/A2P use only.

### Toll-free numbers
- Business identity: Authorized representative’s name and last name; contact phone; company name; company incorporation certificate; proof of allocation from the Dutch national regulator.
- Address: Address worldwide.
- Additional: Service usage description; email address. Business use required. Typical activation ≈72 hours after complete docs.

## Spain
### Local, Toll-free, and Shared-cost numbers
- Personal identity: Name, last name; contact phone; local copy of ID or passport; Spanish DNI or NIE.
- Business identity: Authorized representative’s name and last name; contact phone; company name; local company registration certificate; CIF (Certificado de Identificación Fiscal).
- Address: Address matching the DID area code; PoA (≤3 months).
- Additional: Physical presence required.

### National numbers
- Personal identity: Name, last name; contact phone; local passport or ID copy.
- Business identity: Authorized representative’s name and last name; contact phone; company name; local company registration certificate.
- Address: Address in Spain; PoA (≤3 months).
- Additional: Physical presence required. Typical activation ≈72 hours after complete docs.

## Sweden
- Requirement Groups: Mandatory from September 16, 2024 (see guide link in Overview).

### Local numbers
- Business identity: Authorized representative’s name and last name; contact phone; contact email; company name; passport or ID copy of authorized representative; business registration certificate; business registration number.
- Address: Address matching the DID area code; PoA (≤3 months).
- Additional: Physical presence required. Business use required (private use not allowed).

### National numbers
- Business identity: Same as Local (authorized representative and company details, ID, business registration certificate/number).
- Address: Address in Sweden; PoA (≤3 months).
- Additional: Physical presence required. Business use required.

### Mobile numbers
- Personal identity: Name, last name; contact phone; local passport or ID copy.
- Business identity: Authorized representative’s name and last name; company name; contact phone; local company incorporation certificate.
- Address: Address in Sweden; PoA (≤3 months).
- Additional: Physical presence required.

### Toll-free numbers
- Personal identity: Name, last name; contact phone; local passport or ID copy.
- Business identity: Name, last name; contact phone; company name; local company incorporation certificate.
- Address: Address in Sweden; PoA (≤3 months).
- Additional: Physical presence required.

## United Kingdom (UK)
### Local numbers
- Personal identity: Name, last name; contact phone; local passport or ID copy.
- Business identity: Authorized representative’s name and last name; company name; contact phone; company website; local company registration certificate; business use case.
- Address: Address within the UK; utility bill (≤3 months).
- Additional: Physical presence required.

### National numbers
- Requirements mirror Local numbers (personal/business identity and UK address with utility bill ≤3 months; physical presence required).

### Toll-free numbers
- Personal identity: Name, last name; contact phone; local passport or ID copy.
- Business identity: Authorized representative’s name and last name; company name; contact phone; company website; local company registration certificate; business use case (indicate if sub-allocating).
- Address: Address within the UK; utility bill (≤3 months).
- Additional: Physical presence required. Typical activation ≈72 hours after complete docs.

### Mobile numbers
- Personal identity: Name, last name; contact phone; local passport or ID copy.
- Business identity: Authorized representative’s name and last name; company name; contact phone; company website; local company registration certificate; business use case (indicate if sub-allocating).
- Address: Address within the UK; utility bill (≤3 months).
- Additional: Physical presence required. Typical activation ≈72 hours after complete docs.
