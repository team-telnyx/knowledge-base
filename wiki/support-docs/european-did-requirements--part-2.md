# European DID Requirements

*Part 2 of 3 — see also: [Part 1](european-did-requirements--part-1.md), [Part 3](european-did-requirements--part-3.md)*

This page consolidates the documentation requirements for purchasing Telnyx DID numbers across twelve European countries: Belgium, Bosnia & Herzegovina, Croatia, Czech Republic, Germany, Hungary, Montenegro, Netherlands, North Macedonia, Serbia, Slovakia, and Slovenia. It covers personal identity, business identity, and address verification requirements for local, national, mobile, and toll-free number types, along with EU-wide rules on what counts as a "local" identity document and the standard 72-hour activation timeline.

## Hungary

### Local Numbers

**Personal identity verification**
- Name, last name
- Contact phone number
- Passport or ID copy

**Business identity verification**
- Name, last name of an authorized representative
- Company name
- Contact phone number
- Company registration certificate

**Address verification**
- Address matching the DID area code (street, building number, postal code, city, and country)
- Proof of address (dated within 3 months)

### National Numbers

**Personal identity verification**
- Name, last name
- Contact phone number
- Passport or ID copy

**Business identity verification**
- Name, last name of an authorized representative
- Company name
- Contact phone number
- Company registration certificate

**Address verification**
- Address in Hungary (street, building number, postal code, city, and country)
- Proof of address (dated within 3 months)

### Toll-Free Numbers

**Personal identity verification**
- Name, last name
- Contact phone number
- Passport or ID copy

**Business identity verification**
- Name, last name of an authorized representative
- Company name
- Contact phone number
- Company registration certificate

**Address verification**
- Address in Hungary (street, building number, postal code, city, and country)
- Proof of address (dated within 3 months)

## Montenegro

### Local Numbers

**Business identity verification**
- Name, last name of an authorized representative
- Company name

**Address verification**
- Address worldwide (street, building number, postal code, city and country)

A service usage description is also required. Business use is required; private use is not allowed.

- Passport or ID copy

**Business identity verification**
- Name, last name of an authorized representative
- Company name
- Contact phone number
- Passport or ID copy of an authorized representative
- Company registration certificate

**Address verification**
- Address matching the DID area code (street, building number, postal code, city and country)
- Proof of address (dated within 3 months)

### National Numbers

**Business identity verification**
- Name, last name of an authorized representative
- Company name
- Contact phone number

**Address verification**
- Address in Netherlands (street, building number, postal code, city and country)

Business use is required for Netherlands national numbers; personal use is not allowed.

### Mobile Numbers

**Personal identity verification**
- Name, last name

**Business identity verification**
- Company name

**Address verification**
- Address Worldwide (street, building number, postal code, city and country)

The numbers may only be used as "Application to application" or "Application to person" (A2A / A2P use only).

### Toll-Free Numbers

**Business identity verification**
- Name, last name of an authorized representative
- Contact phone number
- Company name
- Company incorporation certificate copy
- Proof of allocation from the Dutch national regulator

**Address verification**
- Address worldwide (street, building number, postal code, city and country)

A service usage description and email address are also required. Business use is required for Netherlands toll-free numbers; personal use is not allowed.

## Identity Verification: What "Local" Means

For most orders, your proof of identity must be issued by the country in which you are making your purchase. As an exception, customers within the European Union may use a valid passport or national identity card from any EU member state, even if it differs from the EU country associated with the order; in these cases, the document will be treated as local.

All identification is subject to review, and Telnyx reserves the right to request additional documentation or decline any document that cannot be verified or that does not meet applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

## Activation Timeline

Once documentation is received, it takes approximately 72 hours to validate the information and activate the number for use.
