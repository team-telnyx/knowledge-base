# Peru Number Porting

Here you will find a list of the requirements for Peru number portability. See Telnyx guidance and requirements.




## Local / National Numbers

1. LOA
2. Attorney's authorization for the representative (if the number belongs to a company)
3. ID / Passport copy
4. Company Registration certificate
5. Latest Invoice

## Download the Peru Letter of Authorization

Download the porting form [here](https://www.dropbox.com/s/0j3d2r4e5idm9o7/Peruvian_Porting_Form.pdf?dl=0).

## Porting Hours

**Standard time 00:00 AM to 4 AM Local**
