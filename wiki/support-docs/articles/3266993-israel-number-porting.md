# Israel Number Porting

Here you will find all of the requirements for Israel number portability. See Telnyx guidance and requirements.




## **Local/National/Mobile Numbers in Israel**

1. LOA
2. VAT / TAX ID
3. Latest Invoice

Please note if you are Porting a **Mobile** Number under a business, the invoice must be the underlying carrier's additionally an official proof of ownership signed by the underlying carrier will be required to successfully port the numbers.
