# What kind of equipment do you use?

Here we explain the types of equipment that Telnyx uses for our network. See Telnyx guidance and requirements.




## What kind of equipment do you use?

At Telnyx, we employ the best equipment on our networks. We use Cisco ASR routers for our core and edge, the Juniper QFX5100 for aggregation switches, and Brocade Vyatta with virtualized routing platforms for Cloud CE routing.
