# European Number Porting Requirements

*Part 2 of 3 — see also: [Part 1](european-number-porting-requirements--part-1.md), [Part 3](european-number-porting-requirements--part-3.md)*

A comprehensive reference for number porting requirements across European countries supported by Telnyx, covering required documentation, letter of authorization (LOA) templates, porting procedures, and country-specific rules.

## Ireland

**Local, National, and Toll-Free Numbers**

1. LOA
1. UAN / Account Number
1. Latest Invoice

A **UAN** (Universal Access Number) is 8 digits long and can be found on the end-user's invoice. If not listed, the end-user must contact the current provider to obtain it.

Customers porting away from EIR or Open Eir must verify with the current provider that numbers do not have special services, as these may be cancelled upon porting.

**Porting hours:** 10 AM to 3:30 PM local. Requesting a port after 3 PM is strongly discouraged, as limited escalation time is available if issues arise, potentially leaving DIDs down until the next business day.

Download the Ireland LOA [here](https://www.dropbox.com/s/9f8nesm8873og7d/IRELANDGEOPORTINGFORM-edit.pdf?dl=0).

## Italy

**Local / National Numbers**

1. LOA (local address required)
1. Local TAX / VAT ID or company registration number
1. Migration code
1. Latest Invoice
1. Copy of the Passport / ID of the Authorized Person
1. Fiscal code of the Authorized Person (alphanumeric, 16 characters)
1. Proof of local address

There must be a local address in Italian territory and a legal presence (with a valid fiscal code) to port in Italy.

**Toll-Free Numbers**

1. LOA (local address required)
1. Local TAX / VAT ID or company registration number
1. Latest Invoice
1. Copy of the Passport / ID of the Authorized Person
1. Proof of local address

**Cancellation deadlines:**

- **Standard ports:** at least 5 working days before the port date; after that, the port proceeds without possibility of fallback.
- **Mixed Operator / Complex ports:** at least 7 working days before the port date.

Outage during porting may occur.

Download the Italy LOA [here](https://assets.ctfassets.net/taysl255dolk/45LOLnr1ZO8PfWfHpAID9z/cf24bfaa49a0fc3d04900d5c0f9dfac5/LoA_TELNYX_Italy.pdf).

## Netherlands

**Local and Smart Mobile (+31 97) Numbers**

1. LOA (user's local address from the same city as the number to be ported)
1. Latest Invoice
1. Copy of Company Registration certificate (for business) / Copy of ID (for personal)
1. Proof of local address

**National Numbers**

1. LOA (national address required)
1. Latest Invoice
1. Copy of Company Registration certificate (for business) / Copy of ID (for personal)
1. Proof of local address
1. Number allocation document ("OPTA/ACM Beschikking") — applies to +3188 numbers only

**Toll-Free Numbers**

1. LOA
1. Latest Invoice
1. ID / Passport copy
1. Company Registration certificate

**Partial porting:** Not all carriers in the Netherlands agree to partial port or split a number block. If a number is part of a full range, either the full range must be ported or Telnyx can attempt a partial port. However, numbers remaining with the current provider will be down for an indeterminate period on FOC.

**Porting out of Telnyx:** The gaining provider submits a request to Telnyx. A port-out request is created in the end-user's Portal and must be authorized within **2 business days**. Once authorized, Telnyx approves the port out.

Download the Netherlands LOA [here](https://assets.ctfassets.net/taysl255dolk/6YYtHkPiDoOfhR8Vp3QxUz/3f7b77cf78ecdebe280c6c0226ac12ea/LoA_-_Telnyx_-_INTL.pdf).

## Spain

**Local, National, and Toll-Free Numbers**

1. LOA
1. CIF / NIF
1. Latest Invoice
1. Copy of authorized person's Passport / ID
1. Number Type
1. Proof of address

**Number Type classifications:**

- **Analog Geographic (AG):** A simple port of a geographic number with no associated services.
- **PBX Geographic (PBX G):** Number(s) that are part of a PBX extension.
- **Intelligent Network (IN):** VoIP number(s).

Download the Spain LOA [here](https://assets.ctfassets.net/taysl255dolk/4mfyR6jMGb8TvN0p5GQOUZ/b98742b348ba0c623ffab3d99ba3db31/TELNYX_ES_LOA.pdf).

## Sweden

**Local, National, and Toll-Free Numbers**

1. LOA (a local address is mandatory; the signature must be a wet signature)
1. Local TAX ID / VAT (must be 12 characters long)
1. Latest Invoice

Download the Sweden LOA [here](https://assets.ctfassets.net/taysl255dolk/6YYtHkPiDoOfhR8Vp3QxUz/3f7b77cf78ecdebe280c6c0226ac12ea/LoA_-_Telnyx_-_INTL.pdf).

## Switzerland

**Local / National Numbers**

1. LOA (porting wish date must be mentioned; the signature must be a wet signature; the authorized person must have full authority to act on behalf of the business)
1. Latest Invoice
1. VAT / TAX ID
1. Proof of address dated within 3 months of request (latest invoice, utility bill, or insurance)

**Toll-Free Numbers**

1. LOA (porting wish date must be mentioned)
1. Latest Invoice
1. Ownership proof from BAKOM (with reference number)

**DDI Range restriction:** A single number belonging to a DDI range cannot be ported out separately. A DDI range must include at least 10 successive numbers (or a multiple thereof), with the first number ending in 0 and the last ending in 9. Confirm these details with the current provider.

Download the Switzerland LOA [here](https://assets.ctfassets.net/taysl255dolk/6YYtHkPiDoOfhR8Vp3QxUz/3f7b77cf78ecdebe280c6c0226ac12ea/LoA_-_Telnyx_-_INTL.pdf).
