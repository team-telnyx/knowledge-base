# DID Requirements — Georgia, Greece, Malta, Montenegro, Monaco, Turkey, Israel

Consolidated Telnyx documentation of the identity and address requirements to purchase DID numbers in Georgia, Greece, Malta, Montenegro, Monaco, Turkey, and Israel, including number-type specifics, special restrictions, and typical validation timelines.

## Requirements by Country

### Georgia
- Local numbers
  - Personal identity:
    - Name, last name
    - Contact phone number
    - Contact e-mail
    - Passport or ID copy
    - Georgia registration form
  - Business identity:
    - Name, last name of an authorized representative
    - Company name
    - Contact phone number
    - Contact e-mail
    - Company registration certificate
    - Georgia registration form
  - Address verification:
    - Address matching the DID area code (street, building number, postal code, city, country)
    - Utility bill (≤3 months old)
- National numbers
  - Personal identity:
    - Name, last name
    - Contact phone number
    - Passport or ID copy
    - Georgia registration form
  - Business identity:
    - Name, last name of an authorized representative
    - Company name
    - Contact phone number
    - Company registration certificate
    - Georgia registration form
  - Address verification:
    - Address worldwide (street, building number, postal code, city, country)
- Toll-free numbers
  - Personal identity:
    - Name, last name of an authorized representative
    - Contact phone number
    - Contact e-mail
    - Passport or ID copy
    - Georgia registration form
  - Business identity:
    - Name, last name
    - Company name
    - Contact phone number
    - Contact e-mail
    - Company registration certificate
    - Georgia registration form
    - Terms & Conditions forms must be signed
  - Address verification:
    - Address worldwide (street, building number, postal code, city, country)
    - Proof of address (≤3 months old)
- Typical activation after validation: ~72 hours

### Greece
- Local numbers
  - Personal identity:
    - Name, last name
    - Contact phone number
    - Local passport or ID copy
    - VAT number
  - Business identity:
    - Name, last name of an authorized representative
    - Company name
    - Contact phone number
    - Local passport or ID copy of authorized representative
    - Local company registration certificate
    - VAT number of authorized representative
    - LOA/POA (Letter of Authorization/Power of Attorney) filled and signed (form provided by Telnyx). Download: [Telnyx Letter of Authorization Form (PDF)](https://telnyx-48416ce2297b.intercom-attachments-1.com/i/o/ltcafuzd/2270717133/194ddebc4d61ccb780b389ea1ab3/Telnyx+Letter+of+Authorization+Form.pdf)
  - Address verification:
    - Address matching the DID area code (street, building number, postal code, city, country)
    - Proof of address (≤3 months old)
- Additional requirement: End-users must be physically present in Greece when purchasing numbers from Greece

### Malta
- National numbers
  - Personal identity:
    - Name, last name
    - Contact phone number
    - Local passport or ID copy
  - Business identity:
    - Name, last name of an authorized representative
    - Company name
    - Contact phone number
    - Local company registration certificate
  - Address verification:
    - Address in Malta (street, building number, postal code, city, country)
    - Proof of address (≤3 months old)
- Toll-free numbers
  - Personal identity:
    - Name, last name
    - Contact phone number
    - Local passport or ID copy
  - Business identity:
    - Company name
    - Contact phone number
    - Local company registration certificate
  - Address verification:
    - Address in Malta (street, building number, postal code, city, country)
    - Proof of address (≤3 months old)
- Additional requirement: End-users must be physically present in Malta when purchasing numbers from Malta
- Typical activation after validation: ~72 hours

### Montenegro
- Local numbers
  - Business identity (business use only; private use not allowed):
    - Name, last name of an authorized representative
    - Company name
  - Address verification:
    - Address worldwide (street, building number, postal code, city, country)
  - Additional information:
    - Service usage description
    - Business use required; private use is not allowed
- Typical activation after validation: ~72 hours

### Monaco
- Toll-free numbers
  - Personal identity:
    - Name, last name
    - Contact phone number
    - Passport or ID copy
  - Business identity:
    - Company name
    - Contact phone number
    - Company registration certificate
  - Address verification:
    - Address worldwide (street, building number, postal code, city, country)

### Turkey
- Local and National numbers
  - Personal identity:
    - Name, last name of an authorized representative
    - Contact phone number
    - Signed copy of personal subscription agreement (Abonelik Sözleşmesi)
    - Passport or ID copy (Kimlik Fotokopisi)
  - Business identity:
    - Name, last name of an authorized representative
    - Company name
    - Contact phone number
    - Signed copy of corporate subscription agreement (Kurumsal Abonelik Sözleşmesi)
    - Circular of Signatures (İmza Sirküleri)
    - Tax Registration Certificate (Vergi Levhası)
    - Chamber of Commerce Registration Document (Faaliyet Belgesi)
    - Trade Registry Gazette (Ticari Sicil Gazetesi)
    - Passport or ID copy of an authorized representative (Yetkili Kimlik Fotokopisi)
  - Address verification:
    - Address in Turkey (street, building number, postal code, city, country)
    - Address confirmation from Turkish government portal (Adres Beyanı): https://www.turkiye.gov.tr/nvi-yerlesim-yeri-ve-diger-adres-belgesi-sorgulama

### Israel
- Mobile numbers
  - Personal identity:
    - Name, last name
    - Contact e-mail
  - Business identity:
    - Company name
    - Contact e-mail
  - Address verification:
    - Address worldwide (street, building number, postal code, city, country)
- Local and National numbers
  - Personal identity:
    - Name, last name
    - Contact phone number
  - Business identity:
    - Name, last name of the legal representative
    - Contact phone number
    - Company name
    - Business Registration Certificate
    - Business use case
  - Address verification:
    - Worldwide address (street, building number, postal code, city, country)
    - Proof of address (≤3 months old)
  - Note: You may order Local and National numbers using an address from any country; emergency services are only supported for addresses located within Israel
- Toll-free numbers
  - Personal identity:
    - Name, last name
    - Contact phone number
    - Passport or ID copy
  - Business identity:
    - Company name
    - Contact phone number
    - Company registration certificate
  - Address verification:
    - Address worldwide (street, building number, postal code, city, country)

## Processing time and document freshness
- Validation and activation typically complete in approximately 72 hours after Telnyx receives all required documentation (explicitly noted for Georgia, Malta, and Montenegro; timelines may vary by case).
- Proofs of address must generally be dated within the last 3 months where specified.
- Some countries require local presence at time of purchase (e.g., Greece, Malta). Always ensure documents meet any “local” document requirements noted above (e.g., local ID/passport, local company certificates).
