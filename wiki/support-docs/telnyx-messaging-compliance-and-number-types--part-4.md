## Forbidden Messaging Use Cases in the US and Canada

Mobile carriers and industry guidelines (including CTIA standards) restrict certain messaging traffic across the United States and Canada. These restrictions apply to 10DLC (long code), toll-free numbers, and short codes. Sending prohibited messages can lead to carrier filtering, increased error rates, or suspension of messaging capabilities.

### Illegal Products and Substances

Messages promoting or facilitating the sale of illegal goods are not allowed, including:

- Controlled or prescription drugs without proper authorization
- Substances not legally approved for sale
- Unregulated or prohibited supplements

These restrictions apply regardless of business licensing status.

### Gambling and Betting

Generally not supported, including:

- Online casinos or gambling platforms
- Sports betting or wagering services
- Betting tips or odds promotion

Some exceptions may exist for approved short code programs with prior carrier approval.

### SHAFT Categories

Carriers commonly restrict messaging tied to:

- **Sex**: Adult content or services
- **Hate**: Hate speech or discriminatory content
- **Alcohol**: Subject to regional and carrier-specific rules
- **Firearms**: Weapons and related products
- **Tobacco**: Including vape and e-cigarette products

Firearms, tobacco, and vaping messaging is not permitted across standard messaging channels. Alcohol-related messaging may be allowed in certain US cases with appropriate age verification and compliance controls, but policies vary by carrier.

### Age-Restricted Content (Canada)

Canadian carriers enforce stricter rules for age-sensitive content. Content intended for restricted audiences must not be sent without explicit approval; additional registration or exemptions may be required.

### Deceptive or High-Risk Content

Prohibited content includes:

- Phishing or impersonation attempts
- Misleading financial offers or "guaranteed" returns
- Lead generation practices that obscure intent
- Any content designed to manipulate or deceive recipients

### Restricted Business Models

Certain business types are not supported for messaging, even if individual messages appear compliant:

- Businesses operating primarily in prohibited verticals (e.g., gambling or adult services)
- Use cases attempting to evade carrier or regulatory safeguards

Carriers evaluate both message content and overall use case when determining compliance.

### Links and URLs

- Avoid generic or public URL shorteners
- Use branded, recognizable domains to improve trust and reduce filtering risk

### Consent and Opt-In

- All recipients must provide clear and verifiable consent before receiving messages
- Messaging must align with registered use cases (e.g., A2P 10DLC campaigns)

### Carrier Enforcement

Carriers may take action based on:

- Message content and classification
- End-user complaints or opt-out rates
- Sending patterns and traffic anomalies

Enforcement can occur even if content is not explicitly listed.

### Best Practices

- Register messaging campaigns where required (e.g., 10DLC)
- Clearly identify your brand in each message
- Provide transparent opt-in and opt-out mechanisms
- Use compliant language and avoid misleading claims
- Send only relevant, expected communications to opted-in users

## Country-Specific SMS Guidelines

### Norway

- **MCC:** 242
- **Dial Code:** 47
- Alphanumeric Sender ID registration is required; messages from unregistered Sender IDs will be rejected.
- To register, provide the following along with a copy of Business Registration to alpha_sender_id@telnyx.com:
  1. SenderID to be registered
  2. Message/content type
  3. Message/content example
  4. Company name (and brand name if different)
  5. Website of brand or company
  6. Company country of origin
  7. Expected volumes per month
  8. Email linked to your Telnyx account
- Companies must have a valid business case for the requested Alphanumeric Sender ID.
- Consent (proof of opt-in) should be obtained before sending communications.
- Traffic should include clear opt-out options.
- Messages containing URLs are filtered by local operators; valid URLs can be whitelisted.
- Lottery and gambling traffic is not allowed and will be blocked.

### Belize

- **MCC:** 702
- **Dial Code:** 501
- Alphanumeric Sender ID registration is required; messages from unregistered Sender IDs will be rejected.
- Same registration requirements as Norway (SenderID, content type, example, company name, website, country of origin, expected volumes, Telnyx account email).
- Consent (proof of opt-in) should be obtained before sending communications.
- Traffic should include clear opt-out options.

### Palestinian Territory

- **MCC:** 425
- **Dial Code:** 970
- Alphanumeric Sender ID registration is required; messages from unregistered Sender IDs will be rejected.
- Same registration requirements as Norway.
- Consent (proof of opt-in) should be obtained before sending communications.
- Traffic should include clear opt-out options.
- Personal loans, gambling, adult, and cryptocurrency-related traffic is prohibited.

For all country-specific guidelines, refer to the [Acceptable Use Policy for Messaging](acceptable-use-policy-for-messaging.md) and contact alpha_sender_id@telnyx.com for Alpha Sender ID registration questions.
