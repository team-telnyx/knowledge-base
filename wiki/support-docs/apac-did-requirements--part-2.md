# APAC DID Requirements

*Part 2 of 2 — see also: [Part 1](apac-did-requirements--part-1.md)*

Documentation and verification needed to purchase and activate Telnyx DID numbers across Asia-Pacific countries, organized by country and number type, with identity, address, and special compliance notes.

## Kazakhstan

### Local
Personal identity verification:
- Name and last name
- Contact phone number
- LOI (Telnyx-provided template)

Business identity verification:
- Authorized representative’s name and last name
- Company name
- Contact phone number
- LOI (Telnyx-provided template)

Address verification:
- Worldwide address
- Proof of address (≤3 months)

### Mobile
Definitions:
- Purchaser: the individual who pays for the service (or accepts an invitation to use it free of charge from a CSP or its authorized party)
- Service Activator: the person requesting activation of the prepaid mobile carriage service from the CSP

For Purchaser — Information:
- Name of the purchaser
- Number of other activated prepaid mobile carriage services supplied to the purchaser

For Purchaser — Documentation:
- Local ID verification

For Service Activator — Information:
- Personal: name and date of birth
- Business: name and date of birth, company name

For Service Activator — Documentation:
- Local ID verification

Address verification:
- Address in Australia
- Utility bill (≤3 months)

Notes:
- Activation typically ~72 hours after documentation approval

## Key APAC notes
- LOI requirements are common (China shared cost; Malaysia national/mobile; Vietnam local business; Bangladesh; Pakistan; Kazakhstan; Uzbekistan; Indonesia local). Use the provided Telnyx/NumberOps templates where specified.
- Many markets require business use only for certain number types (e.g., China local/toll-free/shared cost; Hong Kong toll-free; Malaysia local/national/mobile; Thailand local/mobile/toll-free; Bangladesh national/mobile).
- Address scope varies by country and number type (local country address vs worldwide). Check each section carefully.
- Where shown, Telnyx typically activates numbers ~72 hours after successful document validation.
