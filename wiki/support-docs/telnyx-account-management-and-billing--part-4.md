## Reselling Telnyx Services

Reselling is permitted. The Mission Control platform supports multi-tenant environments with traffic segregation, tagging, and real-time reporting. All portal functionality is available via the [Telnyx API](https://developers.telnyx.com) for building custom provisioning platforms. For resellers needing to give customers direct access, use the [Managed Accounts](managed-accounts.md) feature instead of Organizations.
