# Africa DID Requirements

*Part 1 of 2 — see also: [Part 2](africa-did-requirements--part-2.md)*

A consolidated guide to the identity, address, and letter-of-intent (LOI) documentation needed to purchase Telnyx DIDs across select African countries, including any business-use restrictions and address constraints by number type.

## How to use this guide
Below you’ll find country-by-country requirements for purchasing Telnyx DIDs (local, national, mobile, toll-free where available). Each section lists identity, address, and any LOI or special notes exactly as specified in Telnyx Help Center articles.

## What to prepare (common documents)
- Personal identity: Name and last name are typical; some countries also require a contact phone, email, and a passport/ID copy.
- Business identity: Authorized representative’s name (where specified), company name, contact phone/email, and company registration/incorporation documents.
- Address: Either worldwide, country-specific, or area-code-matching; proof of address is often required and must be dated within 3 months when stated.
- LOI: Some markets require a signed Letter of Intent; in certain cases it must be dated within 1 month. A Telnyx LOI template is provided in some articles (example: LOI Template.docx available via support resources).

## Country: Algeria
Number type offered: National
- End-user name: Company name (business use) or surname (private use) must be provided when ordering.
- Business identity: Copy of ID of an authorized representative; copy of company registration; name, telephone number, and email address of the legal representative.

## Country: Angola
Number type offered: Local
- Personal identity: Name, last name; contact phone; passport or ID copy.
- Business identity: Company name; contact phone; company registration certificate.
- Address: Worldwide address (street, building number, postal code, city, country).

## Country: Benin
Number type offered: Mobile
- Personal identity: Name, last name; contact phone; contact email; passport or ID copy.
- Business identity: Authorized representative’s name and last name; company name; contact phone; contact email; company registration certificate.
- Address: Worldwide address; proof of address (dated within 3 months).
- Timeline: Validation and activation typically take ~72 hours after documents are received.

## Country: South Africa
Number types offered: Local, National, Mobile, Toll-Free
- Local — Personal identity: Name, last name; contact phone; passport or South African ID copy.
- Local — Business identity: Authorized representative’s name and last name; contact phone; passport or South African ID copy of the authorized representative; company name; company registration certificate.
- Local — Address: Must match the DID area code; proof of address (dated within 3 months).
- National — Personal identity: Name, last name; contact phone; passport or South African ID copy.
- National — Business identity: Authorized representative’s name and last name; contact phone; passport or South African ID copy of the authorized representative; company name; company registration certificate.
- National — Address: Within South Africa; proof of address (dated within 3 months).
- Mobile — Personal identity: Name, last name; contact phone; passport or ID copy.
- Mobile — Business identity: Authorized representative’s name and last name; company name; contact phone; company registration certificate.
- Mobile — Address: Worldwide address; proof of address (dated within 3 months).
- Toll-Free — Personal identity: Name, last name; contact phone; passport or South African ID copy; signed LOI (dated within 1 month).
- Toll-Free — Business identity: Authorized representative’s name and last name; contact phone; passport or South African ID copy of the authorized representative; company name; company registration certificate; signed LOI (dated within 1 month).
- Toll-Free — Address: Worldwide address; proof of address (dated within 3 months).
- Timeline: Validation and activation typically take ~72 hours after documents are received.
