# Telnyx DID Numbers

*Part 1 of 3 — see also: [Part 2](telnyx-did-numbers--part-2.md), [Part 3](telnyx-did-numbers--part-3.md)*

This page covers Telnyx's DID (Direct Inward Dial) number offerings, including domestic long-code, toll-free, and international number types, along with guidance on purchasing, requesting unavailable numbers, and finding GB numbers on the portal. It also explains how to bulk edit number settings (voice, tags, emergency services, messaging profile, and deletion) in Mission Control, and provides reference tables for US N11 codes, international toll-free prefixes, and supported emergency numbers by country, including dialing rules and address registration requirements.

## What is a DID?

A Direct Inward Dial number (DID) is a virtual number that functions as a regular phone number; however, it is not connected to any POTS line (landline). Once you have completed your account configuration through the Telnyx portal, your DID becomes the phone number at which anyone around the world can reach you.

## Types of DID Numbers Offered

Telnyx offers domestic DIDs in the United States and Canada, toll-free DIDs, and international DIDs. To purchase a DID, start by checking the [Numbers Pricing](https://telnyx.com/pricing/numbers) page, then sign up for an account and start acquiring DID numbers instantly.

### Domestic Long-Code

These are typical local numbers required for a local business. Telnyx has coverage in over 20,000 rate centers across the United States and Canada. Domestic long codes support instant provisioning for fax, voice, SMS, and MMS. The domestic coverage map is available on the Telnyx website.

### Domestic Toll-Free

Telnyx is a managing Responsible Organization (RespOrg QIT01) and can acquire, manage, and route toll-free calls to a business, as well as control the ability to quickly reroute traffic for potential service impairments with service providers.

#### Toll-Free Voice

Telnyx allows users to purchase pre-provisioned voice toll-free numbers with routing already set for instant use. Select **quickship** on the number search user interface to instantly receive numbers ready for voice calling. If quickship is not selected during the toll-free search, routing provisioning can take up to one hour to become active after order completion. Quickship only applies to the voice portion of toll-free numbers so they can be instantly provisioned for receiving inbound calls.

#### Toll-Free Messaging

Telnyx supports messaging on toll-free numbers to support large campaigns. Both toll-free SMS and MMS are available for the USA and Canada. The messaging portion is not instantly provisioned, and it is recommended to submit a use case first to avoid spam blocks from non-verified toll-free numbers.

### International Number Types

Telnyx's international coverage continues to grow, offering local, national, toll-free, shared cost, and mobile numbers from select countries across the world. When ordering international numbers, Telnyx may require documentation to comply with local regulators. If documentation is required, the user interface will make this clear at checkout. After ordering, the user is brought to the number orders page where documentation can be uploaded. An email is also sent with a time-frame for providing documentation; if not provided within 10 days, the numbers may expire and be returned to inventory.

## Purchasing DIDs in the US and Canada

Telnyx has access to over 15,000 rate centers across the US and Canada, allowing DIDs to be purchased from all over both countries. To purchase, check the [Numbers Pricing](https://telnyx.com/pricing/numbers) page, create a Telnyx account, and start searching for numbers.

## Finding GB Numbers on the Telnyx Portal

GB local, national, and toll-free numbers are not SMS supported; only mobile numbers support SMS. To find GB numbers on the Telnyx portal, use the following search queries:

- **GB National numbers:** Search in the local numbers section.
- **GB Toll-free numbers:** Search in the toll-free numbers section.
- **SMS capable GB Mobile numbers:** Search in the mobile numbers section.
- **GB Local numbers:** Enter the region or city name in the region search to find numbers for a specific city.

If issues persist, contact the numbering team at [numbering@telnyx.com](mailto:numbering@telnyx.com) or open a numbering request ticket on the portal.
