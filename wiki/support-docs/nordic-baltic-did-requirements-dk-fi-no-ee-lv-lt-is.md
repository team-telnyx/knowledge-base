# Nordic & Baltic DID Requirements (DK, FI, NO, EE, LV, LT, IS)

Consolidated Telnyx documentation on regulatory and documentation requirements to acquire DIDs in Denmark, Finland, Norway, Estonia, Latvia, Lithuania, and Iceland—organized by country and number type, with identity, address, use restrictions, and processing notes.

## Ordering and Requirement Groups (DK, NO)
- Starting September 16, 2024, Requirement Groups are mandatory for ordering phone numbers in Denmark and Norway. They allow you to pre-fill regulatory information once and reuse it across orders. See the external setup guide: Requirement Groups for ordering phone numbers (https://support.telnyx.com/en/articles/9801714-requirement-groups-for-ordering-phone-numbers).

## Denmark (DK)
### Local Numbers
- Business identity (required):
  - Authorized representative’s name and last name
  - Contact phone number and contact email
  - Company name
  - Business registration certificate and Business Registration Number
- Address verification:
  - Address in Denmark (street, building number, postal code, city, country)
  - Proof of address dated within 3 months
- Additional: Business use required; private use not allowed.

### Mobile Numbers
- Personal identity: name, last name, contact phone number
- Or business identity: authorized representative’s name and last name, company name, contact phone number
- Address verification: Address worldwide
- Additional: Service usage description required.

### Toll-Free Numbers
- Personal identity: name, last name, contact phone number
- Or business identity: authorized representative’s name and last name, company name, contact phone number
- Address verification: Address worldwide

## Finland (FI)
### Local Numbers
- Personal identity: name, last name, contact phone number, passport or ID copy
- Or business identity: authorized representative’s name and last name, company name, contact phone number, company registration certificate
- Address verification: Address worldwide

### National Numbers
- Personal identity: name, last name, contact phone number, passport or ID copy
- Or business identity: authorized representative’s name and last name, company name, contact phone number, company registration certificate
- Address verification: Address worldwide

### Mobile Numbers
- Business identity (required): authorized representative’s name and last name, contact phone number, VAT number, company name
- Address verification: Address worldwide
- Additional: Business use required; private use not allowed.

### Toll-Free Numbers
- Business identity (required): authorized representative’s name and last name, contact phone number, company name, VAT number, company incorporation certificate copy
- Address verification: Address worldwide
- Additional: Business use required; private use not allowed.

## Norway (NO)
### Local Numbers
- Business identity (required):
  - Authorized representative’s name and last name
  - Contact phone number and contact email
  - Company name
  - Passport or ID copy of authorized representative
  - Business registration certificate and Business Registration Number
- Address verification:
  - Address in Norway
  - Proof of address dated within 3 months
- Additional: Business use required; private use not allowed.

### Toll-Free Numbers
- Personal identity: name, last name, date of birth, contact phone number, local passport or ID copy
- Or business identity: authorized representative’s name and last name, company name, company ID, contact phone number, local company registration certificate
- Address verification:
  - Address in Norway
  - Proof of address dated within 3 months
- Additional: End-users must be physically present in the country when purchasing numbers from that country.

## Estonia (EE)
### Local Numbers
- Personal identity: name, last name, contact phone number, copy of ID or passport
- Or business identity: legal representative’s name and last name, contact phone number, company name, copy of legal representative’s ID or passport, copy of company registration
- Address verification:
  - Address in Estonia
  - Utility bill (no older than 3 months)

### Toll-Free Numbers
- Personal identity: name, last name, contact phone number, passport or ID copy
- Or business identity: company name, contact phone number, company registration certificate
- Address verification: Address worldwide

## Latvia (LV)
### Local Numbers
- Business identity (required): authorized representative’s name and last name, contact phone number, company name, copy of authorized representative’s ID or passport, company registration certificate
- Address verification:
  - Address in Latvia
  - Proof of address dated within 3 months
- Additional: Business use required; private use not allowed.

### Toll-Free Numbers
- Personal identity: name, last name, contact phone number
- Or business identity: authorized representative’s name and last name, company name, contact phone number
- Address verification: Address in Latvia (street, building number, postal code, city, country)

## Lithuania (LT)
### Local & National Numbers
- Personal identity: name, last name, contact phone number, passport or ID copy
- Or business identity: authorized representative’s name and last name, company name, contact phone number, passport or ID copy of authorized representative, company registration certificate
- Address verification:
  - Address in Lithuania
  - Proof of address dated within 3 months

### Mobile Numbers
- Personal identity: name, last name, contact phone number, passport or ID copy
- Or business identity: authorized representative’s name and last name, company name, contact phone number, company registration certificate
- Address verification: Address worldwide

### Toll-Free Numbers
- Personal identity: name, last name, contact phone number, passport or ID copy
- Or business identity: authorized representative’s name and last name, company name, contact phone number, passport or ID copy of authorized representative, company registration certificate
- Address verification:
  - Address in Lithuania
  - Proof of address dated within 3 months

## Iceland (IS)
### Local Numbers
- Personal identity: name, last name
- Or business identity: authorized representative’s name and last name, company name
- Address verification: Address worldwide

### Toll-Free Numbers
- Personal identity: name, last name, contact phone number
- Or business identity: authorized representative’s name and last name, company name, contact phone number
- Address verification: Address worldwide

## Validation timelines
- Estonia: approximately 72 hours after documentation is received to validate and activate numbers.
- Lithuania: approximately 72 hours after documentation is received to validate and activate numbers.
- Iceland: approximately 72 hours after documentation is received to validate and activate numbers.

## Use and presence restrictions (summary)
- Business use required:
  - Denmark Local; Norway Local; Latvia Local; Finland Mobile and Toll-Free.
- In-country address required (by type):
  - Denmark Local; Norway Local and Toll-Free; Estonia Local; Latvia Local and Toll-Free; Lithuania Local, National, and Toll-Free.
- Worldwide address accepted (by type):
  - Denmark Mobile and Toll-Free; Finland Local, National, Mobile, Toll-Free; Estonia Toll-Free; Lithuania Mobile; Iceland Local and Toll-Free.
- Additional presence rule: Norway requires end-users to be physically present in-country when purchasing numbers from that country (applies to Toll-Free as documented).
