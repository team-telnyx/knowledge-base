# DID Requirements — Central, Eastern & Southeastern Europe

*Part 1 of 3 — see also: [Part 2](did-requirements-central-eastern-southeastern-europe--part-2.md), [Part 3](did-requirements-central-eastern-southeastern-europe--part-3.md)*

A consolidated guide to Telnyx KYC and address requirements for purchasing DID numbers across Albania, Bosnia & Herzegovina, Bulgaria, Croatia, Cyprus, Czech Republic, Hungary, North Macedonia, Poland, Romania, Russia, Serbia, Slovakia, Slovenia, and Ukraine. Requirements are organized by number type (Local, National, Mobile, Toll‑Free) with country‑specific rules, document freshness, and special constraints.

## Scope and covered countries
This guide synthesizes Telnyx DID number purchase requirements for: Albania, Bosnia & Herzegovina, Bulgaria, Croatia, Cyprus, Czech Republic, Hungary, North Macedonia, Poland, Romania, Russia, Serbia, Slovakia, Slovenia, and Ukraine.

## What Telnyx validates
- Personal identity (when allowed): name, surname, contact phone, and a valid Passport/ID (often local for some countries/number types).
- Business identity (when required/used): authorized representative’s name and ID/passport, company name, and company registration/incorporation certificate (often local for some countries/number types).
- Address verification: varies by number type and country:
  - Geographic numbers commonly require an address matching the DID area code.
  - Some types require an in‑country address; others accept a worldwide address.
  - Proof of Address (POA) dating typical: within 3 months; some cases allow a utility bill less than 6 months old (explicitly noted below).
- Special constraints (country/type‑specific):
  - Business‑use only (no private use) in certain markets/types.
  - End‑user physical presence in country for some markets/types.
  - Service usage description, VAT numbers, local national IDs (e.g., PESEL/NIP/REGON/KRS in Poland), and/or a signed Letter of Intent (LOI) with recent date.

## Submission and activation timeline
- After Telnyx receives complete documentation, validation and activation typically take about 72 hours (where noted by the country’s regulator/underlying carrier).
