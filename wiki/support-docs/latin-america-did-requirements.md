# Latin America DID Requirements

Requirements for purchasing DID numbers in Latin American countries through Telnyx, including identity and address verification documents needed for each number type in Brazil, Chile, Colombia, Mexico, and Uruguay.

This page covers the documentation requirements to acquire DID numbers in select Latin American countries. Requirements vary by country and number type, and generally fall into three categories: personal identity verification, business identity verification, and address verification.

## Brazil

### Local and Mobile Numbers

**Personal identity verification:**
- Name, last name
- Contact phone number
- CPF number
- Passport or ID copy

**Business identity verification:**
- Name, last name of an authorized representative
- Company name
- Contact phone number
- Cartão CNPJ (Comprovante de Inscrição e Situação Cadastral)

**Address verification:**
- Address in Brazil (street, building number, postal code, city, and country)
- Proof of address (dated within 3 months)

### Toll-Free and National (3003/4004) Numbers

**Personal identity verification:**
- Name, last name
- Contact phone number
- Contact e-mail
- CPF number
- Passport or ID copy

**Business identity verification:**
- Name, last name of an authorized representative
- Company name
- Contact phone number
- Contact e-mail
- CNPJ number
- Power of Attorney
- Passport or ID copy of an authorized representative
- Company Registration Certificate

**Address verification:**
- Address in Brazil (street, building number, postal code, city, and country)

> It can take up to 3 business days to review and approve Brazil orders.

**Personal identity verification:**
- Name, last name
- Contact phone number
- Signed LOI (dated within 1 month)

**Business identity verification:**
- Name, last name of an authorized representative
- Company name

**Address verification:**
- Address worldwide (street, building number, postal code, city, and country)
- Proof of address (dated within 3 months)

> Once documentation is received, it takes approximately 72 hours to validate the information and activate the number.

## Key Differences by Country

- **Brazil** requires a local Brazilian address for all number types, while most other countries accept a worldwide address.
- **Brazil** uniquely requires a CPF number for personal identity and a CNPJ for business identity.
- **Uruguay** requires a signed Letter of Intent (LOI) dated within 1 month.
- **Chile** toll-free numbers are restricted to business use only.
- **Mexico** and **Uruguay** combine local and toll-free requirements under a single category.
- **Mexico** mobile numbers have the simplest requirements — only name and company name.
