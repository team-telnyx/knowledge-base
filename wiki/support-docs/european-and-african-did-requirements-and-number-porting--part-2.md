# European and African DID Requirements and Number Porting

*Part 2 of 3 — see also: [Part 1](european-and-african-did-requirements-and-number-porting--part-1.md), [Part 3](european-and-african-did-requirements-and-number-porting--part-3.md)*

This page consolidates Telnyx documentation covering DID (Direct Inward Dialing) number requirements and number porting procedures for several European and African countries, including Italy, Sweden, Switzerland, Denmark, Norway, Portugal, Mozambique, and Angola. It also explains the Requirement Groups feature, which became mandatory for ordering numbers in six European countries on September 16, 2024.

## Switzerland DID Requirements

Switzerland requires the use of Requirement Groups for all number orders.

### Local Numbers in Switzerland

**Business identity verification:**
- Name, last name of an authorized representative
- Contact phone number
- Contact e-mail
- Company name
- Passport or ID copy of an authorized representative
- Business registration certificate
- Business registration number

**Address verification:**
- Address matching the DID area code (street, building number, postal code, city, country)
- Proof of address (dated within 3 months)

**Additional details:**
- Business use is required; private use is not allowed.

### Toll-Free Numbers in Switzerland

**Business identity verification:**
- Name, last name of an authorized representative
- Contact phone number
- Company name
- VAT number
- Company registration certificate (dated within 3 months)

**Address verification:**
- Address worldwide (street, building number, postal code, city, country)

**Additional details:**
- Business use is required; private use is not allowed.
- Once documentation is received, validation and number activation take approximately 72 hours.

## Identity Verification: What "Local" Means

For most orders, proof of identity must be issued by the country in which you are making your purchase. As an exception, customers within the European Union may use a valid passport or national identity card from any EU member state, even if it differs from the EU country associated with the order; in these cases, the document will be treated as local.

All identification is subject to review, and Telnyx reserves the right to request additional documentation or decline any document that cannot be verified or that does not meet applicable legal and regulatory requirements.
