# Asia-Pacific DID Requirements

*Part 3 of 3 — see also: [Part 1](asia-pacific-did-requirements--part-1.md), [Part 2](asia-pacific-did-requirements--part-2.md)*

Requirements for purchasing Telnyx DID numbers across Asia-Pacific countries, covering identity, business, and address verification documents needed for local, national, toll-free, mobile, and shared cost number types.

## Vietnam

### Local Numbers

**Personal identity verification:**
- Name, last name
- Contact phone number
- Passport or ID copy
- Utility bill (not older than 3 months)

**Business identity verification:**
- Name, last name of an authorized representative
- Contact phone number
- Contact e-mail
- Company name
- Passport or ID copy of an authorized representative
- Company registration certificate
- Signed LOI (dated within 1 month)
- Utility bill (not older than 3 months)

**Address verification:**
- Address worldwide (street, building number, postal code, city, and country)

### Toll-Free Numbers

**Personal identity verification:**
- Name, last name
- Contact e-mail

**Business identity verification:**
- Company name
- Contact e-mail

**Address verification:**
- Address worldwide (street, building number, postal code, city, and country)

Calling card usage is not permitted for Vietnam toll-free numbers. Documentation validation and number activation takes approximately 72 hours.

## Common Requirements Across Countries

Several patterns appear consistently across Asia-Pacific DID requirements:

- **Proof of address** must typically be dated within 3 months (utility bills, tax receipts, etc.).
- **Letters of Intent (LOI)** are required for several countries (China shared cost, Malaysia national and mobile, Pakistan, Uzbekistan, Vietnam) and must often be signed within 1 month of submission. Telnyx or NumberOps provides the LOI template.
- **Business-only use** is mandatory for China local and shared cost, Hong Kong national and toll-free, Malaysia local, national, and mobile, and Thailand local, mobile, and toll-free numbers. Private use is not allowed for these number types.
- **Passport or ID copies** of an authorized representative are commonly required for business identity verification (China, Georgia, Indonesia, Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand, Vietnam).
- **Company registration certificates** are frequently required for business identity verification (Georgia, Hong Kong, Malaysia, New Zealand toll-free, Singapore, Thailand, Vietnam).
- **Service usage descriptions** are required for China shared cost, Hong Kong toll-free, Singapore local, and Thailand national numbers.
- **Activation time** after documentation submission is approximately 72 hours for Georgia, Hong Kong, Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand, and Vietnam numbers.

For further details on number features and coverage, see [DID Number Feature Availability by Country](did-number-feature-availability-by-country.md) and [DID Number Pricing](did-number-pricing.md).
