# Gulf (Saudi Arabia, UAE, Kuwait, Oman, Qatar) DID Requirements

A consolidated guide to the documents, verification steps, and special rules needed to purchase and use Telnyx phone numbers across five Gulf countries: Saudi Arabia, United Arab Emirates, Kuwait, Oman, and Qatar.

## What’s required at a glance
- Business use only: Personal/private use is not allowed in all cases where explicitly stated (Saudi Arabia, UAE, Kuwait). Assume business use for others unless noted.
- Identity and address checks: Expect company and authorized representative details; some countries require local addresses, proof of address, or specific national IDs.
- LOI and service agreements: Letter of Intent (LOI) and/or a Service Acceptance/Service Agreement form are commonly required and often must be recently dated (within 1 month).
- Traffic forecast and use case: Most requests require a traffic forecast; some ask for a brief service usage description.
- Minimum service period: A 1-year minimum applies in Saudi Arabia (local and toll-free) and the UAE (local and toll-free). Early termination is not permitted within this period.

## Saudi Arabia
### Local numbers (business identity)
- Authorized representative: Full name and contact phone number
- Company details: Company name
- Documents: Passport or ID copy of an authorized representative; company registration certificate; trade license (Saudi companies only)
- Forecast and agreements: Traffic forecast; Signed Service Agreement Form (provided by Telnyx/NumberOps)

### Local numbers (address verification)
- Saudi address: Street, building number, postal code, city, country
- Proof of address: Dated within 3 months

### Toll-free numbers (business identity)
- Authorized representative: Full name, contact phone number
- Company details: Company name; company registration certificate; trade license (Saudi companies only)
- Documents and forms: Passport or ID copy of an authorized representative; Signed LOI (dated within 1 month; template provided by Telnyx/NumberOps); Signed Service Agreement Form (provided by Telnyx/NumberOps)
- Forecast: Traffic forecast

### Toll-free numbers (address verification)
- Worldwide address accepted: Street, building number, postal code, city, country
- Proof of address: Dated within 3 months

### Additional notes (Saudi Arabia)
- Saudi companies: The representative’s passport/ID must be issued by Saudi Arabia.
- Business use required; private use not allowed.
- Minimum service period: 1 year (early termination not permitted; all fees due for the period).

## United Arab Emirates
### Local numbers (business identity)
- Company owner: Full name and contact phone number
- Company details: Company name; certificate of registration
- Documents: Copy of ID or passport of the company owner
- Usage details: Business use case; traffic forecast

### Local numbers (address verification)
- UAE address: Street, building number, postal code, city, country

### Toll-free numbers (business identity)
- Authorized representative: Full name, contact phone number, contact email
- Company details: Company name; company registration certificate (must be a non‑UAE entity)
- Documents and forms: Recently signed LOI (signed within 1 month; template provided by Telnyx)
- Forecast: Traffic forecast

### Toll-free numbers (address verification)
- UAE address: Street, building number, postal code, city, country

### Additional notes (UAE)
- Business use required; private use not allowed.
- Compliance review: Numbers are subject to Telnyx Compliance approval and may be declined at discretion.
- Toll-free routing: Calls cannot land in or be forwarded to destinations within the UAE.
- Toll-free usage: Subject to disconnection if no traffic for 6 consecutive months.
- Toll-free end-user: Must not be based in the UAE.
- Minimum service period: 1 year (early termination not permitted; all fees due for the period).

## Kuwait
### Local numbers (business identity)
- Authorized representative: Full name and contact phone number
- Company details: Company name and website
- Documents: Kuwaiti ID copy of an authorized representative; local company registration certificate
- Forecast and forms: Traffic forecast; signed LOI (dated within 1 month; Telnyx-provided); signed Service Acceptance/Service Agreement form (Telnyx-provided)

### Additional notes (Kuwait)
- Provider approval: Every order is subject to local provider approval.
- Business use required; private use not allowed.
- Processing time: Approximately 72 hours after Telnyx receives complete documentation.

## Oman
### Local numbers (business identity)
- Authorized representative: Full name; contact phone number
- Company details: Company name; local business registration certificate
- Forms: LOI (Telnyx-provided)

### Local numbers (address verification)
- Oman address: Street, building number, postal code, city, country
- Proof of address: Dated within 3 months

### Toll-free numbers (business identity)
- Authorized representative: Full name; contact phone number
- Company details and documents: Company name; passport or ID copy; establishment card; latest trade license
- Forms: LOI (Telnyx-provided)

### Toll-free numbers (address verification)
- National address: Street, building number, postal code, city, country

### Additional notes (Oman)
- Provide a brief service usage description.
- Include a traffic forecast.

## Qatar
### Toll-free numbers
- Identity verification (personal and business): Recently signed LOI (dated within the month)
- Processing time: Approximately 72 hours after Telnyx receives complete documentation

## Submission tips and timelines
- Date sensitivity: LOIs typically must be signed within the last month; proofs of address (where required) must be dated within the last 3 months.
- Completeness matters: Missing items (IDs, registrations, trade licenses, traffic forecasts, addresses) delay review.
- Processing expectations: Where stated, allow ~72 hours from complete submission; complex cases or compliance reviews (e.g., UAE) may take longer.
