# APAC DID Requirements

*Part 1 of 2 — see also: [Part 2](apac-did-requirements--part-2.md)*

Documentation and verification needed to purchase and activate Telnyx DID numbers across Asia-Pacific countries, organized by country and number type, with identity, address, and special compliance notes.

## China

### Local and Toll-free
Business identity verification:
- Authorized representative’s name and last name
- Company name
- Authorized representative’s contact phone number
- Copy of the authorized representative’s Chinese ID card with company stamp
- Photo of the authorized representative holding the ID card
- Chinese Business License with company stamp (must contain “副本”)

Additional documents (provided by NumberOps):
- Signed and stamped Customer Liability Authorization Letter
- Signed and stamped Network and Information Security Commitment Letter
- Signed and stamped Scenario & Script Declaration (include at least 3 outbound script examples; if no outbound, include inbound script)

Notes:
- Business use required; private use not allowed
- Only a Chinese ID card for the representative is acceptable (no passport/driver’s license)

### Shared Cost
Business identity verification:
- Authorized representative’s name and last name
- Authorized representative’s contact phone number
- Passport or ID copy of the authorized representative
- Photo of the authorized representative holding their passport/ID
- Company name
- Company incorporation certificate copy
- LOI signed within 1 month (provided by NumberOps)

Address verification:
- Worldwide address (street, building number, postal code, city, country)
- Utility bill (≤3 months)

Additional:
- Service usage description
- Business use required; private use not allowed
