# SMS Guidelines for Eastern Europe, Caucasus, and Surrounding Regions

This page provides SMS delivery guidelines for countries in Eastern Europe, the Caucasus, and surrounding regions, detailing Alphanumeric Sender ID support, registration processes, content restrictions, and compliance recommendations based on Telnyx documentation.

## Armenia
- **MCC:** 374
- **Dial Code:** 283
- **Alphanumeric Sender IDs:** Supported with registration. Registration is required; without it, Alpha Sender IDs will be overwritten to a generic Alpha Sender ID or rejected.
- **Registration:** Contact [alpha_sender_id@telnyx.com](mailto:alpha_sender_id@telnyx.com) for registration details.
- **Compliance:** Refer to the [Acceptable Use Policy for Messaging](https://support.telnyx.com/en/articles/1310359-acceptable-use-policy-for-messaging).

## Azerbaijan
- **MCC:** 400
- **Dial Code:** 994
- **Alphanumeric Sender IDs:** Supported with registration. Registration is required.
- **Registration:** Provide the following details and a copy of your Business Registration to [alpha_sender_id@telnyx.com](mailto:alpha_sender_id@telnyx.com):
  1. Sender ID to be registered
  2. Message/Content type
  3. Message/Content example
  4. Company name (and Brand name if different)
  5. Website of brand or company
  6. Company Country of origin
  7. Expected volumes per month
  8. Email linked to your Telnyx account
  
  Companies must have a valid business case; if the relationship between the company/brand and the Sender ID is unclear, provide additional supporting documentation.
- **Best Practices:** Obtain consent (Proof of Opt-in) before sending marketing SMS and include clear Opt-Out options.
- **Compliance:** Refer to the [Acceptable Use Policy for Messaging](https://support.telnyx.com/en/articles/1310359-acceptable-use-policy-for-messaging).

## Russian Federation
- **MCC:** 250
- **Dial Code:** 7
- **Alphanumeric Sender IDs:** Supported with registration. Registration is required; without it, Alpha Senders will be overwritten to Generic Alpha Sender IDs or will not deliver.
- **Registration:** Provide the following details and a copy of your Business Registration to [alpha_sender_id@telnyx.com](mailto:alpha_sender_id@telnyx.com):
  1. Sender ID to be registered
  2. Message/Content Type (Transactional/Promotional/Notification)
  3. Message/Content example(s)
  4. Company name (and Brand name if different)
  5. Website of brand or company
  6. Company Country of origin
  7. Expected volumes per month
  8. Email linked to your Telnyx account
- **Costs:** There is a monthly recurring cost of $250 for every Sender ID registered.
- **Compliance:** Refer to the [Acceptable Use Policy for Messaging](https://support.telnyx.com/en/articles/1310359-acceptable-use-policy-for-messaging).

## Turkey
- **MCC:** 286
- **Dial Code:** 90
- **Alphanumeric Sender IDs:** Supported with registration. Registration is required.
- **Restrictions:** A 4-character code beginning with the letter "B" will be added at the end of the message content by the terminating operator in line with the guidelines implemented by the Information and Communication Technologies Authority. Gambling traffic is not allowed.
- **Registration:** Submit the required registration form and the following details to [alpha_sender_id@telnyx.com](mailto:alpha_sender_id@telnyx.com):
  1. Sender ID to be registered
  2. Message/Content Type (Transactional/Promotional/Notification)
  3. Message/Content example(s)
  4. Company name (and Brand name if different)
  5. Website of brand or company
  6. Company Country of origin
  7. Expected volumes per month
  8. Email linked to your Telnyx account
- **Compliance:** Refer to the [Acceptable Use Policy for Messaging](https://support.telnyx.com/en/articles/1310359-acceptable-use-policy-for-messaging).

## Ukraine
- **MCC:** 255
- **Dial Code:** 380
- **Alphanumeric Sender IDs:** Partially supported; registration is not possible. Occasionally, the Alphanumeric Sender ID might be overwritten to a random Short Code to ensure delivery.
- **Restrictions:** Due to the ongoing situation, SMS delivery towards Ukraine is considered best-effort. The use of generic Alpha Sender IDs is not recommended as they can be rejected or blocked by local operators; Sender IDs should be directly related to the message content.
- **Compliance:** Refer to the [Acceptable Use Policy for Messaging](https://support.telnyx.com/en/articles/1310359-acceptable-use-policy-for-messaging).
