# DID Requirements: Bangladesh, Kazakhstan, Pakistan, Turkey, Uzbekistan

Documentation needed to purchase Telnyx DIDs in Bangladesh, Kazakhstan, Pakistan, Turkey, and Uzbekistan, including identity and address verification by number type and any country-specific constraints.

## Bangladesh
- Number types: National and Mobile
- Business identity verification:
  - Name and last name of an authorized representative
  - Company name
  - Contact phone number
  - Passport or ID copy of an authorized representative
  - Business Registration Certificate
  - Signed LOI (dated within 1 month)
- Address verification:
  - Worldwide address (street, building number, postal code, city, and country)
  - Proof of Address matching the address in the Business Registration Certificate (dated within 3 months)
- Additional conditions:
  - Business use required; private use is not allowed
  - Activation timeline: approximately 72 hours after documentation is received

## Kazakhstan
- Number types: Local
- Personal identity verification:
  - Name, last name
  - Contact phone number
  - LOI (provided by Telnyx)
- Business identity verification:
  - Name, last name of an authorized representative
  - Company name
  - Contact phone number
  - LOI (provided by Telnyx)
- Address verification:
  - Worldwide address (street, building number, postal code, city, and country)
  - Proof of address (dated within 3 months)

## Pakistan
- Number types: Local
- Personal identity verification:
  - Name, last name
  - Contact phone number
  - LOI (provided by Telnyx)
- Business identity verification:
  - Name, last name of an authorized representative
  - Company name
  - Contact phone number
  - LOI (provided by Telnyx)
- Address verification:
  - Worldwide address (street, building number, postal code, city, and country)
  - Proof of address (dated within 3 months)

## Turkey
- Number types: Local and National
- Personal identity verification:
  - Name, last name of an authorized representative
  - Contact phone number
  - Signed copy of personal subscription agreement (Abonelik Sozlesmesi)
  - Passport or ID copy (Kimlik Fotokopisi)
- Business identity verification:
  - Name, last name of an authorized representative
  - Company name
  - Contact phone number
  - Signed copy of corporate subscription agreement (Kurumsal Abonelik Sözleşmesi)
  - Circular of Signatures (İmza Sirküleri)
  - Tax Registration Certificate (Vergi Levhası)
  - Chamber of Commerce Registration Document (Faaliyet Belgesi)
  - Trade Registry Gazette (Ticari Sicil Gazetesi)
  - Passport or ID copy of an authorized representative (Yetkili Kimlik Fotokopisi)
- Address verification:
  - Address in Turkey (street, building number, postal code, city, and country)
  - Address confirmation from Turkish government portal (Adres Beyani): https://www.turkiye.gov.tr/nvi-yerlesim-yeri-ve-diger-adres-belgesi-sorgulama

## Uzbekistan
- Number types: Local
- Personal identity verification:
  - Name, last name
  - Contact phone number
  - LOI (provided by Telnyx)
- Business identity verification:
  - Name, last name of an authorized representative
  - Company name
  - Contact phone number
  - LOI (provided by Telnyx)
- Address verification:
  - Worldwide address (street, building number, postal code, city, and country)
  - Proof of address (dated within 3 months)

## Additional notes
- LOI: Telnyx supplies the Letter of Intent; ensure it’s completed as required and signed/dated where specified by the country.
- Proof of address recency: Where required, documents must be dated within the last 3 months. Bangladesh additionally requires the proof of address to match the Business Registration Certificate.
- Local residency: Turkey requires a Turkish address and official address confirmation via the government portal for address verification.
