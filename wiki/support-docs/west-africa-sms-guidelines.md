# West Africa: SMS Guidelines

Requirements and best practices for sending SMS to West African destinations covered by Telnyx, including Sender ID support, operator-specific registration (notably MTN networks), content restrictions, and key dialing/MCC details.

## Regional overview and best practices
- Alphanumeric Sender IDs are widely supported across West Africa. Several MTN networks require preregistration; without it, messages may be overwritten to a generic alpha or fail delivery.
- Avoid generic Alpha Sender IDs; use a sender clearly related to the message content.
- Review Telnyx’s Acceptable Use Policy for Messaging: https://support.telnyx.com/en/articles/1310359-acceptable-use-policy-for-messaging
- For Alpha Sender ID registration details, contact: mailto:alpha_sender_id@telnyx.com

## Benin
- MCC: 616; Dial code: +229
- Alphanumeric Sender IDs supported.
- MTN (61603): Alpha Sender IDs require registration; unregistered traffic may be overwritten to a generic alpha or not deliver.
- Generic alpha discouraged; sender should match message content.
- Refer to AUP (link above). For registration, email alpha_sender_id@telnyx.com.

## Burkina Faso

- Alphanumeric Sender IDs supported and maintained; no registration required.
- No content restrictions noted.
