# Telnyx Global Number Requirements and Porting Guide

*Part 2 of 4 — see also: [Part 1](telnyx-global-number-requirements-and-porting-guide--part-1.md), [Part 3](telnyx-global-number-requirements-and-porting-guide--part-3.md), [Part 4](telnyx-global-number-requirements-and-porting-guide--part-4.md)*

A consolidated reference covering Telnyx DID purchase requirements across multiple countries (Turkey, Australia, Uruguay, Cyprus, Greece, Montenegro, Brazil, North Macedonia, Serbia) and number porting procedures for Australia, New Zealand, and Singapore, including document checklists, processing timeframes, and category-specific rules.

## Greece DID Requirements

To purchase a Greece number, the following must be provided.

### Local Numbers

For **personal identity** verification:
- Name, last name
- Contact phone number
- Local Passport or ID copy
- VAT number

For **business identity** verification:
- Name, last name of an authorized representative
- Company name
- Contact phone number
- Local Passport or ID copy of an authorized representative
- Local Company registration certificate
- VAT number of an authorized representative
- LOA or POA — Letter of Authorization or Power of Attorney, to be filled in and signed (provided by Telnyx)

For **address** verification:
- Address matching the DID area code (street, building number, postal code, city, and country)
- Proof of address (dated within 3 months)

End-users must be physically present in the country when purchasing numbers from that country.

### Identity Verification: What "Local" Means

For most orders, proof of identity must be issued by the country in which the purchase is being made. As an exception, customers within the European Union may use a valid passport or national identity card from any EU member state, even if it differs from the EU country associated with the order; in these cases, the document will be treated as local. All identification is subject to review, and Telnyx reserves the right to request additional documentation or decline any document that cannot be verified or does not meet applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

## Montenegro DID Requirements

To purchase a Montenegro number, the following must be provided. Once documentation is received, it takes approximately 72 hours to validate and activate the number.

### Local Numbers

For **business identity** verification:
- Name, last name of an authorized representative
- Company name

For **address** verification:
- Address worldwide (street, building number, postal code, city and country)

Additional information required:
- Service usage description
- Business use is required; private use is not allowed.

## Brazil DID Requirements

To purchase a Brazilian number, the following must be provided. It may take up to 3 business days to review and approve Brazil orders.

### Local/Mobile Numbers

For **personal identity** verification:
- Name, last name
- Contact phone number
- CPF number
- Passport or ID copy

For **business identity** verification:
- Name, last name of an authorized representative
- Company name
- Contact phone number
- Cartão CNPJ (Comprovante de Inscrição e Situação Cadastral)

For **address** verification:
- Address in Brazil (street, building number, postal code, city and country)
- Proof of address (dated within 3 months)

### Toll-Free/National (3003/4004) Numbers

For **personal identity** verification:
- Name, last name
- Contact phone number
- Contact e-mail
- CPF number
- Passport or ID copy

For **business identity** verification:
- Name, last name of an authorized representative
- Company name
- Contact phone number
- Contact e-mail
- CNPJ number
- Power of Attorney
- Passport or ID copy of an authorized representative
- Company Registration Certificate

For **address** verification:
- Address in Brazil (street, building number, postal code, city and country)
