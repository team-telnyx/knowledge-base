# Latin America DID Requirements

*Part 1 of 3 — see also: [Part 2](latin-america-did-requirements--part-2.md), [Part 3](latin-america-did-requirements--part-3.md)*

This page consolidates Telnyx's documentation requirements for acquiring and porting Direct Inward Dialing (DID) numbers across Latin American countries, including Chile, Colombia, Costa Rica, Ecuador, Georgia, Honduras, Iceland, Mexico, Nicaragua, Panama, Paraguay, Peru, and Venezuela. It covers identity, business, and address verification documentation, as well as porting procedures and timelines for Mexico.

## Overview

Telnyx provides Direct Inward Dialing (DID) numbers across many Latin American countries. Requirements vary by country and by number type (local, national, mobile, toll-free, or shared cost). In general, customers must supply personal or business identity documentation, address verification, and in some cases a signed Letter of Intent (LOI) or registration form. For most countries, validation and activation take approximately 72 hours after documentation is received.

## Chile

### Local Numbers
- **Personal identity:** Name, last name; Passport or ID copy.
- **Business identity:** Name, last name of an authorized representative; Company name; Passport or ID copy of an authorized representative.
- **Address:** Address worldwide (street, building number, postal code, city and country).

### Toll-Free Numbers
- **Business identity:** Name, last name of an authorized representative; Company name; Passport or ID copy of an authorized representative.
- **Address:** Address worldwide (street, building number, postal code, city and country).
- Business use is required; private use is not allowed.

### National and Shared Cost Numbers
- **Personal identity:** Name, last name; Contact phone number; Email address; Passport or ID copy.
- **Business identity:** Company name; Contact phone number; Email address; TAX/RUT number; Company registration certificate.
- **Address:** Address worldwide (street, building number, postal code, city and country).

For custom pricing, see the [Chile number pricing](https://telnyx.com/pricing/numbers) page.

## Costa Rica

### National Numbers
- **Business identity:** Name, last name; Contact phone number; Company name; Company incorporation certificate copy.
- **Address:** Address worldwide (street, building number, postal code, city and country).
- Business use is required; private use is not allowed.

### Toll-Free Numbers
- **Business identity:** Name, last name of an authorized representative; Company name; Contact phone number; Passport or ID copy of an authorized representative; Power of attorney; Extract from the Commercial Register (Chamber of Commerce).
- **Address:** Address worldwide (street, building number, postal code, city and country); Proof of address (dated within 3 months).
- Business use is required; private use is not allowed.

## Ecuador

### Toll-Free Numbers
- **Personal identity:** Name, last name; Contact phone number; Signed LOI (dated within 1 month).
- **Business identity:** Name, last name of an authorized representative; Company name; Contact phone number; Signed LOI (dated within 1 month).
- **Address:** Address worldwide (street, building number, postal code, city, and country).

### Mobile Numbers
- **Personal identity:** Name, last name; Contact phone number; LOI (provided by Telnyx).
- **Business identity:** Name, last name of an authorized representative; Company name; Contact phone number; LOI (provided by Telnyx).
- **Address:** Worldwide address (street, building number, postal code, city and country); Proof of address (dated within 3 months).

Once documentation is received, validation and activation take approximately 72 hours.

## Georgia

### Local Numbers
- **Personal identity:** Name, last name; Contact phone number; Contact e-mail; Passport or ID copy; Georgia registration form.
- **Business identity:** Name, last name of an authorized representative; Company name; Contact phone number; Contact e-mail; Company registration certificate; Georgia registration form.
- **Address:** Address matching the DID area code (street, building number, postal code, city and country); A copy of a utility bill (less than 3 months old).

### National Numbers
- **Personal identity:** Name, last name; Contact phone number; Passport or ID copy; Georgia registration form.
- **Business identity:** Name, last name of an authorized representative; Company name; Contact phone number; Company registration certificate; Georgia registration form.
- **Address:** Address worldwide (street, building number, postal code, city, and country).

### Toll-Free Numbers
- **Personal identity:** Name, last name of an authorized representative; Contact phone number; Contact e-mail; Passport or ID copy; Georgia registration form.
- **Business identity:** Name, last name; Company name; Contact phone number; Contact e-mail; Company registration certificate; Georgia registration form; Forms holding Terms & Conditions must be signed.
- **Address:** Address worldwide (street, building number, postal code, city, and country); Proof of address (dated within 3 months).

Once documentation is received, validation and activation take approximately 72 hours.
