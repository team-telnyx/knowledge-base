# Latin America DID Requirements

*Part 1 of 2 — see also: [Part 2](latin-america-did-requirements--part-2.md)*

Use this guide to understand the documents Telnyx needs to provision phone numbers (DIDs) across Latin America, broken down by country and number type (local, mobile, toll-free, national, and shared cost). It also highlights timing expectations, templates, and country-specific notes.

## What you’ll typically need
- Personal identity: Full name, contact phone; in some countries a passport or national ID is required.
- Business identity: Company name; an authorized representative’s name (and sometimes their ID); company registration documents (e.g., certificate, CNPJ/CNPJ card in Brazil, TAX/RUT in Chile, local company certificates in Peru/Venezuela).
- Address verification: Address may be worldwide or must be in-country/area-code depending on the country and number type; proof of address is often required and must be recent (typically within 3 months).
- Additional forms/info (when applicable):
  - LOI (Letter of Intent), often signed within the last month (e.g., Ecuador toll-free/mobile; Uruguay toll-free; Paraguay toll-free).
  - POA (Power of Attorney) for some Brazil toll-free/national orders.
  - Service usage description (e.g., Venezuela local/toll-free; Panama toll-free).
- “Authorized representative” means the individual empowered to act for the company; their ID/passport may be required.

## Review timelines and validation
- Typical review/activation time after complete documentation submission: about 72 hours (3 business days).
- Brazil orders may take up to 3 business days to review/approve.

## Country-by-country requirements
### Argentina
- Local and Shared Cost
  - Personal: Name, last name; contact phone; passport or ID copy.
  - Business: Company name; contact phone; company registration certificate.
  - Address: Worldwide.
- Toll-Free
  - Personal: Name, last name.
  - Business: Company name.
  - Address: Worldwide.

### Bolivia
- Toll-Free and National
  - Personal: Name, last name; contact phone; passport or ID copy.
  - Business: Company name; contact phone; company registration certificate.
  - Address: Worldwide.

### Brazil
- Local/Mobile
  - Personal: Name, last name; contact phone; CPF number; passport or ID copy.
  - Business: Authorized representative’s name, last name; company name; contact phone; Cartão CNPJ (Comprovante de Inscrição e Situação Cadastral).
  - Address: In Brazil; proof of address (≤3 months).
- Toll-Free/National (3003/4004)
  - Personal: Name, last name; contact phone; contact email; CPF; passport or ID copy.
  - Business: Authorized representative’s name, last name; company name; contact phone; contact email; CNPJ number; Power of Attorney; authorized representative’s passport or ID; company registration certificate.
  - Address: In Brazil.
  - Timing: Review can take up to 3 business days.

### Chile
- Local
  - Personal: Name, last name; passport or ID copy.
  - Business: Authorized representative’s name, last name; company name; authorized representative’s passport or ID copy.
  - Address: Worldwide.
- Toll-Free
  - Business: Authorized representative’s name, last name; company name; authorized representative’s passport or ID copy.
  - Address: Worldwide.
  - Note: Business use required (private use not allowed).
- National and Shared Cost
  - Personal: Name, last name; contact phone; email address; passport or ID copy.
  - Business: Company name; contact phone; email address; TAX/RUT number; company registration certificate.
  - Address: Worldwide.

### Colombia
- Local
  - Personal: Name, last name.
  - Business: Authorized representative’s name, last name; company name.
  - Address: Worldwide.
- Mobile
  - Personal: Name, last name.
  - Business: Authorized representative’s name, last name; company name.
  - Address: Worldwide.
- Toll-Free
  - Personal: Name, last name; contact phone; passport or ID copy.
  - Business: Company name; contact phone; company registration certificate.
  - Address: Worldwide.

### Ecuador
- Toll-Free
  - Personal: Name, last name; contact phone; signed LOI (dated within 1 month).
  - Business: Authorized representative’s name, last name; company name; contact phone; signed LOI (dated within 1 month).
  - Address: Worldwide.
- Mobile
  - Personal: Name, last name; contact phone; LOI (Telnyx-provided template available).
  - Business: Authorized representative’s name, last name; company name; contact phone; LOI.
  - Address: Worldwide; proof of address (≤3 months).
  - Timing: Validation/activation typically ~72 hours after docs are received.

### Mexico
- Local and Toll-Free
  - Personal: Name, last name; contact phone; passport or ID copy.
  - Business: Company name; contact phone; company registration certificate.
  - Address: Worldwide.
- Mobile
  - Personal: Name, last name.
  - Business: Company name.
  - Address: Worldwide.

### Panama
- Local
  - Personal: Name, last name; contact phone; passport or ID copy.
  - Business: Company name; contact phone; company registration certificate.
  - Address: Worldwide.
- Toll-Free
  - Business: Authorized representative’s name, last name; company name; contact phone; authorized representative’s passport or ID copy; Panama registration form.
  - Address: Worldwide.
  - Additional: Service usage description; business use required (private use not allowed).
  - Timing: Typically ~72 hours after docs are received.

### Paraguay
- Local
  - Personal: Name, last name; contact phone; passport or ID copy.
  - Business: Company name; contact phone; company registration certificate.
  - Address: Worldwide.
- Toll-Free
  - Personal: Name, last name; contact phone; passport or ID copy; signed LOI (dated within 1 month).
  - Business: Company name; contact phone; company registration certificate; signed LOI (dated within 1 month).
  - Address: Worldwide.

### Peru
- Local
  - Business: Authorized representative’s name, last name; company name; contact phone; authorized representative’s passport or ID copy; Peruvian company registration certificate.
  - Address: Must match the DID area code; proof of address (≤3 months).
  - Note: Business use required (private use not allowed).
- Toll-Free
  - Business: Authorized representative’s name, last name; company name; contact phone; authorized representative’s passport or ID copy; Peruvian company registration certificate.
  - Address: In Peru; proof of address (≤3 months).
  - Note: Business use required (private use not allowed).
- Shared Cost
  - Personal: Name, last name; contact phone; passport or ID copy.
  - Business: Company name; contact phone; company registration certificate.
  - Address: Worldwide.
  - Timing: Typically ~72 hours after docs are received.

### Uruguay
- Toll-Free
  - Personal: Name, last name; contact phone; signed LOI (dated within 1 month).
  - Business: Authorized representative’s name, last name; company name; contact phone; signed LOI (dated within 1 month).
  - Address: Worldwide; proof of address (≤3 months).
  - Timing: Typically ~72 hours after docs are received.

### Venezuela
- Local
  - Personal: Name, last name; contact phone; Venezuelan passport or ID copy.
  - Business: Authorized representative’s name, last name; contact phone; company name; Venezuelan company registration certificate.
  - Address: In Venezuela; proof of address (≤3 months).
  - Additional: Service usage description.
- Toll-Free
  - Personal: Name, last name; contact phone.
  - Business: Authorized representative’s name, last name; company name; contact phone; company registration certificate.
  - Address: Worldwide; proof of address (≤3 months).
  - Additional: Service usage description.
  - Timing: Typically ~72 hours after docs are received.
