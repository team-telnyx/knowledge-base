# Telnyx Platform Overview

*Part 1 of 3 — see also: [Part 2](telnyx-platform-overview--part-2.md), [Part 3](telnyx-platform-overview--part-3.md)*

Telnyx is a Communications Platform as a Service (CPaaS) provider founded in 2009 and based in Austin, Texas, offering voice, messaging, networking, AI inference, storage, and workflow automation through a globally distributed private IP network and robust APIs.

## Company Overview

Telnyx was founded in 2009 and is headquartered at 600 Congress Avenue, 14th Floor, Austin, TX 78701, with additional offices in Denver, Dublin, and Amsterdam, plus remote employees worldwide. As a cutting-edge CPaaS provider, Telnyx enables businesses to integrate real-time communication services—voice, messaging, and connectivity—into their applications and workflows via robust APIs.

Telnyx distinguishes itself through its privately-owned, fully-meshed IP network, which delivers reduced latency, enhanced reliability, and industry-leading quality by not relying on third-party carriers for routing. The platform supports VoIP, SMS, MMS, video, SIP trunking, and network-related offerings, and has expanded to include AI-powered Inference, cloud Storage, and the Flow workflow automation tool.

## Core Products and Services

### VoIP and Voice Services

Telnyx provides carrier-grade VoIP solutions supporting call routing, IVR, number masking, and global coverage. The platform is ideal for call centers, customer support services, and unified communication platforms, with active monitoring and global points of presence (PoPs) ensuring optimal call routing and voice quality.

### Elastic SIP Trunking

Elastic SIP Trunking offers pay-as-you-go SIP trunking with no minimum commitments, allowing businesses to add or remove channels dynamically. It provides high-availability, multi-region redundancy, advanced failover mechanisms, and configurable call failover policies.

### SMS and MMS Messaging

Through its messaging API, Telnyx supports high-throughput programmable SMS and MMS for customer engagement, marketing, two-factor authentication (2FA), and automation, with global delivery and easy integration into CRMs, websites, and mobile apps.

### WebRTC and Real-Time Communications

Telnyx provides WebRTC SDKs for integrating secure, high-quality video and audio calls into web and mobile applications. Detailed setup guides cover iOS and Android push notifications using the WebRTC Voice SDK. See [WebRTC Voice SDK](webrtc-voice-sdk.md) for more information.

### AI-Powered Inference

Telnyx Inference adds machine learning capabilities to communication workflows, including speech-to-text, text-to-speech, sentiment analysis, and language detection. Use cases include voice bots, call transcription, intent detection, and real-time analytics.

### Cloud Storage

Telnyx Storage provides secure cloud-based media storage for call recordings, voicemails, video, and message attachments. It integrates tightly with communication services, supports compliance (GDPR, HIPAA), offers accessible APIs, and encrypts all stored media.

### Flow Workflow Automation

Telnyx Flow is a visual drag-and-drop builder for automated communication workflows, supporting multi-channel (voice, SMS, MMS, email), API integration, event-driven triggers, and custom notifications—without requiring extensive coding.

## Global Network and Infrastructure

Telnyx operates a fully-meshed private IP network providing a global backbone for optimized communication traffic. The network infrastructure uses:

- **Cisco ASR routers** for core and edge routing
- **Juniper QFX5100** for aggregation switches
- **Brocade Vyatta** with virtualized routing platforms for Cloud CE routing

The company continues to expand its private network with additional PoPs and enhanced Direct Routing services for Microsoft Teams and Operator Connect. More details are available on the [Telnyx Network Page](https://telnyx.com/our-network).

## International Coverage and Number Management

### Number Provisioning and Porting

Telnyx provides a self-service platform for provisioning, managing, and porting phone numbers in real time across 100+ countries, supporting local, toll-free, and international numbers. Number porting is streamlined to minimize downtime. Porting requirements vary by country; for example, South Africa porting requires an LOA, latest invoice, ID/passport copy, company registration certificate, and proof of address. See [Number Porting Rules and Guidelines](number-porting-rules-and-guidelines.md) for more details.

### International Number Coverage

Telnyx offers phone numbers in over 100 countries, including the United States, Canada, United Kingdom, Germany, France, Australia, Japan, Brazil, India, and many more across Europe, Asia, Africa, and the Americas. International termination is available worldwide; pricing is accessible through the [Telnyx Portal](https://portal.telnyx.com/#/pricing/voice).

### Numbering Team Best Practices

Before contacting the numbering team, search for desired numbers in the portal. If unavailable, use the "Request Number" button. Pending orders with regulatory requirements should be fulfilled within 2 days to avoid auto-cancellation. The numbering team is available Monday–Friday, 9 AM – 5 PM CT, via portal chat or [numbering@telnyx.com](mailto:numbering@telnyx.com). For new number requests, use the portal form; for order updates, use chat; for urgent issues, email the numbering team directly.

## Hardware Compatibility

Telnyx is a cloud-based platform and does not provide hardware. However, it is compatible with almost any SIP-enabled device. Recommended hardware configurations require SIP compatibility and support for the G.729, G.711, or Opus audio codecs. Configuration guides for many platforms (Yealink, Grandstream, Fanvil, MicroSIP, Cisco, etc.) are available in the Telnyx knowledge base.
