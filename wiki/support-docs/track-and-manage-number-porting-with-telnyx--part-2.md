# Track and Manage Number Porting with Telnyx

*Part 2 of 2 — see also: [Part 1](track-and-manage-number-porting-with-telnyx--part-1.md)*

Learn how to check the real-time status of your port requests in Mission Control, submit ports with the right documents, use automated validation and FastPort, follow best practices, understand timelines, handle port-outs, and know when PINs/CSRs are needed.

## Processing hours, holidays, and communications
- Submission: 24/7/365 via Mission Control; processing Mon–Fri, 9am–5pm CT.
- US porting holidays observed: New Year’s Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving Day, Christmas Day. Other carriers may observe additional holidays, potentially delaying timelines.
- Communication: Use the request’s Comments in Mission Control for status-related updates and document uploads. You’ll also get automated email updates.
