# France and French Territories Number Requirements & Porting

*Part 1 of 2 — see also: [Part 2](france-and-french-territories-number-requirements-porting--part-2.md)*

This page consolidates Telnyx requirements for acquiring DID numbers and porting existing numbers in France, its overseas territories (French Guiana, Guadeloupe, Martinique, Mayotte, Reunion, Saint Barthélemy, Saint Martin), and Luxembourg. It covers identity, business, and address documentation for each number type, plus porting procedures, timelines, and common rejection reasons.

## Overview

Telnyx offers DID (Direct Inward Dialing) numbers across France, its overseas territories, and Luxembourg. Requirements differ by country and number type (local, national, toll-free). This page consolidates the documentation needed to purchase new numbers and to port existing numbers into Telnyx.

## France DID Requirements

### Local Numbers in France

**Personal identity verification:**
- Name, last name
- Contact phone number
- Local copy of ID or passport

**Business identity verification:**
- Name, last name of an authorized representative
- Company name
- Contact phone number
- Local company registration certificate

**Address verification:**
- Address in France (street, building number, postal code, city, and country)
- Proof of address (dated within 3 months)

### National Numbers in France

**Personal identity verification:**
- Name, last name
- Contact phone number
- Local passport or ID copy

**Business identity verification:**
- Name, last name of an authorized representative
- Company name
- Contact phone number
- Local company registration certificate

**Address verification:**
- Address in France (street, building number, postal code, city, and country)
- Proof of address (dated within 3 months)

### Toll-Free Numbers in France

**Business identity verification:**
- Name, last name of an authorized representative
- Company name
- Contact phone number
- Local company registration certificate
- Company Registration Number (SIREN number)

**Address verification:**
- Address in France (street, building number, postal code, city, and country)
- Proof of address (dated within 3 months)

**Additional notes for France:**
- End-users must be physically present in the country when purchasing numbers from that country.
- Business use is required for toll-free numbers; private use is not allowed.
- Once documentation is received, validation and number activation take approximately 72 hours.

### Identity Verification: What "Local" Means

For most orders, proof of identity must be issued by the country in which the purchase is being made. As an exception, customers within the European Union may use a valid passport or national identity card from any EU member state, even if it differs from the EU country associated with the order; in these cases, the document will be treated as local.

All identification is subject to review, and Telnyx reserves the right to request additional documentation or decline any document that cannot be verified or that does not meet applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

## France Number Porting

### Required Documents (Local / National / Toll-Free)

1. LOA (Letter of Authorization) — national address mandatory
2. SIRET code (business accounts only, must be 14 digits long)
3. RIO code (must be 12 characters long)
4. Latest invoice with the current provider
5. Proof of address (dated within 3 months of the request)

[Download the France LOA](https://assets.ctfassets.net/taysl255dolk/4pv4cqjnsUfEGbyexn1Ycy/536af1c7e37734422a9556ea38582ceb/Telnyx_LoA_-_France.pdf)

### Notes on RIO and SIRET Codes

- **RIO code:** The customer can obtain this code by dialing 3179 or 0 805 92 3179. If the current carrier does not allow calls to special numbers, the customer must contact the current carrier directly to retrieve the RIO.
- **SIRET code:** An INSEE code that allows the geographic identification of any French establishment or business. Any business registered in France will have a SIRET of 14 digits.

### Port Types

There are four types of ports possible in France. All take at least 8 business days to port.

1. **Isolated** — Porting only one number.
2. **Full portability** — Migrating a main number along with all associated numbers.
3. **Partial portability** — Migrating some of the associated numbers to a BTN (Billing Telephone Number), without porting the actual BTN.
4. **Complex portability** — Migrating a main phone number without the associated numbers to that number. The customer must inform which number will become the new BTN.

### Time of Port

There are only two available time slots:

1. **Morning trigger (10 AM trigger)** — The request will port from 10 AM to 1 PM.
2. **Afternoon trigger (2 PM trigger)** — The request will port from 2 PM to 5 PM.

### Information on File

It is crucial to verify the information before Telnyx submits a port request. Telnyx must wait until the losing carrier rejects the initial port request before submitting a new one. This can take up to 3 business days and delay the process.

*Example:* If the customer provided an incorrect business address and the port request has already been sent, Telnyx must wait for the losing communications provider to reject the request before submitting another port request for the same number with a different address; otherwise, the new port request will be rejected for a pending port request.

### Most Common Rejections for France Number Porting

- Business address mismatch
- SIRET code invalid
- Business name mismatch
- Pending porting request with another carrier

## Luxembourg DID Requirements

### Local Numbers in Luxembourg

**Business identity verification:**
- Name, last name of an authorized representative
- Company name
- Contact phone number
- Contact e-mail
- Company registration certificate

**Address verification:**
- Address in Luxembourg (street, building number, postal code, city, and country)
- Proof of address (dated within 3 months)

Business use is required; private use is not allowed.

### National Numbers in Luxembourg

**Personal identity verification:**
- Name, last name
- Contact phone number
- Date of birth
- Place of birth

**Business identity verification:**
- Name, last name of an authorized representative
- Company name
- Contact phone number

**Address verification:**
- Address in Luxembourg (street, building number, postal code, city, and country)

### Toll-Free Numbers in Luxembourg

**Business identity verification:**
- Name, last name
- Company name
- Contact phone number
- Extract from the Commercial Register (Chamber of Commerce)

**Address verification:**
- Address worldwide (street, building number, postal code, city, and country)

Business use is required; private use is not allowed.

Once documentation is received, validation and number activation take approximately 72 hours.
