# Caribbean and Nearby Territories: SMS Guidelines (Multi-country)

Consolidated SMS guidelines for Montserrat, Netherlands Antilles, Saint Kitts & Nevis, Saint Lucia, Saint Pierre and Miquelon, Saint Vincent and the Grenadines, Trinidad & Tobago, Turks and Caicos Islands, and Virgin Islands (British), including MCCs, dial codes, sender ID support, content notes, and any special delivery considerations.

## Overview
Across these destinations, alphanumeric Sender IDs are supported and generally maintained without registration requirements. Most destinations list no additional content restrictions beyond Telnyx’s Acceptable Use Policy. One special routing caveat applies in Trinidad & Tobago (see below).

## Compliance and acceptable use
Always ensure your messaging complies with Telnyx policies and local regulations. Refer to Telnyx’s Acceptable Use Policy for Messaging: https://support.telnyx.com/en/articles/1310359-acceptable-use-policy-for-messaging

## Montserrat
- MCC: 354
- Dial Code: 1664
- Alphanumeric Sender IDs: Supported and maintained; no registration required
- Content restrictions: None noted toward this destination

## Netherlands Antilles
- MCC: 362
- Dial Code: 599
- Alphanumeric Sender IDs: Supported and maintained; no registration required
- Content restrictions: None noted toward this destination

## Saint Kitts & Nevis
- MCC: 356
- Dial Code: 1869
- Alphanumeric Sender IDs: Supported and maintained; no registration required
- Content restrictions: None noted toward this destination

## Saint Lucia
- MCC: 358
- Dial Code: 1758
- Alphanumeric Sender IDs: Supported and maintained; no registration required
- Content restrictions: None noted toward this destination

## Saint Pierre and Miquelon
- MCC: 308
- Dial Code: 508
- Alphanumeric Sender IDs: Supported and maintained; no registration required
- Content restrictions: None noted toward this destination

## Saint Vincent and the Grenadines
- MCC: 360
- Dial Code: 1784
- Alphanumeric Sender IDs: Supported and maintained; no registration required
- Content restrictions: None noted toward this destination

## Trinidad & Tobago
- MCC: 374
- Dial Code: 1868
- Alphanumeric Sender IDs: Supported; no registration required
- Special delivery note: Sender ID may occasionally be overwritten to a random long code to ensure delivery toward network Bmobile (37412)

## Turks and Caicos Islands
- MCC: 376
- Dial Code: 1649
- Alphanumeric Sender IDs: Supported and maintained; no registration required
- Content restrictions: None noted toward this destination

## Virgin Islands (British)
- MCC: 348
- Dial Code: 1340
- Alphanumeric Sender IDs: Supported and maintained; no registration required
- Content restrictions: None noted toward this destination
