# Eastern Europe DID Requirements & Caller ID Policy

*Part 1 of 2 — see also: [Part 2](eastern-europe-did-requirements-caller-id-policy--part-2.md)*

Covers DID number purchasing requirements for Eastern European countries (Albania, Czech Republic, Hungary, Latvia, Romania, Russia, Slovakia, Ukraine) including identity, business, and address verification rules, along with Telnyx's Caller ID Number Policy for outbound calls.

## DID Number Requirements by Country

The following sections detail the identity, business, and address verification requirements for purchasing DID numbers in each supported Eastern European country. Common rules apply across many countries: proof of address documents must be dated within 3 months; Letters of Intent (LOI) must be dated within 1 month; and once documentation is received, it takes approximately 72 hours to validate and activate the number.

## Albania

**Local Numbers** — Business identity verification only. Private use is not allowed.

- **Business identity:** Name and last name of an authorized representative, company name, contact phone number, passport or ID copy of the authorized representative, company registration certificate.
- **Address:** Worldwide address (street, building number, postal code, city, country).

**Toll-Free Numbers** — Business identity verification only. Private use is not allowed.

- **Business identity:** Company name, passport or ID copy of an authorized representative, company registration certificate.
- **Address:** Worldwide address (street, building number, postal code, city, country).

## Czech Republic

**Local Numbers**

- **Personal identity:** Name and last name, contact phone number, local passport or ID copy.
- **Business identity:** Name and last name of an authorized representative, company name, contact phone number, local company registration certificate.
- **Address:** Address matching the DID area code (street, building number, postal code, city, country), plus proof of address (dated within 3 months).
- **Additional:** End-users must be physically present in the country when purchasing.

**Toll-Free Numbers**

- **Personal identity:** Name and last name, contact phone number, local passport or ID copy.
- **Business identity:** Company name, contact phone number, local company registration certificate.
- **Address:** Address in Czech Republic (street, building number, postal code, city, country), plus proof of address (dated within 3 months).
- **Additional:** End-users must be physically present in the country when purchasing.

**EU Identity Exception:** Customers within the European Union may use a valid passport or national identity card from any EU member state as proof of identity; it will be treated as local even if it differs from the country of the order. All identification is subject to review, and Telnyx reserves the right to request additional documentation or decline documents that cannot be verified.

## Hungary

**Local Numbers**

- **Personal identity:** Name and last name, contact phone number, passport or ID copy.
- **Business identity:** Name and last name of an authorized representative, company name, contact phone number, company registration certificate.
- **Address:** Address matching the DID area code (street, building number, postal code, city, country), plus proof of address (dated within 3 months).

**National Numbers**

- **Personal identity:** Name and last name, contact phone number, passport or ID copy.
- **Business identity:** Name and last name of an authorized representative, company name, contact phone number, company registration certificate.
- **Address:** Address in Hungary (street, building number, postal code, city, country), plus proof of address (dated within 3 months).

**Toll-Free Numbers**

- **Personal identity:** Name and last name, contact phone number, passport or ID copy.
- **Business identity:** Name and last name of an authorized representative, company name, contact phone number, company registration certificate.
- **Address:** Address in Hungary (street, building number, postal code, city, country), plus proof of address (dated within 3 months).

## Latvia

**Local Numbers** — Business identity verification only. Private use is not allowed.

- **Business identity:** Name and last name of an authorized representative, contact phone number, company name, copy of ID or passport of the authorized representative, company registration certificate.
- **Address:** Address in Latvia (street, building number, postal code, city, country), plus proof of address (dated within 3 months).

**Toll-Free Numbers**

- **Personal identity:** Name and last name, contact phone number.
- **Business identity:** Name and last name of an authorized representative, company name, contact phone number.
- **Address:** Address in Latvia (street, building number, postal code, city, country).

## Romania

**Local, National, and Toll-Free Numbers** — Requirements are the same across all three number types.

- **Personal identity:** Name and last name, contact phone number, local passport or ID copy.
- **Business identity:** Name and last name of an authorized representative, company name, contact phone number, local company registration certificate.
- **Address:** Address in Romania (street, building number, postal code, city, country), plus proof of address (dated within 3 months).
- **Additional:** End-users must be physically present in the country when purchasing.

**EU Identity Exception:** Customers within the European Union may use a valid passport or national identity card from any EU member state as proof of identity; it will be treated as local even if it differs from the country of the order. All identification is subject to review, and Telnyx reserves the right to request additional documentation or decline documents that cannot be verified.

## Russia

**Mobile Numbers** — Business identity verification only. Private use is not allowed.

- **Business identity:** Name and last name of an authorized representative, company name, contact phone number, passport or ID copy of the authorized representative, business registration certificate, signed LOI (dated within 1 month).
- **Address:** Worldwide address (street, building number, postal code, city, country), plus proof of address matching the address in the business registration certificate (dated within 3 months).

## Slovakia

**Local Numbers**

- **Personal identity:** Name and last name, contact phone number, copy of ID or passport.
- **Business identity:** Name and last name of an authorized representative, company name, contact phone number, copy of ID or passport of the authorized representative, company registration certificate.
- **Address:** Address matching the DID area code (street, building number, postal code, city, country), plus proof of address (dated within 3 months).

**Toll-Free Numbers** — Business identity verification only. Private use is not allowed.

- **Business identity:** Name and last name of an authorized representative, company name, company registration certificate, VAT number.
- **Address:** Worldwide address (street, building number, postal code, city, country).

## Ukraine

**Local & National Numbers** — Business identity verification only. Private use is not allowed.

- **Business identity:** Name and last name of an authorized representative, company name, contact phone number, passport or ID copy of the authorized representative, business registration certificate, signed LOI (dated within 1 month).
- **Address:** Worldwide address (street, building number, postal code, city, country), plus proof of address matching the address in the business registration certificate (dated within 3 months).

**Mobile & Toll-Free Numbers**

- **Personal identity:** Name and last name, contact phone number, passport or ID copy.
- **Business identity:** Company name, contact phone number, company registration certificate.
- **Address:** Worldwide address (street, building number, postal code, city, country).
