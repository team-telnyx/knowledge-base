# Europe DID Requirements (Telnyx)

*Part 2 of 2 — see also: [Part 1](europe-did-requirements-telnyx--part-1.md)*

Consolidated regulatory requirements for purchasing Telnyx DID numbers across selected European countries. This guide lists identity and address documents by number type, highlights special constraints (e.g., business-only use, physical-presence rules, regulator forms), and notes where processing typically takes ~72 hours after document submission.

## Luxembourg
### Local
- Business: Authorized representative name, company name, contact phone, contact email, company registration certificate.
- Address: Address in Luxembourg; proof of address (≤3 months).
- Notes: Business use only (no private use).

### National
- Personal: Name, last name, contact phone, date of birth, place of birth.
- Business: Authorized representative name, company name, contact phone.
- Address: Address in Luxembourg.

### Toll-Free
- Business: Name, last name, company name, contact phone, extract from the Commercial Register (Chamber of Commerce).
- Address: Worldwide address permitted.
- Notes: Business use only. ~72-hour validation.

## Netherlands
### Local
- Personal: Name, last name, contact phone, passport/ID copy.
- Business: Authorized representative name, company name, contact phone, passport/ID copy (rep), company registration certificate.
- Address: Address matching DID area code; proof of address (≤3 months).

### National
- Business: Authorized representative name, company name, contact phone.
- Address: Address in the Netherlands.
- Notes: Business use only (no personal use).

### Mobile
- Personal: Name, last name.
- Business: Company name.
- Address: Worldwide address permitted.
- Notes: A2A/A2P use only (no person-to-person voice/SMS usage outside those scopes).

### Toll-Free
- Business: Authorized representative name, contact phone, company name, company incorporation certificate copy, proof of allocation from the Dutch national regulator.
- Address: Worldwide address permitted.
- Additional: Service usage description; email address.
- Notes: Business use only. ~72-hour validation.

## Portugal
- Requirement Groups mandatory for ordering.

### Local
- Business: Authorized representative name, contact phone, contact email, company name, NIF, business registration certificate, business registration number.
- Address: Address matching DID area code; proof of address (≤3 months).
- Notes: Business use only (no private use).

### National
- Personal: Name, last name, contact phone.
- Business: Authorized representative name, company name, contact phone.
- Address: Address in Portugal.

### Toll-Free
- Personal: Name, last name, contact phone.
- Business: Name, contact phone, company name.
- Address: Worldwide address permitted.

## Spain
### Local, Toll-Free, and Shared-Cost
- Personal: Name, last name, contact phone, local copy of ID or passport, Spanish National ID (DNI) or Foreigners’ Identity Number (NIE).
- Business: Authorized representative name, contact phone, company name, local company registration certificate, CIF (Certificado de Identificación Fiscal).
- Address: Address matching DID area code; proof of address (≤3 months).
- Notes: End-user must be physically present in Spain. ~72-hour validation.

### National
- Personal: Name, last name, contact phone, local passport/ID copy.
- Business: Authorized representative name, contact phone, company name, local company registration certificate.
- Address: Address in Spain; proof of address (≤3 months).
- Notes: Physical presence required. ~72-hour validation.

## Sweden
- Requirement Groups mandatory for ordering.

### Local
- Business: Authorized representative name, contact phone, contact email, company name, passport/ID (rep), business registration certificate, business registration number.
- Address: Address matching DID area code; proof of address (≤3 months).
- Notes: Physical presence required. Business use only (no private use).

### National
- Business: Same as Local (rep identity + company docs + registration number).
- Address: Address in Sweden; proof of address (≤3 months).
- Notes: Physical presence required. Business use only.

### Mobile
- Personal: Name, last name, contact phone, local passport/ID copy.
- Business: Authorized representative name, company name, contact phone, local company incorporation certificate.
- Address: Address in Sweden; proof of address (≤3 months).
- Notes: Physical presence required.

### Toll-Free
- Personal: Name, last name, contact phone, local passport/ID copy.
- Business: Name, contact phone, company name, local company incorporation certificate.
- Address: Address in Sweden; proof of address (≤3 months).
- Notes: Physical presence required.

## Switzerland
- Requirement Groups mandatory for ordering.

### Local
- Business: Authorized representative name, contact phone, contact email, company name, passport/ID (rep), business registration certificate, business registration number.
- Address: Address matching DID area code; proof of address (≤3 months).
- Notes: Business use only (no private use).

### Toll-Free
- Business: Authorized representative name, contact phone, company name, VAT number, company registration certificate (dated within 3 months).
- Address: Worldwide address permitted.
- Notes: Business use only. ~72-hour validation.

## United Kingdom (UK)
### Local
- Personal: Name, last name, contact phone, local passport/ID copy.
- Business: Authorized representative name, company name, contact phone, company website, local company registration certificate, business use case.
- Address: Address within the UK; utility bill (≤3 months).
- Notes: End-user must be physically present in the UK.

### National
- Personal: Name, last name, contact phone, local passport/ID copy.
- Business: As Local (incl. website, registration certificate, business use case).
- Address: Address within the UK; utility bill (≤3 months).
- Notes: Physical presence required.

### Toll-Free & Mobile
- Personal: Name, last name, contact phone, local passport/ID copy.
- Business: Authorized representative name, company name, contact phone, company website, local company registration certificate, business use case (indicate if sub-allocating).
- Address: Address within the UK; utility bill (≤3 months).
- Notes: Physical presence required. ~72-hour validation after docs received.
