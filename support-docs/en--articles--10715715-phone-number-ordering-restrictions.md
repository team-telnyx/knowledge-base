# Phone Number Ordering Restrictions

Get your account Verified to remove all restrictions on phone number ordering.

Written by Telnyx Engineering

September 19, 2025

Table of contents

---

Telnyx has introduced phone number ordering restrictions to combat fraudulent activity and enhance the security of our platform. In this guide, we’ll walk you through what these restrictions mean and how they may affect your ability to order numbers.

---

### Account Verification Level

Ordering restrictions are enforced based on your account verification level. There are two verification frameworks, each with their own set of ordering restrictions:

* The Level 1 / Level 2 framework (Legacy)
* The Trial-Paid-Verified-Enterprise (TPVE) framework

Before proceeding, please refer [to this guide](https://support.telnyx.com/en/articles/1130595-account-verification) to figure out your own account's verification level.

---

## +1 Toll Free ordering restrictions

Effective September 22, 2025, accounts created with a freemail domain (e.g., gmail.com, yahoo.com, outlook.com, etc) will face restrictions when ordering +1 toll-free phone numbers.

1. **Freemail accounts created on or after September 22, 2025**

   * **Cannot order +1 toll-free phone numbers. Regardless of your account verification level or framework.**
2. Freemail accounts created before September 22, 2025

   * You are still eligible to order +1 toll-free numbers if you meet the other account qualifications (read the rest of this guide)
3. Non-freemail accounts (corporate or custom domains)

   * You are still eligible to order +1 toll-free numbers if you meet the other account qualifications (read the rest of this guide)

---

### **`Level 1 / Level 2`** `(Legacy)` Ordering Restrictions

**Accounts created after March 24, 2025** **with a Level 1 (L1) status** will only be able to order **local** phone numbers in their account's **country of origin**.

**Pre-existing L1 accounts (created before March 24, 2025) have only one ordering restriction: they will not be able to order toll-free phone numbers**. Telnyx reserves the right to update these restrictions in the future.

**All Level 2 (L2) accounts have no ordering restrictions.**

In other words:

* Accounts created *before* March 24, 2025

  + L1: Cannot order toll-free phone numbers. No further restricstions
  + L2: No restrictions
* Accounts created *after* March 24, 2025:

  + L1: Can only order local numbers from their country of origin
  + L2: No restrictions

For more details on your account status and how to get L2 verified, please refer to [this support article](https://support.telnyx.com/en/articles/1130595-account-verification).

**Sub users:**

**Sub user ordering restrictions will be based on their organization owner's account signup date and verification status**. It will not be determined based on the sub users account.

**Managed Accounts:**

Ordering restrictions will be based on the account signup date and verification status of each individual ManagER or ManagED account.

---

## `Trial-Paid-Verified-Enterprise (TPVE)` ordering restrictions

**I**f your account is a part of the `Trial-Paid-Verified-Enterprise (TPVE)` framework, Please go to <https://developers.telnyx.com/docs/account-setup/levels-and-capabilities> to learn more about your account level, applicable restrictions, and how to upgrade it.

---

**We appreciate your understanding as we implement these changes to maintain the security and integrity of our platform.**
