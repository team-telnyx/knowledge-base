# Congo DID Requirements

Requirements to acquire Democratic Republic of the Congo numbers. See Telnyx guidance and requirements.




## DID Number Requirements for Democratic Republic of the Congo

In order to purchase a Democratic Republic of the Congo number you will need to provide the following:

## **Mobile Numbers in** Democratic Republic of the Congo

For **personal identity** verification:

\* Name, last name

\* Contact phone number

\* Contact e-mail

For **business identity** verification:

\* Name, last name of an authorized representative

\* Company name

\* Contact phone number

\* Contact e-mail

For address verification:

\* Worldwide Address (street, building number, postal code, city, and country)

\* Proof of address (dated within 3 months)

Please note that once the documentation is received it will take approximately 72 hours to validate the information and activate the number for use.
