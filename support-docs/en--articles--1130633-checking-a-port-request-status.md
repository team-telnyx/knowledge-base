# How do I check the status of my port request?

|  |
| --- |
| **What is porting?**  If you're switching service providers you may be able to keep your existing phone number. This process, often referred to as *phone number porting*, can be done between wireline, IP and wireless providers.  * Please use our [portability tool](https://portal.telnyx.com/#/app/numbers/port-numbers/new) to see if a number is portable. * If there is a telephone number you need to acquire that is not currently in our inventory, please request it [following these steps](https://support.telnyx.com/en/articles/3562148-requesting-numbers). |

## Check Porting Status in Mission Control

You can **view the status of your port request** in real-time directly from the [Mission Control portal](https://portal.telnyx.com/#/login/sign-in).

To dig into more detail on how to port, including how to check the status, take a look at:

* Our [Step-by-Step Guide to Porting](https://support.telnyx.com/en/articles/1130634-port-numbers-to-telnyx)
* An explanation of our [porting policies](https://support.telnyx.com/en/articles/1130630-porting-policy-procedure)
* [Port Request Statuses](https://support.telnyx.com/en/articles/3284588-port-request-statuses) defined.

There are also instructions on how to fill out your Letter of Authorization and a template to download, [here](https://support.telnyx.com/en/articles/2034326-how-to-fill-out-an-loa).

If you need more information, you can reach out directly to our friendly porting team at [porting@telnyx.com](mailto:porting@telnyx.com) or by calling us at [+1 312 270 8001](tel:+13122708001). Our Porting Experts are available to you anytime from 9AM - 5PM Central time!
