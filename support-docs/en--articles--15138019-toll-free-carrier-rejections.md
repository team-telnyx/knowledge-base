# Toll-Free Carrier Rejections

This article will emphasise the meaning of carrier rejection responses on Toll-Free submissions and how to handle them. See Telnyx guidance and requirements.



This article will emphasise the meaning of carrier rejection responses on Toll-Free submissions and how to handle them.

**Carrier Rejections**

**Agreeing to receive messages must be optional**

-This means that the SMS opt-in is forced by submitting a form by entering a phone number.

-Solution: The phone number field must be optional, or a checkbox must be added to the SMS disclosures. Once updated, resubmit.

**Additional information requested - justification for more than 5 numbers per business**

-This means a justification is needed when submitting 6 or more numbers in one submission or submitting 6 separate submissions for the same business.

-Solution: Provide valid explanation in the additional use case details field, on why multiple numbers are needed. Then resubmit.

**Business email address must use an official domain**

-This means the business email domain entered in the toll-free submission does not match the business website domain, or match the contact email on the website.

-Solution: Update the email address in the submission and resubmit.

**Business information could not be verified - contact, email, address, or url is invalid**

-This means the business details entered in the toll-free submission do not match what is present on the website.

-Solution: Make sure any business details on the website match the submission details and if they are absent, then have them added to the website. Once completed resubmit.

**Business registration number is missing or invalid**

-This means the wrong business name was entered into the business name field, and it did not match their EIN form or the company is less than 6 months old and the IRS only does batch updates quarterly.

-Solution: EMail [tfverification@telnyx.com](mailto:tfverification@telnyx.com) and provide the business registration document. Do not resubmit.

**Consent for messaging cannot be part of other agreements**

-This means the SMS opt-in has been grouped in with a product or service, or terms and conditions.

-Solution: Provide a separate opt-in for SMS consent only, or remove terms and conditions from the opt-in language. The SMS opt-in must be optional. Once edited, please resubmit.

**Contact name must belong to a business representative**

-This means the first and last name entered into the submission details, is unintelligible or does not make sense. (Example: First Name: FRANK, Last Name: FRANK)

-Solution: Update contact name and resubmit.

**Disallowed content - cannabis, cbd, or other illegal substances**

-This means the business website promotes, markets, or sells cannabis.

-Solution: Remove any and all cannabis related content from the website. But, if the use-case for the Toll-Free submission is 2FA, Employee Alerts, or HR/Staffing, then no action may need to be taken, which will be handled on a case by case basis. Then resubmit.

**Disallowed content - third-party lead generation or multi-level marketing**

-This means any reference to lead generation using third party affiliates to produce leads, is prohibited.

-Solution: Remove all lead generation language from the business website. Lead alerts, and lead management is allowed. Then resubmit.

**End business details must be accurate and complete**

-This means the brand name or DBA within the opt-in does not match the business details in the toll-free submission.

-Solution: Make sure the brand name or DBA name has been correctly entered into the toll-free submission or update the brand name in the opt-in disclosure language. Then resubmit.

**Entity misclassification (legal entity type mismatch)**

-This means the business entity selected does not match the official record.

-Solution: This rejection usually triggers when the sole proprietor is selected, but the business is actually a private entity with a BRN. Update business details and resubmit.

**High risk - known spam campaign**

-This means the business in the toll-free submission details, has been flagged by the carrier prior to joining Telnyx.

-Solution: This is usually a hard block. We can request a reimmediation, but there is less than one percent conversion rate in these cases. EMail [tfverification@telnyx.com](mailto:tfverification@telnyx.com) to request an appeal at the carrier level.

**High risk - submission flagged for high-risk domain issues**

-This means the domain details are similar to a prior flagged business or the domain age is very new.

-Solution: EMail [tfverification@telnyx.com](mailto:tfverification@telnyx.com) and we will appeal. Do not resubmit.

**Invalid or inaccessible website url**

-This means the website was inaccessible with the provided website url.

-Solution: Make sure the website is live, and the domain is public. If this is an error, then EMail [tfverification@telnyx.com](mailto:tfverification@telnyx.com) and we will investigate.

**Marketing messages require express written consent**

-This means marketing and promotional SMS can not be mentioned with any other use case in the same opt-in disclosure language.

-Solution: Add a separate opt-in for marketing and promotional SMS. (Note: Opt-in’s that state “receive promotional or marketing and informational SMS” will be rejected. The word informational must be defined. This is usually a transactional use case and will be in a separate opt-in. Resubmit after the correction has been made.

**Opt-in - consent for messaging is a requirement for service**

-This means the SMS is not optional and mandatory to use or purchase service from the business.

-Solution: Make the SMS opt-in optional by adding a checkbox. Once updated, re-submit

**Opt-in does not match the use case**

-This means the opt-in disclosure mentions a use case that does not align with the use case selected in the submission.

-Solution: Update opt-in to match use case or edit the use case in the submission to match the opt-in. Then resubmit.

**Opt-in example must be complete, branded, and legible.**

-This means the opt-in image provided was not branded, missing details, or blurry image was provided.

-Solution: Provide a new screenshot for the opt-in link and resubmit.

**Opt-ins must clearly reflect the end business**

-This means the opt-in is branded with something other than what was submitted for verification, or the opt-in has (business name) in the opt-in and not the actual name.

-Solution: Update opt-in and re-seubmit.

**Opt-in url not accessible**

-This means the opt-in link provided was inaccessible or 404 not found.

-Solution: Update link and verify that it works. Then resubmit. If this is an error, then EMail [tfverification@telnyx.com](mailto:tfverification@telnyx.com) and we will investigate..

**Shaft violation - alcohol message content without a 21+ robust age gate**

-This means the website or checkout page of the website does not have an age verification, to block minors from viewing any alcohol content.

-Solution: Once an age gate is added to the website, resubmit.

**Social platform must be established and active**

-This means the social media link to facebook, instagram, or linkedin, is missing business is missing business contact details.

-Solution: Update social media page with contact details and re-submit.

**Submission editing timed out**

-This means the submission was not resubmitted within 7 days of rejection and the carrier’s ticket ID was closed.

-Solution: Email [tfverification@telnyx.com](mailto:tfverification@telnyx.com) and request details on the rejection. Do not re-submit until you receive feedback on the initial rejection reason.

**Website is password protected or requires login**

-This means the website is behind a login wall.

-Solution: The website must be made public. Then resubmit.

**Website must be established and active**

-This means the website is missing one or more of the following requirements: Home Page, Contact Information (address/email/etc), Products/Services offered by the business, About page, Contact us page, Privacy and Terms page

-Solution: Update website and resubmit.

Please email [tfverification@telnyx.com](mailto:tfverification@telnyx.com) for any questions.
