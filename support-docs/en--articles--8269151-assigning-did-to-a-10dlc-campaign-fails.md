## Assigning DID to a 10DLC Campaign Fails

If you are assigning a DID to a 10DLC Campaign and it fails then please reach out to us at [10dlcquestions@telnyx.com](mailto:10dlcquestions@telnyx.com). Please share your Telnyx account email, Campaign Ids, and any errors returned so that we can help more quickly.
