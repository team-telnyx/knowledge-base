# Common Questions about IoT SIM and eSIM

## **Do the SIM cards support voice?**

At the moment, Telnyx SIMs are data only.

## **Are the SIM cards two-way SMS capable?**

Yes through AT commands.

## **Do the SIMs work internationally?**

Yes. For more information, please see our International IoT SIM Coverage page [here](https://telnyx.com/iot-global-coverage#table-iot-global-coverage).

## **How long does shipping usually take?**

For orders under 50 [SIM cards](https://telnyx.com/products/iot-sim-card), we would send out via USPS which would normally take a few days to reach a destination within the 48 states, slightly longer for Alaska and Hawaii.

For large orders, typically over 50 SIM cards, please contact our sales team to discuss your use case and verify if we can expedite shipping.

International orders outside the United States may take longer than 7 business working days.

## **How do I get a SIM card?**

Purchase a SIM card or beta test one for free by ordering at [portal.telnyx.com](https://portal.telnyx.com)

## **What are the associated costs?**

Subsequent SIM cards are available for purchase from $1 per SIM (triple-cut plastic SIMs and embedded MFF2 SIM chips) and $0.70 per over-the-air eSIM.

There is a monthly recurring charge of $2 per activated SIM and $0.20 per inactive SIM.  
​

Cost per MB of data starts at $0.0125.

## **Does Telnyx offer pay-as-you-go on data pricing?**

Yes.

## **What is the $ amount** rounding policy for data usage**?**

We charge to the nearest MB.

## **Suspension and Reactivation Fees: Do you charge for suspending and reactivating devices, particularly for those used seasonally?**

There is a Monthly Recurring Charge (MRC) for active and standby SIMs, however, we do not charge MRC for disabled SIMs. There are no additional fees for changing the SIM state.

## **Auto-Provisioning: Are the SIM cards auto-provisioned upon activation?**

Once a SIM is registered to a customer account in either the standby or active state it will be provisioned and ready for use.

## **SIM Data Management: Is it possible to manage SIM data on-the-fly, allowing for real-time adjustments and monitoring?**

The portal and API allow for data limits to be set at SIM and SIM Group levels to control usage. Usage of SIMs is reported in near real-time.

## **Is there analytics tracking to look at real-time data usage?**

Yes, you can read more on reporting [here](https://support.telnyx.com/en/articles/3679913-sim-reporting-analytics).

## Can I order unbranded Telnyx SIM for reselling purposes ?

Yes, we can issue unbranded Telnyx SIMs however the minimum order quantity for this type of SIMs would be 5000.

## Can I transfer my existing SIM from Telnyx Account A to Telnyx Account B ?

Yes, you can delete the SIM card from Telnyx Account A and then register the same SIM using registration code on Telnyx Account B. If you need help with Registration code, please reach out to [support@telnyx.com](mailto:support@telnyx.com).
