# Mexico Number Porting

Here you will find a detailed list of requirements for Mexico number portability.

Written by Kim Dorson-King

February 10, 2026

Table of contents

# Requirements

# Local / National Numbers in Mexico

1. LOA (NIP (for individual person)) signed by the Authorized Person with a signature matching as closely as possible the one on the ID.
2. Copy of the National ID / Passport of the Authorized Person.
3. Latest Invoice.
4. If the number(s) belong to a Company, the Company Registration Certificate – “Acta Constitutiva”.
5. If the number(s) belong to a Company and the Authorized Person is not mentioned on the Company Registration Certificate, an Attorney's Authorization for Representative – “Poder notarial”.

## Toll-free Numbers in Mexico

1. LOA signed by the Authorized Person with a signature matching as closely as possible the one on the ID.
2. Copy of the ID / Passport of the Authorized Person.
3. Latest Invoice.
4. If the number(s) belong to a Company, the Company Registration Certificate – “Acta Constitutiva”.
5. If the number(s) belong to a Company and the Authorized Person is not mentioned on the Company Registration Certificate, an Attorney's Authorization for Representative – “Poder notarial”.

## Mobile Numbers in Mexico

1. LOA signed by the Authorized Person with a signature matching as closely as possible the one on the ID.
2. Copy of the National ID / Passport of the Authorized Person.
3. Assignment Letter completed and signed by the Authorized Person.
4. If the number(s) belong to a Company, copy of the Notarial Deed (POA) in which the company grants General Administrative Powers to the representative who signs the LOA.

# Porting Hours and Earliest FOC date

## For local/national/toll-free

**Porting hours (local time)**

Monday to Friday at 2:00 AM.

*The porting of the number will be automatically triggered from 2 AM local time and can take up to 2 hours to fully complete.*

**Earliest FOC date**

2 business days from submission.

## For Mobile

**Porting hours (local time)**

Monday to Friday at 2:00 AM to 7:30 AM.

*It is important to note that when carrying out the MOBILE portability to our VOIP service, there will be a loss of data service (the customer should consider the entire window as a period of service interruption). The customer won't be able to use internet data or maintain the connections they had with the previous provider once the port completes.*

**Earliest FOC date**

7 business days from submission.
