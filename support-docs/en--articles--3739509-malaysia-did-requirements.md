# Malaysia DID Requirements

Here we will provide a detailed list of requirements to acquire Malaysian numbers. See Telnyx guidance and requirements.




## DID Number Requirements for Malaysia

In order to purchase a Malaysia number you will need to provide the following:

## **Local Numbers in Malaysia**

For **business identity** verification:

\* Name, last name of an authorizes representative

\* Contact phone number

\* Company name
\* Passport or ID copy of an authorized representative

​

For address verification:

\* Address in Malaysia (street, building number, postal code, city and country)

\* Proof of address (dated within 3 months)

​Additional information:

\* Business use required: Business use is required, private use is not allowed.

## **National Numbers in Malaysia**

For **business identity** verification:

\* Name, last name of an authorized representative

\* Contact phone number

\* Company name

\* Passport or ID copy of an authorized representative

\* Malaysian company registration certificate

\* Signed LOI (dated within 1 month)

​

For address verification:

\* Address in Malaysia (street, building number, postal code, city and country)

\* Proof of address (dated within 3 months)

​Additional information:

\* Business use required: Business use is required, private use is not allowed.

## **Toll-Free Numbers in Malaysia**

For **personal identity** verification:

\* Name, last name

\* Contact phone number

​

For **business identity** verification:

\* Name, last name of an authorized representative

\* Contact phone number

\* Company name

​

For address verification:

\* Address in Malaysia (street, building number, postal code, city and country)

\* Proof of address (dated within 3 months)

## **Mobile Numbers in Malaysia**

For **business identity** verification:

\* Name, last name of an authorized representative

\* Contact phone number

\* Passport or ID copy of an authorized representative

\* Company name

\* Company registration certificate

​\* Signed LOI (dated within 1 month)

For address verification:

\* Worldwide Address (street, building number, postal code, city and country)

\* Proof of address (dated within 3 months)

​

​Additional information:

\* Business use required: Business use is required, private use is not allowed.

\*\*\*Please note that once the documentation is received it will take approximately 72 hours to validate the information and activate the number for use.
