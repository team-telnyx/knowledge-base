# Guide to assigning messaging profile in Bulk

A step-by-step guide to bulk edit Messaging Profile settings of selected numbers.  
​

## **Step 1**

Log in to your Telnyx Mission Control account and click on the NUMBERS on the top left side of the page.

## **Step 2**

Click on the [MY NUMBERS](https://portal.telnyx.com/#/app/numbers/my-numbers) tab

## **Step 3**

Select the numbers by selecting checkboxes in front of the numbers you'd like to edit.

## **Step 4**

You can also select all the numbers displayed on the page by using the checkbox above the top number (highlighted in red).

## **Step 5**

Once you have selected your desired numbers, click on the Bulk Actions dropdown and select Edit Messaging Profile.  
​

​

## **Step 6**

Once you click on "Edit Messaging Profile," a new window will open, allowing you to choose the desired messaging profile from the dropdown menu.

[![Edit Messaging Profile section. ](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/877067628/4ad397052f3bcfada6c39e15/Screenshot+2023-11-07+at+9.27.29+pm.png?expires=1781167500&signature=32987db12e01907eec9c3f87fb2c1f6059815acca83914286dae5b2444681a3c&req=fCcgFs95m4NXFb4f3HP0gM%2F6VblWJSXboF41aChuBbDg8g63LS0xIW4uSqnl%0AOnwrt5LMzqN169iUxA%3D%3D%0A)](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/877067628/4ad397052f3bcfada6c39e15/Screenshot+2023-11-07+at+9.27.29+pm.png?expires=1781167500&signature=32987db12e01907eec9c3f87fb2c1f6059815acca83914286dae5b2444681a3c&req=fCcgFs95m4NXFb4f3HP0gM%2F6VblWJSXboF41aChuBbDg8g63LS0xIW4uSqnl%0AOnwrt5LMzqN169iUxA%3D%3D%0A)

Upon selecting a profile, you will see a message regarding the MRC charge for assigning a messaging profile to a number. To proceed, you must accept these charges and then save your selection. The chosen messaging profile will then be applied to the bulk selected numbers.

[![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/ltcafuzd/1312416567/7e8cf5a6bc68084993124f31ef4f/image.png?expires=1781167500&signature=5ce9f588b2c311fbe97b218549ef23e88497abee4440f1adff0695906d09211e&req=dSMmFM1%2Fm4RZXvMW1HO4zZpnedMoY9SU55aQ11r%2B079103Wj2P%2BgaMtk0KA%2F%0Af2VCZavr5RHsY7R%2BtEM%3D%0A)](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/ltcafuzd/1312416567/7e8cf5a6bc68084993124f31ef4f/image.png?expires=1781167500&signature=5ce9f588b2c311fbe97b218549ef23e88497abee4440f1adff0695906d09211e&req=dSMmFM1%2Fm4RZXvMW1HO4zZpnedMoY9SU55aQ11r%2B079103Wj2P%2BgaMtk0KA%2F%0Af2VCZavr5RHsY7R%2BtEM%3D%0A)

Once you click "Save," you will notice that all the bulk-selected numbers have been updated with the chosen messaging profile.
