Understanding the necessary criteria for obtaining Algerian numbers: Get key details. See Telnyx guidance and requirements.




## DID Number Requirements for Algeria

## **National numbers in Algeria**

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Enduser name | The company name for business usage or the Surname for private usage is needed when ordering. |
| Business use: Copy ID of an authorized representative | Copy ID of an authorized representative |
| Business use: copy company registration | A copy of the company registration must be provided |
| Name, tel.-number & email representative | Name, telephone number & email-address legal representative |
