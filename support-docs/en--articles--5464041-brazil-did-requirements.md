# DID Number Requirements for Brazil

In order to purchase a Brazilian number you will need to provide the following:

## **Local/Mobile Numbers in Brazil**

For personal identity verification:

\* Name, last name

\* Contact phone number

\* CPF number

\* Passport or ID copy

For business identity verification:

\* Name, last name of an authorized representative

\* Company name

\* Contact phone number

\* Cartão CNPJ (Comprovante de Inscrição e Situação Cadastral)

For address verification:

\* Address in Brazil (street, building number, postal code, city and country)

\* Proof of address (dated within 3 months)

## **Toll-Free/National (3003/4004) Numbers in Brazil**

For **personal identity** verification:

\* Name, last name

\* Contact phone number

\* Contact e-mail

\* CPF number  
\* Passport or ID copy  
​

For **business identity** verification:

\* Name, last name of an authorized representative

\* Company name

\* Contact phone number

\* Contact e-mail

\* CNPJ number

\* Power of Attorney

\* Passport or ID copy of an authorized representative

\* Company Registration Certificate  
​

For **address** verification:

\* Address in Brazil (street, building number, postal code, city and country)  
​  
\*\*Please note: It could take up to 3 business days to review/approve Brazil orders.
