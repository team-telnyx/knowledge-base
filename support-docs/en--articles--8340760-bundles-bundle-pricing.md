# **Introduction to Bundles**

Customers have the option to purchase numbers using bundle pricing, transfer existing numbers to bundle pricing, or update an existing phone number to bundle pricing.

Bundle pricing is an excellent option for users of Operator Connect and Zoom Phone, offering a way to control telephony expenses on a per-user basis. Each bundle comes with a US local number and E911, ensuring connectivity and compliance.

Customers can select from two types of bundles based on their business needs: a cost-effective pay-as-you-go bundle or a standard bundle that includes a set number of minutes.

* The basic bundle provides a 1 US number and E911 Emergency Calling, and all inbound and outbound calling billed pay-as-you-go.

* The standard bundle includes 1 US Number, E911 Emergency Calling, Up to 1000 minutes of US inbound and outbound calling, and International inbound and outbound calling billed pay-as-you-go.

Calls made within our network (on-net calls) will consume the bundle's included minutes. However, programmed voice calls are charged separately and do not affect the bundle's minutes. At present, premium numbers are not eligible for inclusion in any bundle ([more details here](https://support.telnyx.com/en/articles/4640720-what-is-an-exhausted-npa)).

For pay-as-you-go calls, charges are applied according to the Bundles rate-deck, which can be downloaded from the Pricing page.

## **How do I get started with bundles?**

You can purchase bundles by logging into your [Mission Control Portal](https://portal.telnyx.com/) account and navigating to the Bundles section on the left-side navigation.

[Bundles Page](https://portal.telnyx.com/#/app/bundles/order)

To add a bundle to your account, follow these steps:

1. Select either a basic or standard bundle by clicking the green "Add" button to add them to your.

2. Use the Search and Buy numbers tab to choose your number, then go to your Cart in the top right corner to link the number with your bundle.

Please note that bundle offers are currently limited to **Operator Connect** and **Zoom Phone** users, including only US numbers and calls. We are in the process of expanding our offerings - for more information, please contact [sales@telnyx.com](mailto:sales@telnyx.com).

After selecting your number, proceed to the External Voice Integrations section under the Voice tab in the left-side menu to associate your new bundle number with the desired External Voice Integration.

Following these steps, you will be all set to make calls using your bundle.
